# Evidence and coverage

## Current inventory

Commerce Brain contains 5,899 content documents: 1,312 web articles, 632 YouTube transcripts, 58 course documents, and 3,897 podcast documents. The repo also contains a curated register of 126 learning sources across 13 in-scope categories.

## Coverage levels

**Deep corpus:** DTC, B2B/wholesale, retail/omnichannel, and B2B2C/marketplace operator. These categories supplied the current pull configuration.

**Catalog-only or partial:** handmade, digital products, subscription box/kitting, marketplace seller, social/live commerce, creator/affiliate commerce, recommerce, rental, and agentic commerce.

Catalog-only does not mean false. It means the source was discovered and checked, but Ecom Brain has not yet extracted enough content to claim a mature doctrine.

## Reliability rules

- Prefer first-party specifications for platform behavior and protocols.
- Treat vendor blogs as product-informed but commercially interested.
- Prefer filings, datasets, and operator evidence for economics.
- Preserve dates for fast-moving subjects, especially agentic commerce.
- Label projections, scale figures, and benchmarks with their source.
- If evidence conflicts, show the disagreement instead of averaging it away.

## Known limitations

- Four web sources were blocked during collection and were not bypassed.
- One Retail Remix episode timed out in Whisper and retains only thin show notes.
- The corpus is internal third-party research and must not be redistributed.
