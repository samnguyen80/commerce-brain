# Commerce model card method

Use the same fields when analyzing or comparing models:

1. Definition and axis.
2. Merchant archetype and buyer.
3. Customer ownership and channel dependence.
4. Revenue and repeat mechanism.
5. Cost structure, working capital, and cash timing.
6. Operational bottleneck.
7. Growth loop.
8. Defensibility.
9. Failure modes.
10. Avada-relevant software opportunities.
11. Evidence coverage, verification date, and primary learning sources.

Do not present qualitative labels such as “high margin” without stating what material costs, service cost, acquisition, returns, and tax are excluded.
