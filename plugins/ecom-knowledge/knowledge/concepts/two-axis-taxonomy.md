# Two-axis commerce taxonomy

Commerce labels answer different questions and should not be flattened into one list.

## Axis 1 — offer, supply, and fulfillment

The complete research taxonomy includes POD, dropshipping, private label, white label, wholesale-buying, arbitrage, handmade, made-to-order, in-house manufacturing, digital products, and subscription box/kitting.

Avada's selected scope is [[handmade-craft]], [[digital-products]], and [[subscription-box-kitting]].

## Axis 2 — channel and customer relationship

Avada selected all ten: [[dtc]], [[b2b-wholesale]], [[marketplace-seller]], [[retail-omnichannel]], [[social-live-commerce]], [[creator-affiliate-commerce]], [[recommerce-resale]], [[rental-commerce]], [[b2b2c-marketplace-operator]], and [[agentic-commerce]].

## Classification rule

Return at least one coordinate from each applicable axis. If facts are missing, state the missing fact instead of forcing a label. A merchant can occupy multiple channel coordinates at once.
