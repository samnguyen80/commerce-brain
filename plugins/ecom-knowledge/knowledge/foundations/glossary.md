# Commerce glossary

**Fulfillment axis** — how an offer is created, stocked, assembled, delivered, or licensed.

**Channel axis** — where the transaction happens and who owns the customer relationship.

**Merchant** — the party selling the offer. A marketplace operator is infrastructure for other merchants and can also be a merchant itself.

**Customer ownership** — practical control of identity, transaction history, communication permission, and repeat-purchase relationship. It is not binary.

**Take rate** — marketplace or platform revenue divided by transaction value flowing through it.

**AOV** — average order value.

**CAC** — customer acquisition cost.

**LTV** — contribution value expected from a customer over the relationship, not gross revenue alone.

**MOQ** — minimum order quantity in wholesale or manufacturing.

**Kitting** — assembling multiple SKUs or components into one sellable package.

**Voluntary churn** — cancellation chosen by the subscriber.

**Involuntary churn** — subscription loss caused by payment failure or operational failure.

**Reverse logistics** — return, inspection, cleaning, repair, refurbishment, restocking, or disposal after an item comes back.

**Network effect** — the product becomes more valuable as participation grows. Marketplace liquidity is not automatically defensibility.

**Merchant of record** — the entity legally responsible for the transaction, tax, refunds, and payment relationship.

**Agentic commerce** — discovery or purchasing performed partly by an AI agent using feeds, tools, or commerce protocols.

**Deep corpus** — the category has locally extracted articles, videos, courses, or podcast material.

**Catalog-only** — sources were found and checked, but their content has not been pulled and synthesized deeply.
