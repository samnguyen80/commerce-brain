# Ecom Brain knowledge

Ecom Brain maps commerce businesses on two independent axes: how the offer is produced or fulfilled, and how the merchant reaches and transacts with a customer. It is a decision layer distilled from Commerce Brain research; the 5,899-document corpus remains evidence and is not copied into this plugin.

## Boundaries

- Research is internal and includes third-party material; do not redistribute the corpus.
- Vendor-produced sources are useful but not neutral.
- Figures and market claims must retain their source and verification date.
- Missing evidence must be stated, not filled with generic ecommerce advice.
