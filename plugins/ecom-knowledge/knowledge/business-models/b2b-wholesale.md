# B2B and wholesale selling

## Model

The merchant sells to retailers, distributors, businesses, or organizational buyers rather than directly to the final consumer.

## Economics and operations

- Orders are larger and less frequent, with negotiated price, MOQ, catalogs, terms, quotes, and approvals.
- Revenue quality depends on account retention, replenishment, fill rate, margin by account, and payment collection.
- Working capital is shaped by inventory commitment and net payment terms.

## Growth and defensibility

Reliable supply, assortment, account relationships, sales workflow, operational integration, and product availability matter more than consumer-style checkout polish alone.

## Failure modes

One catalog for every account, manual price errors, poor inventory visibility, unprofitable terms, weak onboarding, and measuring only first-order GMV.

## Avada opportunities

Company/location accounts, catalogs, MOQ and price lists, quote-to-order, terms/AR workflows, rep tooling, retailer portals, replenishment, and trade-show lead conversion.
