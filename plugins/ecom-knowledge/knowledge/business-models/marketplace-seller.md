# Marketplace seller

## Model

The merchant sells inside a platform such as Amazon, Etsy, eBay, Walmart, or TikTok Shop, using the platform's demand, rules, ranking, and transaction infrastructure.

## Economics and operations

- Revenue is order based; contribution must include commission, fulfillment, ads, storage, returns, and penalties.
- The platform generally controls discovery and limits direct customer access.
- Catalog quality, service metrics, availability, and policy compliance are operating inputs.

## Growth and defensibility

Reviews, ranking history, listing quality, operational reliability, brand recognition, and differentiated supply help. Platform position alone is fragile defensibility.

## Failure modes

Single-platform dependence, account suspension, fee blindness, ranking volatility, counterfeit/hijack exposure, and confusing marketplace GMV with owned demand.

## Avada opportunities

Listing and catalog intelligence, policy monitoring, cross-marketplace inventory, profitability by SKU/channel, review workflows, and marketplace-to-owned-channel strategy within platform rules.
