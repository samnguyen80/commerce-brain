# Agentic commerce

## Model

An AI agent participates in product discovery, comparison, cart construction, checkout, or post-purchase work on behalf of a user or organization. It is an emerging channel layer, not a new fulfillment model.

## Economics and operations

- The merchant still needs authoritative catalog, price, availability, policy, identity, checkout, tax, and fulfillment systems.
- Protocols may expose discovery and transaction capabilities while payment authorization remains a separate layer.
- Merchant-of-record responsibility does not disappear because an agent initiated the order.

## Growth and defensibility

Machine-readable product truth, trusted authorization, differentiated supply, service reliability, and direct post-purchase relationships matter. Protocol compliance alone is not defensibility.

## Failure modes

Treating demos as volume, confusing UCP/ACP checkout with payment authorization, stale feeds, bot fraud, unclear consent, missing idempotency, and losing customer context to the agent layer.

## Avada opportunities

Catalog readiness, structured policies, agent traffic verification, signed intent, checkout adapters, delegated-payment observability, post-purchase handoff, and agent-channel attribution.

## Evidence

Catalog-only and fast-moving. Prefer current first-party specifications: Shopify/UCP, OpenAI/ACP, Stripe, and Google AP2 in [[source-register]]. Re-check anything older than 30 days.
