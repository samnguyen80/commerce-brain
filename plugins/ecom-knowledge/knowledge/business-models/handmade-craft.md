# Handmade and craft

## Model

The merchant makes or materially transforms the product. Differentiation comes from craft, design, customization, story, and trust rather than access to a shared supplier.

## Economics and operations

- Revenue is usually per item, custom order, collection, or wholesale account.
- Material COGS is visible; labor, rework, packaging, and owner time are often undercounted.
- Capacity, batch planning, lead time, and quality consistency constrain growth before demand does.
- Marketplace discovery is common, while a DTC channel improves customer ownership.

## Growth and defensibility

Distinctive design, proof of craftsmanship, repeatable production, reviews, and an owned audience matter more than catalog breadth. Wholesale can add volume but creates MOQ, line-sheet, margin, and production-planning pressure.

## Failure modes

Pricing as if labor were free; copying marketplace trends; custom work without scope control; inventory split across Etsy and Shopify; scaling demand beyond production capacity.

## Avada opportunities

Simple multi-channel inventory, material/BOM tracking, production queues, true-cost pricing, custom-order workflows, and Etsy-to-Shopify customer migration.
