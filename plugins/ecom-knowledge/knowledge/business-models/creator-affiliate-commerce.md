# Creator and affiliate commerce

## Model

Demand is created or converted by a creator, publisher, advocate, or affiliate who controls audience trust while the brand supplies the offer and commercial infrastructure.

## Economics and operations

- Compensation may be commission, fixed fee, product, licensing, tiered bonus, or a hybrid.
- Economics require incrementality, margin after commission, returns, fraud, content rights, and payment timing.
- Relationship operations include discovery, outreach, contracting, briefing, asset approval, attribution, and reactivation.

## Growth and defensibility

Durable creator relationships, fast product/content feedback, trustworthy attribution, reusable rights, and an owned partner community compound better than one-off placements.

## Failure modes

Last-click overpayment, coupon leakage, fake engagement, return fraud, creator churn, unclear usage rights, and treating creators as an infinite outbound list.

## Avada opportunities

Partner CRM, contracts and rights, fraud-aware attribution, cohort quality, product seeding, payout reconciliation, creative library, and long-term relationship workflows.
