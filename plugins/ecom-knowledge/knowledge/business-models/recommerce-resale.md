# Recommerce and resale

## Model

Previously owned goods are resold through consignment, managed inventory, peer-to-peer marketplaces, trade-in, refurbishment, or brand-owned resale programs.

## Economics and operations

- Revenue may be gross margin, commission, take rate, service fees, or trade-in-assisted primary sales.
- Unit economics depend on sourcing, authentication, intake, grading, photography, storage, returns, and markdown risk.
- Every item may be unique, increasing catalog and operational cost.

## Growth and defensibility

Trusted supply, authentication, liquidity, category expertise, pricing data, community, and efficient reverse logistics can compound.

## Failure modes

Ignoring labor per item, counterfeit exposure, poor condition standards, thin liquidity, slow sell-through, high returns, and treating sustainability narrative as unit economics.

## Avada opportunities

Unique-item listing, condition grading, pricing support, authentication workflow, trade-in credit, seller operations, reverse logistics, and circular-program analytics.
