# Digital products

## Model

The offer is a file, license, course, membership, template, software entitlement, or other non-physical access. Delivery is digital, but entitlement and support are real operations.

## Economics and operations

- Near-zero unit reproduction cost does not imply near-zero total cost.
- Revenue may be one-time, subscription, membership, bundle, seat, or usage based.
- Checkout, tax, delivery, access control, updates, support, and refunds replace physical fulfillment.
- Merchant-of-record platforms can absorb tax and payment complexity in exchange for fees and control.

## Growth and defensibility

Positioning, proprietary expertise, continuous updates, community, workflow integration, and audience trust defend the offer. Marketplace validation can precede an owned storefront.

## Failure modes

Generic information, weak transformation promise, uncontrolled sharing, confusing licenses, no update policy, and selling internationally without tax or refund clarity.

## Avada opportunities

License and entitlement management, secure delivery, cross-border tax workflows, team-seat sales, product-led upsells, and lifecycle automation tied to consumption.
