# Rental commerce

## Model

The customer pays for temporary access rather than ownership. The operator may own inventory or coordinate peer-to-peer supply.

## Economics and operations

- Revenue may be per rental, subscription, membership, protection, delivery, or marketplace take rate.
- Core measures include utilization, revenue per asset, payback period, damage/loss, cleaning, turnaround, delivery, and residual value.
- Availability is date based; one asset can generate many transactions but also repeated service cost.

## Growth and defensibility

Dense supply, utilization data, trust, operational turnaround, local logistics, category expertise, and repeat access can compound.

## Failure modes

Low utilization, inventory-heavy growth, underestimated cleaning and reverse logistics, double booking, damage disputes, and confusing recurring revenue with good asset economics.

## Avada opportunities

Date-based inventory, deposits, inspection, damage evidence, cleaning/repair queues, delivery scheduling, subscription access, utilization analytics, and buy-plus-rent storefronts.
