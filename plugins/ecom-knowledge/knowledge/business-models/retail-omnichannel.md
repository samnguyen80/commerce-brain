# Retail and omnichannel

## Model

The merchant coordinates physical stores with digital discovery, ordering, fulfillment, service, and returns. Omnichannel means one operating experience, not merely several channels.

## Economics and operations

- Revenue occurs across store, web, marketplace, social, and assisted channels.
- Store labor, occupancy, shrink, inventory placement, fulfillment, markdowns, and returns shape margin.
- Inventory accuracy and customer identity must survive channel switching.

## Growth and defensibility

Location, trust, convenience, assortment, service, fulfillment density, loyalty, and unified data can reinforce one another.

## Failure modes

Separate inventories, conflicting promotions, broken pickup promises, fragmented customer profiles, channel-level attribution fights, and digital features unsupported by store operations.

## Avada opportunities

Unified inventory, store fulfillment, clienteling, loyalty identity, returns orchestration, localized merchandising, and cross-channel analytics.
