# B2B2C and marketplace operator

## Model

The company enables third-party sellers or providers to transact with end customers. It must serve both supply and demand and govern the transaction layer.

## Economics and operations

- Revenue may be take rate, subscription, listing, ads, payments, fulfillment, lead fees, or services.
- The operator manages onboarding, catalog, search, liquidity, trust, safety, payment, disputes, and policy.
- GMV is not revenue; take rate is not contribution margin.

## Growth and defensibility

Network effects require useful participation, liquidity, repeat transactions, and low disintermediation. Workflow integration, trust, payments, data, and specialized supply can reinforce the network.

## Failure modes

Launching both sides too broadly, charging before value exists, supply without demand, demand without availability, fraud, weak quality control, and assuming network effects from user count alone.

## Avada opportunities

Seller onboarding, catalog normalization, marketplace search, commission and payout, trust/safety, dispute tooling, multi-party promotions, liquidity analytics, and operator dashboards.
