# Subscription box and kitting

## Model

Multiple SKUs or components are curated and assembled into a recurring or prepaid physical offer. Value comes from convenience, discovery, replenishment, personalization, or curation.

## Economics and operations

- Revenue is recurring, prepaid, build-a-box, or membership plus add-ons.
- Margin depends on procurement, packaging, pick-pack-kitting, postage, breakage, and support.
- Cash timing is shaped by billing date, supplier terms, cut-off dates, and inventory commitments.
- Churn includes voluntary cancellation and involuntary payment failure.

## Growth and defensibility

Retention, habit, personalization data, supplier access, and operational reliability compound. A box without an ongoing job-to-be-done becomes novelty inventory.

## Failure modes

Acquiring customers before contribution economics work; measuring MRR without cohort retention; overbuying components; weak skip/pause controls; ignoring dunning and fulfillment SLA.

## Avada opportunities

Churn intelligence, dunning, build-a-box UX, procurement/BOM planning, cut-off automation, subscriber cohort analytics, and 3PL exception handling.
