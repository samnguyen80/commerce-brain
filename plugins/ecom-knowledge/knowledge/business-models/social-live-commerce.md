# Social and live commerce

## Model

Discovery, persuasion, interaction, and sometimes checkout happen inside social feeds or livestreams. The channel rewards native content and real-time participation.

## Economics and operations

- Revenue may be direct shop orders, live sales, affiliate-attributed orders, or traffic to an owned store.
- Costs include content production, hosts, samples, commissions, ads, moderation, returns, and platform fees.
- The platform owns most distribution and much of the audience relationship.

## Growth and defensibility

Repeatable content formats, credible hosts, community, rapid merchandising, creator supply, and a content reuse system are stronger than isolated viral hits.

## Failure modes

Dashboard theater, policy violations, copied scripts, unsustainable host hours, weak post-live reuse, high returns, and dependence on one platform algorithm.

## Avada opportunities

Live operations, product pinning, moderation, deal scheduling, content-to-product attribution, creator asset reuse, returns/fraud analysis, and owned-customer capture where permitted.
