# Map opportunities for Avada

Read:

1. `../../knowledge/foundations/master-playbook.md`
2. `../../knowledge/concepts/evidence-and-coverage.md`
3. the relevant cards in `../../knowledge/business-models/`

## Qualification test

An opportunity must have:

- a recurring workflow or failure;
- a named operational owner;
- measurable cost, delay, risk, or lost revenue;
- a software or data leverage point;
- a plausible acquisition or distribution path;
- evidence strength stated as deep-corpus or catalog-only.

Distinguish a content gap from a product gap. Do not promote a category merely because few courses exist.

## Output

Rank no more than five opportunities. For each give: target coordinate, job, current workaround, evidence, product wedge, buyer, measurable outcome, distribution fit, and largest invalidating assumption. End with the smallest validation step, not a full speculative roadmap.
