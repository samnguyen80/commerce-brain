# Diagnose a merchant

Read `../../knowledge/foundations/master-playbook.md` and `../../knowledge/concepts/model-card-method.md`.

## Minimum facts

Ask only for missing items:

1. Offer and fulfillment setup.
2. Sales channels and checkout owner.
3. Revenue mix and repeat behavior.
4. Gross margin plus major channel, fulfillment, return, and labor costs.
5. Inventory or working-capital exposure.
6. The symptom the merchant wants to change.

First classify the merchant using `classify-commerce-model`. Then read the matching model cards.

## Output

Name one primary constraint, its evidence, the tempting but incorrect diagnosis, and one next measurement or job. Separate a channel problem from a fulfillment problem. If the supplied data cannot distinguish them, state the discriminating measurement instead of guessing.
