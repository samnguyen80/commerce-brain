# Compare commerce models

Read `../../knowledge/concepts/model-card-method.md`, then read every matching card in `../../knowledge/business-models/`.

## Comparison contract

Compare symmetrically:

| Dimension | Questions |
|---|---|
| Customer ownership | Who controls identity, checkout, and repeat communication? |
| Revenue | Transaction, subscription, take rate, fee, or hybrid? |
| Costs | Acquisition, supply, fulfillment, returns, service, platform, and labor? |
| Cash | Inventory commitment, payment timing, and working capital? |
| Operations | What breaks first at scale? |
| Growth | What repeatable loop creates demand or supply? |
| Defensibility | What compounds and what is rented? |
| Platform risk | Which rules or intermediaries can change economics? |
| Evidence | Deep corpus or catalog-only? |

End with a conditional recommendation: which model fits which constraints. Do not declare a universal winner. State evidence gaps and preserve dates for fast-moving categories.
