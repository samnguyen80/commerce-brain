## Realize any marketplace concept

 Most marketplaces built with Sharetribe are for products, services, and rentals. But Sharetribe can power many other types of platforms too. Here are some examples.

 - 

#### Classified ads
 Build a classic platform for classified ads in the vein of Craigslist and Gumtree. Use Sharetribe’s built-in listing creation and free messaging to help people connect. Charge a listing fee from users to get their listing published.
 
 + 

#### Bartering and gifting platform
 Create a marketplace where no money exchanges hands. Enable users to barter or gift their products or time.
 
 + 

#### Matchmaking site
 Build a site where you match people with businesses or each other. Such as a job board, a dating app, or something else. Use Sharetribe’s built-in search and messaging features to help people find each other.

 Build a marketplace where customers list what they need. Providers make offers based on the customer’s request and the customer chooses the best one.
 
 + 

#### Multiflow marketplace
 Enable multiple different transaction types from rentals to product sales, on the same platform. Let suppliers both offer services and rent spaces. Or rent and sell things. Add both regular and reverse transactions.

 Users and listings

 

### Let users create profiles and add listings.

 - 

#### User profiles
 Every user gets a profile page where they can add information. The profile includes their listings and reviews from other users to create trust.
 
 + 

#### Customizable user data
 Create different user types, like providers and customers, and build each group a unique user experience. Add custom user fields to the signup form to collect important information. Ask for different information based on user type. Restrict permissions to post listings, start transactions, or even view listings on user-by-user basis. Add operator-controlled metadata to assign things like verification or power-user status.
 
 + 

#### Streamlined listing creation
 Users enjoy a clear, step-by-step listing creation process. They can save draft listings and finish them later.
 
 + 

#### Stunning listing pages
 Choose the listing page layout that fits your marketplace best. Go for a cover photo or an image carousel.
 
 + 

#### Customizable listing data
 Create categories and subcategories. Add custom fields to create a unique listing creation experience. Collect all the relevant information about what your suppliers offer. Add operator-controlled metadata for things like search ranking, featured listing status, or trust signals like verification.
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 

 Search and matching

 

### Choose the search functionality and layout that fits your platform best.

 - 

#### Location or keyword search
 Make your primary search function location-based or keyword-based. Sharetribe’s versatile search experience works for both local and global marketplaces.
 
 + 

#### Map or list view
 Choose your search page layout. Map and listings for location-based marketplaces. Listings in a grid list for others.
 
 + 

#### Listing type filter
 Create unique search experiences based on the type of listing someone is looking for. Add a listing type filter on your search page or build entirely separate search paths for different audiences, such as buyers and sellers.
 
 + 

#### Category filter
 Help customers find what they&#x27;re looking for with listing categories. Create up to three levels of categories and add category-specific filters to build a dynamic search experience.
 
 + 

#### Custom search filters
 Customize your search experience to your platform’s needs. Use custom listing data fields as search filters and create an effortless matching experience for customers.

 Transactions

 

### Enable exchanges of value between users. Monetary or not.

 - 

#### Non-monetary transactions
 Make transactions conversations without online payments. Perfect for sites where matching itself is the value.
 
 + 

#### Bookings and product sales
 Support bookings by the day, night, or hour—or let providers define their own booking length. Enable product sales with inventory and shipping options. Or let sellers upload digital products to the listings that get unlocked automatically after purchase.
 
 + 

#### Price negotiation
 Build a marketplace where customers and suppliers can negotiate on the price, using a regular or reverse transaction flow. Customers can request for quotes from providers or post requests on the marketplace and get multiple offers.
 
 + 

#### Custom transaction fields
 Ask customers and providers for the right information at the beginning of a transaction. Use transaction fields to structure inquiries, quote requests, offers and more with relevant details and make transacting more easy and efficient.
 
 + 

#### Secure payments
 Enable secure payments through Stripe, complete with a KYC process for suppliers. Support payments in 26 countries. Find out if Stripe supports payments in your currency. 
 
 + 

#### Automatic payouts
 Stripe automatically sends a payment to the supplier once a booking is over. You do not need to send money manually.
 
 + 

#### Automatic commissions
 Define your commission percentage and an optional minimum commission fee in Console. Stripe automatically charges it on all bookings.
 
 + 

#### Reviews
 Build trust through two-sided reviews. Every monetary transactions automatically includes one to five star ratings and text reviews from both parties.

### What is a marketplace?
 An online marketplace is a website (or a mobile app) that brings supply from multiple people or companies to the same place. Customers find a wide variety of supply on the same platform and transact with suppliers. A transaction can be a purchase, a booking, a message, or anything that leads to an exchange of value between a customer and a supplier.
