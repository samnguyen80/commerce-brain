 Secure payments

 

### Monetize your rental marketplace through commission

 - 

#### Secure online payments
 Sharetribe uses Stripe to facilitate secure online payments. Stripe is an online payment processing solution for businesses of all sizes and has great solutions for marketplace payments.
 
 + 

#### KYC process for suppliers
 Collect all the necessary information from your suppliers to send them money. Stripe conducts a Know Your Customer process for each supplier who signs up to receive payments through your platform.
 
 + 

#### Payments in 26 countries
 Through Stripe, Sharetribe automatically supports payments in 26 countries. Find out if we support payments in your currency. 
 
 + 

#### Automatic payouts
 Once a booking is over, Stripe will automatically send a payment to the supplier. You do not need to send money to anyone manually.
 
 + 

#### Delayed payouts for bookings
 Stripe holds the customer&#x27;s payment until the booking period is over. Only after a successful rental transaction is concluded will the payment be sent to the supplier.
 
 + 

#### Automatic commission payments
 Define your commission percentage and an optional minimum commission fee in Console. Stripe automatically charges it on all bookings.

### What is a rental marketplace?
 An online rental marketplace is a website (or a mobile app) that brings rental property from multiple suppliers to the same place. Customers find many rentals on the same platform and transact with the suppliers. A transaction can be a calendar booking, an inquiry, or anything else that leads to an exchange of value between a customer and a supplier. Airbnb, Vrbo, and Turo are examples of popular rental marketplaces. Here&#x27;s our complete guide on building a rental marketplace .
