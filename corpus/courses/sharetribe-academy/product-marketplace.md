 Inventory management

 

### Enable sellers to manage their inventory

 - 

#### Multiple or one-item inventory
 One-item inventory is perfect for second-hand marketplaces and platforms selling unique items. Multiple-item inventory is great for marketplaces selling new products.
 
 + 

#### Inventory editing
 Sellers define how many items they have when creating a listing. The seller can manually modify inventory when editing a listing.
 
 + 

#### Automatic inventory reduction
 Inventory automatically reduces when products are sold. When the last item is bought, the listing closes automatically until the seller adds new inventory.

 Reviews

#### Five-star ratings and text reviews

### What is a product marketplace?
 An online product marketplace is a website (or sometimes a mobile app) that brings sellers of physical or digital products to the same place. Buyers find products from different sellers and transact with them. A transaction can be a purchase, an inquiry, a bid in an auction, a swap, or anything else that leads to an exchange of value between a buyer and a seller. eBay, Etsy, and Depop are examples of popular product marketplaces.
