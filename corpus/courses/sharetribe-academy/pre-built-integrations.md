## Easily connect your marketplace to industry-leading services to enable payments, analytics, maps, and other features

 
 
 
 
 
 

 Payments

 

### Process marketplace payments through Stripe

 - 

#### Secure online payments
 Sharetribe uses Stripe to facilitate secure online payments. Stripe is an industry-leading online payment processor for businesses of all sizes with great solutions for marketplace payments.
 
 + 

#### KYC process for suppliers
 Collect all the necessary information from your suppliers to send them money. Stripe conducts a Know Your Customer process for each supplier who signs up to receive payments through your platform.
 
 + 

#### Payments in 26 countries
 Through Stripe, Sharetribe automatically supports payments in 26 countries. Find out if we support payments in your currency. 
 
 + 

#### Automatic payouts
 Stripe will automatically send a payment to the supplier once a transaction is concluded. You do not need to send money to anyone manually.
 
 + 

#### Delayed payouts
 Stripe holds a customer&#x27;s payment until a transaction is concluded. The payment will be sent to the supplier after your marketplace signals Stripe that the transaction is over.
 
 + 

#### Automatic commission payments
 Define your customer and provider commission percentages in Console. Stripe automatically charges it on all paid transactions.
 
 
 Learn more about Stripe
