# Content management

## Create all the content your marketplace needs without coding

## Define your marketplace’s content, language, and style down to the smallest details

 
 
 
 
 

 Pages

 

### Manage your content without coding

 - 

#### Landing page
 Communicate your value proposition with a beautiful landing page. Add a search bar with multiple options. Include custom sections with text, images, and video. Feature categories, providers, and locations.
 
 + 

#### Content pages
 Create the content pages you need, like an FAQ or a team page. Design additional, highly converting landing pages. 
 
 + 

#### Featured listings
 Feature listings on any content page and create pages built around your actual supply. Highlight your latest inventory or highest reviewed listings, create targeted content for specific categories or location, or hand-pick curated collections.
 
 + 

#### Blog
 Create a simple blog for content marketing. Build your expert status and feature your suppliers in your blog.
 
 + 

#### SEO and social media
 Optimize your content for search engines and social media. Add SEO and social-media-specific meta tags, descriptions, and images to your pages. 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 

 Marketplace text editor

 

### Edit even the smallest texts in your marketplace to match your concept

 - 

#### Tone and style
 Modify the user interface texts and set the tone of your marketplace language. Is your marketplace calm and polite, warm and friendly, or whimsical and goofy?
 
 + 

#### Terminology
 Use the terms your target audience uses. Are your suppliers hosts, sellers, or providers? Are your customers guests, buyers, or clients? Change each word to fit your marketplace concept.
 
 + 

#### Button, error, and help texts
 Modify the smallest marketplace texts from button labels and to error messages and help texts to match your chosen communication style. 
 
 + 

#### Translate your marketplace into another language
 Translate the marketplace texts into the language of your choice to localize your platform.
 
 
 

 
 
 

 Email text editor

 

### Determine the text content of your marketplace’s email notifications

 - 

#### Tone and style
 Edit your emails to fit your tone and style. Is your marketplace casual or official, humorous or serious?
 
 + 

#### Terminology
 Use your marketplace’s terminology in your emails. Are listings rentals, services, or products? Are new transactions bookings, purchases, or inquiries?
 
 + 

#### Translate your emails
 Translate your email notifications into the language of your choice to localize your marketplace.
