 Users and listings

 

### Let users create profiles and add their services

 - 

#### Stunning service provider pages
 Choose the service provider page layout that fits your marketplace best. Go for a cover photo or an image carousel. The page will also display customer reviews.
 
 + 

#### User profiles
 Each customer and provider gets a personal user profile. User profiles include information about the individual or business and reviews to create trust.
 
 + 

#### Customizable user data
 Create unique user experiences for service providers and customers with user types. Add custom user fields to the signup flow to collect important information. Ask for different information based on user type. Restrict permissions to post listings, start transactions, or view listings on user-by-user basis. Add operator-controlled metadata to assign things like verification or power-user status. 
 
 + 

#### Streamlined listing creation
 Users enjoy a clear, step-by-step listing creation process. They can save draft listings and finish them later.
 
 + 

#### Customizable listing data
 Add custom fields and filters to create unique listing creation and search experiences. Create categories and subcategories for different types of services or requests. Create metadata fields, fully controlled by you, the marketplace operator, for search ranking, featured listings, or trust signals like verification.
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 

 Availability and bookings

 

### Let providers manage availability and bookings

 - 

#### Availability management
 Service providers set their availability in an intuitive availability management system. They define a default weekly schedule and add exceptions to availability. 
 
 + 

#### Flexible calendar bookings
 Your marketplace can have calendar bookings by the hour or by the day—or you can let providers determine any booking session length from 15 minutes to all the way up to 11 hours and 45 minutes.
 
 + 

#### Price variations
 Let service providers add several different price points, packages, or tiers to their listing—and manage all bookings of varying lengths in the same calendar to keep track of availability. 
 
 + 

#### One or multiple seats
 Choose whether services on your marketplace are one-on-one or if multiple people can join the same class, tour, or event.
 
 + 

#### Custom booking details
 At the start of a booking, ask customers for relevant information, such as current skill level for tutoring, so providers can prepare appropriately

 Secure payments

 

### Monetize your platform through commissions on bookings

 - 

#### Secure online payments
 Use Stripe to facilitate secure online payments. Stripe is an online processing solution for businesses of all sizes and has great solutions for marketplace payments.
 
 + 

#### KYC process for providers
 Collect all the necessary information from your providers to send them money through Stripe&#x27;s Know Your Customer process.
 
 + 

#### Payments in 26 countries
 Through Stripe, Sharetribe automatically supports payments in 26 countries. Find out if payments are supported in your currency. 
 
 + 

#### Automatic payouts
 Stripe automatically sends a payment to the provider once a booking is over or an order has been delivered. You do not need to send money manually or worry about regulation or compliance in money transfers. Stripe takes care of that for you.
 
 + 

#### Delayed payouts
 Stripe securely holds the customer&#x27;s payment until the booking period is over or an order has been delivered. Only after a successful transaction is concluded will the payment be sent to the provider.
 
 + 

#### Automatic commissions
 Define your commission percentages and an optional minimum commission fee in Console. Stripe automatically charges commission on all monetary transactions.

### What is a service marketplace?
 An online service marketplace is a website (or sometimes a mobile app) that brings services from multiple suppliers (providers) to the same place. Customers find many services on the same platform and transact with the providers. A transaction can be a calendar booking, a message requesting a quote, or anything else that leads to an exchange of value between a customer and a supplier. Upwork and Thumbtack are examples of popular service marketplaces.
