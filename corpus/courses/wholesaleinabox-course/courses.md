 Honestly, it will work really well for both newer folks and more established makers. Second, we want this to be a comprehensive reference that you can use in a targeted way now (we’ll help you navigate the sections) and return to over time as your needs and questions change.

 You say this course is for "makers" -- is it for me? 
 
 
 
 

 

 

 
 

 
 

 
 
 We’ve found that Wholesale In a Box is the best fit for folks who design and/or make something that they are really passionate about, and that is unique in the marketplace. Generally, these are handmade goods or ones that are designed in-house but produced in a thoughtful outsourced way. We’ve worked with folks who design and make themselves every single thing they sell; and others who designed a line and produce it overseas.
 Beyond that, we’ve worked with independent lines of all product types and all aesthetics -- and we work with any stage of makers, from complete newbies to folks with several hundred store accounts. To get a sense of the folks we’ve helped, head over here.

 Can you help me get accepted by Faire? 
 
 
 
 

 

 

 
 

 
 

 
 
 I know that can be really frustrating when you get absolute silence from the marketplaces. In all honesty, the marketplaces' acceptance decisions are based on a mix of their goals, their retailer base, how many makers they have of each type, plus technical considerations. So it's not always possible to do anything to change that. That said, our course will certainly cover the types of things you'll want to improve in your marketing and product to improve your chances of being accepted. 
 In other words, we don't tackle "what to do about silent marketplaces" directly but we will help you get your wholesale materials as good as they can be for overall wholesale success (likely improving your marketplace chances in the process.)

 I’m a maker based outside of North America. Will this be a good fit? 
 
 
 
 

 

 

 
 

 
 

 
 
 If you’re an international maker interested in growing wholesale with North American stores, we’ll be a PERFECT fit. If you’re an international maker interested in growing wholesale outside of North America, much of what we teach will be helpful to you -- but you may need to do some “translation” to your particular context. The majority of our experience is with small, independent retailers in the US and Canada. We have worked with a lot of international makers to help them grow wholesale, but it’s important to know that we have a primarily North American orientation when it comes to pitching methods. For instance, in the European Union, there are different guidelines around GDPR and what is appropriate to do in terms of reaching out to retailers… and there may be nuances to pricing, duties, taxes, wholesale terms, or presenting your products that differ from what is expected here.
