Hello and welcome to the DTC Podcast. Today, we’re diving into the world of whiskey with Duncan Frew from Wolfie's, the rock star whiskey brand co-founded with Rod Stewart. Wolfie's is not just another celebrity-endorsed product; it's a unique blend of Scottish tradition and rock and roll rebellion.

Key Insights:
Brand Origin: Inspired by late-night whiskey sessions and rock star stories, Wolfie's was born out of a desire to disrupt the traditional whiskey market.
Product Development: Focused on creating a high-quality product with extensive taste testing and market research.
Rapid Expansion: From a DTC launch to being sold in 23 countries within a year, Wolfie's is taking the world by storm.
Creative Marketing: Utilizing Rod Stewart’s persona to craft a brand that is both respectful of whiskey traditions and rebellious in spirit.
