way. Part three, I looked at, as you mentioned, an $80 billion market that hasn't changed for a long

really valuable thing to do and also a great thing to do. And here we are five years later,
all sorts of exciting clients, all sorts of exciting companies, all sorts of fun data to
talk about. And that's why we're here. And that's the fun of it. We exist to inform every intuition
