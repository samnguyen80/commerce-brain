The transition from summer to fall is one of the biggest merchandising shifts that you will make all year.
You're not just changing out your clothing but you're changing out the complete shopping mindset of your customers.
So they used to be shopping for tanks and denim shorts and for beach days and now they're completely changed over to fall items.
So cozy knits, denim, fall accessories, all of that good stuff.

When your customer walks into your store they need to be so inspired to shop for fall and that starts with you doing a big seasonal changeover so that they can feel that as soon as they walk in.
We often get asked when the best time is to do this flip and we always suggest towards the end of August.
So you always want to stay one step ahead of your customer and they will be looking to shop for fall as the weather shifts into September.
So if you stay one step ahead of them and you create that immersive fall shopping experience in your store towards the end of August,
