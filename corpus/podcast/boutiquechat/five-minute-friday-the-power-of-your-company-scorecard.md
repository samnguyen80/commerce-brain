And the idea is this.
I have coached for a long time for you guys on how to become a CEO versus an entrepreneur, right?
I want you to be a CEO that values time, right, more than DIYing and saving money along the way
so that you can have a life that you love to live and a business you don't feel like you are always

So the best way to do that is the company scorecard.
The idea is if you were to go on vacation on some beautiful island
and you didn't have any communication with your team and your business, right?
None of your employees could call you, text you, message you, no social media, nothing.
The only way you knew that your business was either being successful or had been burnt to
the ground was you had five metrics that you received on a company scorecard, and that was
your only measure of success.
So every week you got the scorecard.
Every week it was red or it was green, and you were making the decision, should I stay on
this island and enjoy my vacation or should I come back home?
So my challenge to you is what's your company scorecard?
What are the five metrics that do or die you and your company operate by?
I can guarantee you it is not sales alone.
Sales is a lagging indicator of what happened in the week prior, but it's not a leading indicator
that's telling you what is going to happen in the weeks to come.
So if I were you and I were considering what were a few of my scorecard numbers I might look
at, I might also consider average order value, traffic, customer acquisition, my customer
return rate, my dwell time.
I would look at what is my conversion rate.
I would look at what was happening in some of my different categories and classes and
where were the sales actually coming from.
You can slice and dice your company scorecard however you like.
My ask for you today is that you simply start one.
When we talk about in retail bootcamp or in membership at the hub, having a date with your
numbers, this is as simple as it could be.
It might be just having a spreadsheet with these few metrics that every single week you
sit down and you fill in.
What's really neat to watch happen, and we've watched this happen again and again with retail
bootcamp, is one of the first things we do together in retail bootcamp is we go through
and we have a massive company scorecard that we give you.
And you can assign, you know, what am I going to keep for myself to fill in for the scorecard?
Or what is my team going to help me fill in on a weekly basis?
And it's just holding you accountable to tracking those numbers.
The feedback I often hear when retailers fill in this company scorecard is, wow, I felt
that I was having a really bad week.
I felt that I was struggling.
I felt that I was, you know, falling behind and for this and that reason, whatever.

So I challenge you to get rid of the emotion.

scorecard for yourself.

that your team is operating smoothly and soundly with you.
Now, here's the next step.
Once you have your company scorecard in place, you have a date with your numbers, you have
a process, your entire team should be held accountable to what's happening on the scorecard.
So you would hire, you would train, and you would fire based on performance of the company
scorecard.

your team members by their participation and the outcome that they see together with you
