So here's how I like to attack my notebook. First, I like to circle the things that I'm like,
oh, that's good. I took a lot of notes and there's things that are like, oh yeah, that's a good
reminder. But the things that I'm like, oh, that's important. I need to remember that.
I'm going to circle those things. After I go through a couple of pages of circling,
I'm going to go back and I'm going to look for the things that are actions. They're things that
I must do. I need to take action and follow up on them. And I star all of those things.
Then I'm going to open a new notebook paper. And step three is I'm going to go look at all the
things I starred, which might be a lot. It might be really overwhelming, but I'm going to start to
prioritize all of my stars. I'm going to add in order in a running to-do list numbered one through 10 or
one through 50, whatever these things are. And then I'm going to assign dates behind them.
Like this is, I need to do it this week. Next in the list is I need to do it this month. After that
is this quarter, next quarter, fourth quarter, next year, or it's a big picture idea that I need to
take action on. That's going to help you actually absorb and put some accountability behind the things
you learn so you can actually see them come to fruition in your business.
All right. Now you might also, you know, not only have taken notes in your notebook,
but sometimes when I'm learning through a podcast or a book or something else, I also have notes in
maybe my notes app. What's been helpful for me is to create one master space where all of my notes
and my lessons and my good ideas go. For me, it's a Google doc. I know some people hate Google docs.
I just love them. So I'll take the notebook that I had at the event and I'll transpose that list and
those big things I need to remember into that overall Google doc. Same with my notes app. If
I'm listening to a podcast on the go, I'll take all those things from my notes app and I'll put it

I have all of this knowledge I've compiled from all these different amazing people
