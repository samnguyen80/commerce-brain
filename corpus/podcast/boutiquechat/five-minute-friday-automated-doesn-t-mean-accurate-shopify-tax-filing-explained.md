Hi everyone, it's Juliette here with BoutiqueBookkeeping.co.
Today I'm going to talk to you about something that we've been getting a lot of questions
about lately, Shopify's automated sales tax filing.
Automated doesn't always mean accurate, and that's something that business owners really
need to understand when it comes to sales tax.
A lot of boutique owners assume that when they turn automation on, everything is fully
taken care of.
But the reality is, Shopify might file your sales tax returns, but they don't take responsibility
for making sure that it's done correctly.
At the end of the day, you as the business owner are still the one responsible for what's
being filed.
And because the system is automated, there's no human reviewing your filings or catching
issues that could potentially slip through.
Things like marketplace sales, VAT being double counted, unsupported sales channels, or missing
transactions from certain apps or integrations.
There are also some limitations that business owners might not realize.
Shopify doesn't handle state registrations.
They don't support prepayments.
If something is filed incorrectly, Shopify will not do an amendment.
They recommend you seeking a professional.
Even Shopify's own disclaimer reminds users that tax laws change frequently and that business
owners are responsible for making sure that taxes are filed correctly.
Now that doesn't mean automation is bad.
Automation tools can absolutely be helpful and save time, but they should support the process,
not replace oversight entirely.
This is why understanding your numbers matters so much.
Reviewing your reports, knowing what's actually been filed, and having someone keep an eye on
the bigger picture, this is what truly protects your business.
If you're currently using automated filing and you haven't reviewed your setup in a while,
this is probably a good time to take a closer look and make sure everything is working the
way you think it is.
