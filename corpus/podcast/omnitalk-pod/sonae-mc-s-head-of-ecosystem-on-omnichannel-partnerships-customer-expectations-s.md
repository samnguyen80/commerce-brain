In this exclusive interview from the Walmart Commerce Technologies Studio at Shoptalk Europe, Bruno Mourão, Head of Ecosystem at Sonae MC, shares insights on the company's approach to omnichannel retail, partnerships, and meeting customer expectations. Sonae MC, a leading Portuguese grocery retailer with a strong presence in both physical stores and e-commerce, is focusing on expanding its ecosystem beyond grocery to include food, health and wellness, loyalty, medical services, and marketplaces.Bruno discusses the company's journey from hypermarkets to supermarkets, convenience stores, and franchising models, as well as its efforts to consolidate omnichannel services like click and collect, curbside pickup, and quick commerce.
