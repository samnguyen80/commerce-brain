In today's Omni Talk Retail Fast Five Podcast, sponsored by Microsoft, the A&M Consumer and Retail Group, Takeoff, and Sezzle, Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga had a blast:

- Taking a victory lap on Amazon’s price positioning in Amazon Fresh stores.

- Opening our minds to GNC's new food delivery service.

- Lauding Simon, after slagging it last week, for something it is calling “Simon Search.”

- Sounding alarm bells (that likely only our loyal Omni Talk fans will hear) on Amazon’s second Style store that is coming soon to Columbus, OH.

- And closed with a right proper debate on how cool Walmart's partnership with Roku really is.

There’s all that, plus favorite vacation drinks, the last time Anne took down a full box of Kraft Mac & Cheese, and the over/under on how many angry gun toting residents will actually shoot down Amazon's drones.
