In this week’s Omni Talk Fast Five Podcast, sponsored by the A&M Consumer and Retail Group, Firework, Trigo, Sezzle, and Silk, Chris and Anne discussed:

- What Amazon’s recently announced “pause” of its Amazon Fresh rollout really means.
- The pros and cons of Giant Eagle’s move to discontinue mailing its weekly advertising circulars.
- Instacart’s rather interesting choice of its first rollout partner, Foodcellar Market, for its new scan-and-pay technology.
- Belk opening its first outlet store and how much technology investment should even be made within this growing off-price retail trend. 
- And closed with a look at whether Gap’s new partnership with UPS subsidiary, Ware2Go, is cool or just a 3PL horse of a different color.

There’s all that, plus movie theater price discrimination, Adidas arm stripe tattoos, and whether the Cheesecake Factory is over or underrated.
