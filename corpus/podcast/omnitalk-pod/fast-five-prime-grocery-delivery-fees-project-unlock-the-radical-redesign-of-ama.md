In this week’s Omni Talk Fast Five Podcast, sponsored by the A&M Consumer and Retail Group, Firework, Trigo, Sezzle, and Silk, Chris and Anne:

- Found renewed optimism in Amazon Fresh’s newest stores in London
- Debated whether Walmart will come out on the winning end of Amazon charging Prime customers for grocery orders
- Lauded Sam’s Club for its history of innovation and store expansion plans
- Discussed the pros and cons of Walmart’s new-seller savings for its marketplace vendors
- And closed with a look at Lowe’s Project Unlock and why it is yet another important early stage use case for RFID with the customer at the heart of its design

There’s all that, plus Costco atop apartment buildings as the seventh circle of hell, engagement dos and don’ts, and the origin story of “never look a gift horse in the mouth.”
