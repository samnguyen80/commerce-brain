In the latest edition of the Omni Talk Retail Spotlight Series, Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga sit down with Talkdesk's VP Global Industry Strategy for Retail & Consumer Goods and fellow Top 100 Retail Influencer Shannon Flanagan.

Together the three of them discuss:

- The role of retailers to provide great product and great service
- The dwindling use of the "secret shop"
- Creating experiences that make your customers happy, when pre-purchase customer service is now just as important as post-purchase service
- How navigating 10 screens to handle a customer service issue is absolutely untenable
- And, why real-time cloud-based solutions that drive better customer service are now a bona fide weapon of brand differentiation
