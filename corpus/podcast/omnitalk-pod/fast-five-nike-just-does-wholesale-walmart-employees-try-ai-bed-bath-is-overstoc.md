In this week’s Omni Talk Fast Five, sponsored by the A&M Consumer and Retail Group, Firework, SPS Commerce, and Sezzle, Chris and Anne discussed:

- Nike’s shrewd handling of its wholesale distribution strategy
- Whether Macy’s Chief Digital Officer heading to Stitch Fix says more about Macy’s or Stitch Fix
- Walmart making the right move on AI for its employees
- Why Overstock potentially getting Bed Bath & Beyond’s assets for just north of $20 million is a steal of a deal
- And closed with a look at what Uber car sharing could mean for the future of commerce

There’s all that, plus Vanna White, San Francisco retail nostalgia, and what Anne’s hypothetical first day agenda as Krispy Kreme’s Chief Donut Dreamer would look like.
