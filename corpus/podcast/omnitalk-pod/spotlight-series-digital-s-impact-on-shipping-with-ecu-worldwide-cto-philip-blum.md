In our latest Omni Talk Spotlight Series Podcast live from the Manifest Conference in Las Vegas, brought to you in association with 1010data, we are pleased to bring you ECU Worldwide Chief Transformation Officer Philip Blumenthal.

Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga sit down with Philip to get his perspective on all things related to shipping products across an ocean. Together, they discuss:

– How e-commerce is driving demand for less than container full shipments
– Why there are some products consumers are willing to wait more than 2 days to receive
– And why Factory-to-Consumer (aka FTC) commerce will only continue to grow in the years ahead
