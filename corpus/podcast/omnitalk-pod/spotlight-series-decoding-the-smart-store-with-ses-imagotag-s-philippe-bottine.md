SES-imagotag's CEO North America Philippe Bottine joins Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga to discuss all the key ingredients that go into building a smart store and why the idea itself is so important to the future of retail.

Together the three of them discuss:

- Computer vision
- Cloud and edge computing
- Electronic Shelf Labels
- And, last but not least, both the operational and environmental benefits of building a smart store the right way.
