In today’s Omni Talk Retail Fast Five Podcast, sponsored by Microsoft, the A&M Consumer and Retail Group, Takeoff, Sezzle, and Silk, Chris and Anne:

- Expressed mutual joy (and sadness) over Peloton being sold at Dick’s Sporting Goods
- Discussed Walmart selling its tech to Asda and what it bodes for the strength of Instacart’s new business model 
- Lauded Schnucks for once again setting the pace of grocery innovation with its new flexforce employment option
- Praised and “yes, and-ed” Walgreens move into robotic fulfillment
- And closed with a look at Deliveroo’s new physical store partnership with Morrisons

There’s all that, plus hair transplants, NES Paperboy, and which type of Cheeto reigns supreme.
