As part of their Spotlight AI Series from the NVIDIA and Lenovo NRF Innovation Lab, Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga sat down with Vistry.AI Founder and CEO, Atif Kureishy, to learn how his company is leveraging Ai to improve the overall operations and consumer experience in the QSR industry. 

In this podcast, Atif does an excellent job of relaying all the different ways QSRs can find benefits from AI, including:

- Voice ordering automation at the drive thru
- Kitchen production control
- Ingredient forecasting to lower waste
- And much more, including our shared discussion of what the future of a QSR parking lot looks like!
