In this special Groceryshop edition of our Omni Talk Spotlight Series, sponsored by Cleveron and UntieNots, Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga interview Shipt Chief Business Officer Rina Hurst about Shipt's new partnership with Visa that gives free Shipt memberships to Visa card holders.

In addition, Rina also shares Shipt's three ring strategy for expansion:

- Ring 1 -- local and beloved grocers
- Ring 2 -- general merchandisers
- Ring 3 -- specialty retailers
