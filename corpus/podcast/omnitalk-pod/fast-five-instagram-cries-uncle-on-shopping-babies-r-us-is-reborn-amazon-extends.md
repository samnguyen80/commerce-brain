In this week’s Omni Talk Fast Five Podcast, sponsored by the A&M Consumer and Retail Group, Firework, Trigo, Sezzle, and Silk, Chris and Anne:

- Argued over whether Instagram removing its shop tab was a good idea
- Praised Amazon for taking Buy With Prime to all retailers
- Found much to like in the rebirth of Babies R Us and also had some fun predicting what could come next and with whom
- Took notice of Nestlé building its first smart DTC warehouse with Alibaba
- And closed with a look at the operational pros and cons of Arizona’s new “sip and stroll” law that lets mall shoppers shop with beer or wine in hand

There’s all that, plus Colin Farrell’s filmography, Cheetos pretzels, and the Kohler smart toilet feature Anne would prefer over all others.
