In this special Groceryshop edition of our Omni Talk Spotlight Series, sponsored by Cleveron and UntieNots, Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga sit down to chat with FRONTdoor CEO and Co-Founder Dan Bourgault.

When Chris and Anne first heard the news of what Dan and FRONTdoor collective have been working on, they immediately said to each other, "We have to get Dan on our podcast."

Dan and his team have created what they are calling the "first micro-last mile delivery network" with the capacity to cover 90% of residences and businesses in the US and Canada.

And the idea makes a ton of intuitive sense. 

There our hundreds of drivers out there delivering for Amazon and FedEx every day, so why not bring them together and forge better strength and bargaining power in numbers?
