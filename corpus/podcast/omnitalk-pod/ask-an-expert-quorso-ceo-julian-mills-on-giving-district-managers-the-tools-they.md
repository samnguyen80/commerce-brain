Retail store employees are drowning in workload like never before with very few tools to help them survive.

And, as a result, so much of their day is wasted on less impactful actions and convoluted processes that oftentimes get clouded and more confused by the actions of management from the top down.

Omni Talk Expert Quorso CEO Julian Mills once again joins Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga for the latest installment of the Omni Talk Ask an Expert Series to delve into one simple answer that may hold the key to solving the issue — making the District Manager's life simpler, easier, and more efficient.

Together they discussed how Agile Store Management principles deployed at the District Manager level, specifically, can help to:

- Simplify, speed up, and better engage store-level employees
- The exact recipe for success (exception management, prioritization, planning, and the recording of workflows week-on-week)
- And the 3 Stage Process to help you, the retailer, put everything into action as quickly as possible

All of which results in a District Manager more equipped and more engaged to lead his or her team with powerful insights and data as the guide.
