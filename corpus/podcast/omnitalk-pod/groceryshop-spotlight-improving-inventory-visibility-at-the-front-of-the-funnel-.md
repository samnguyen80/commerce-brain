In this special Groceryshop edition of the Omni Talk Retail Spotlight series, Blue Yonder's Senior Director of Solutions Strategy Erin Halka and Microsoft’s Director of Retail and Consumer Goods Paula Beckel join Chris and Anne to discuss the next generation order management capabilities required to succeed in this new era of omnichannel retailing.

Together they discuss:

- How inventory visibility is impacted across every level of a retail operation, from the warehouse to in store planograms to what is available on an e-commerce website
- Why having accurate inventory upfront in the purchase journey, on every product detail page, is essential
- And why a proper functioning cloud-based order management system is perhaps the most important foundational technology in which grocers should invest
