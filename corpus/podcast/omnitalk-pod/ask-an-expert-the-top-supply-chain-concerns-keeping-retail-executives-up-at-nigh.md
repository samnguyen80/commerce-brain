Supply chain. Supply chain. Supply chain. The topic is certainly top of mind of late, but what about it is really on the minds of your average retail executive?

So Omni Talk is pleased to announce that Microsoft’s Director of Retail & Consumer Insights Paula Beckel and Blue Yonder’s Corporate VP of Global Retail Industry Strategies Shannon Wu-Lebron joined Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga for the latest installment of the Omni Talk Retail Ask An Expert Series to answer that very question.

Is it forecasting? Staffing? Inventory accuracy? Mounting online fraud? Heck, even supply chain’s role in the organization overall?

It is a conversation that they promise will get to the heart of the real issues at play.
