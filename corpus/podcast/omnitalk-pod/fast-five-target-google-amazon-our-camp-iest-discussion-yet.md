In the latest edition of the Omni Talk Fast Five, sponsored by the A&M Consumer & Retail Group, Takeoff, and Manhattan Associates, Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga debate:

- Target's billion dollar plus move into alcohol delivery and what CEO Brian Cornell likely wears to the gym twice a day.
- Amazon Go Grocery's rebranding to Amazon Fresh and what it heralds for the future (hint: it's more than you think).
- Google and Shopify's new partnership and whether it will amount to a hill of beans.
- The rumors that the RealReal is planning to create its own private label line.
- And we close with a discussion about retail startup CAMP's latest innovation that lets 3 year-olds shop online for toys with their 1% parents' money.
