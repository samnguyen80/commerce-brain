In this latest episode of the Omni Talk Retail Spotlight Series, Cleveron Co-Founder Indrek Oolup returns to discuss with Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga the ins and outs of installing a fully functional parcel robot for package pickup at one of Zara's busiest locations -- it's new flagship store in Madrid.

Together the three of them discuss:

- The skinny on the 402 robot (i.e. the official name of the robot)
- What it took to coordinate the robot into the architectural design of the building
- The early lessons from the implementation since Zara's flagship opening in April
