In this week’s Omni Talk Fast Five Podcast, sponsored by the A&M Consumer and Retail Group, Firework, SPS Commerce, and Sezzle, Chris and Anne discussed:

– The pros and cons (mostly pros) of Walmart’s new social media-inspired website design 
– IKEA launching its As-Is program for used items
– The long-term potential of American Eagle deploying Radar’s inventory tracking system
– Kohl’s going all-in on a digital marketplace
– And closed with a look at why we just love Walmart’s new pickleball partnership with Break The Love

There’s all that, plus which of Anne’s parents would most likely be responsible for the two of us looking alike, annual pizza budgets, and dirty old Irish bars in San Francisco.
