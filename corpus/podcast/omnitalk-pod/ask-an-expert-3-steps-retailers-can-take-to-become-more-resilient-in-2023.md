Resilience is the hallmark of any great retailer. It is what separates those with staying power from the flash in the pans that garner so much unwarranted media attention.

Smart and steady always wins the race.

So Omni Talk is pleased to announce that Microsoft’s very own David Leibowitz and Ricardo Belmar joined Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga once again for the latest installment of Omni Talk’s Live Ask An Expert Series on LinkedIn to discuss the steps retailers can take right now to become more resilient in the year ahead.

Chris, Anne, David, and Ricardo will go deep on:

- Inventory and Pricing Optimization
- Returns and Margin Management
- Labor Allocation and Productivity
- Sensor Data and Energy Efficiency
- Marketing Analytics Optimization
