In this special OmniStars edition of the Omni Talk Retail Spotlight Series, brought to you in partnership with Quorso, Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga sit down with Raghu Mahadevan, the Chief Digital Officer at 7-Eleven, to discuss how he and his team continue to set the bar for convenience store innovation.

Together they discuss:

- The purposefully broad scope of Raghu's role at 7-Eleven
- How Raghu determines the right omnichannel priorities
- The ongoing feedback loop with 7-Eleven stores
- And, most importantly, where Raghu and 7-Eleven plan to take things next
