In this week’s Omni Talk Fast Five, sponsored by the A&M Consumer and Retail Group, Firework, SPS Commerce, and Sezzle, the A&M Consumer and Retail Group’s Truett Horne and Jeff Dwyer joined Chris and Anne to discuss:

- Walmart's DOA Bonobos acquisition and why it sheds light on just much (or how little) Marc Lore actually knows about e-commerce
- What went wrong with Lululemon’s purchase of Mirror
- How retailers should approach and contingency plan for the growing labor troubles at West Coast ports
- ShopRite placing large digital screens at its checklanes
- And closed with a look at what we can all learn from Petco’s latest recycling initiative

There’s all that, plus embroidered TMNT denim jackets, special sauces, and Anne’s serene, mammal-filled vision of Heaven.
