In this Omni Talk Retail interview, recorded live from the Consumer Goods Forum Global Summit 2026 in Vienna, Chris Walton sits down with Rhiannon Thomas, Global Lead of Consumer, Retail, and Luxury at Kearney, to discuss how retail and consumer goods companies are redefining value creation in an AI-driven world as Kearney celebrates its 100th anniversary.Rhiannon explains why traditional scale advantages are becoming less important than category expertise, how leading organizations are creating value by connecting functions across the business, and why AI success requires more than simply implementing use cases.
