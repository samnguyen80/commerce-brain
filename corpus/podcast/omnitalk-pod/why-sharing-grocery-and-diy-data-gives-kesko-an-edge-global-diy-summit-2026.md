In this Omni Talk Retail interview, recorded live from the Global DIY Summit 2026 in Amsterdam, Chris Walton talks with Harri Karumo, Senior Vice President and K-Rauta Chain Director at Kesko, about what makes Nordic home improvement retail unique and how Kesko leverages insights across multiple retail businesses.Harri shares his journey from finance to retail leadership, explains Kesko's retailer-owned business model, and discusses the opportunities and challenges of serving customers across Finland and the broader Nordic region.The conversation explores how Kesko combines data from both its grocery and home improvement businesses to better understand customer behavior, improve marketing effectiveness, and identify new opportunities for growth.
