In this week’s Omni Talk Fast Five, sponsored by the A&M Consumer and Retail Group, Firework, SPS Commerce, and Sezzle, Chris and Anne discussed:

– Walmart’s massive push and deployment of electronic shelf labels
– Shopify’s layoffs and its smart decision to sell off Deliverr
– Firework’s latest AI upgrade to its shoppable video and livestreaming capabilities
– Lululemon’s hotly contested strategy to thwart the dupe market
– And closed with a look at yet another brilliant move from Sephora, as it partners with Lucky to enable more efficient direct-to-consumer commerce for the brands it sells within its stores.

There’s all that, plus the sexiest robots of all-time, Miller High Life infused chocolate, and sewists.
