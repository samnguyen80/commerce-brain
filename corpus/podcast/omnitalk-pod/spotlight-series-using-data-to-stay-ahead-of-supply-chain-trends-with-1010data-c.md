In our latest Omni Talk Spotlight Series Podcast live from the Manifest Conference in Las Vegas, brought to you in association with 1010data, we are pleased to bring you 1010data CEO Inna Kuznetsova.

Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga sit down with Inna to get her insightful and oftentimes hilarious take on why data collaboration holds they key to getting out in front of retail trends. Together, they discuss:

– How better supplier and retailer data collaboration can ease supply chain bottlenecks
– What categories of business continue to grow amid the pandemic
– And why subscription businesses particularly found their niche over the last two years, especially on Amazon
