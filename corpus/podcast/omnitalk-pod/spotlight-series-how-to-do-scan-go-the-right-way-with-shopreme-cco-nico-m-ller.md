In this latest episode of the Omni Talk Retail Spotlight Series, Shopreme Chief Customer Officer Nico Müller joins Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga to discuss what it takes to make a scan-and-go checkout system really work. 

With all the noise surrounding Wegmans' announcement last year, Chris and Anne wanted to learn what makes scan-and-go so successful in Europe. Or said another way, what is the real recipe for success that everyone should follow?

To that end, Chris and Anne get the skinny from Nico on:

– Loss prevention tactics, particularly closed entry and exit points
- Easy mobile signup
- Localized rollouts vs. one store pilots
- Mobile-only payment options
- And the importance of hands-free shopping for larger grocery trips

There's a lot of noise out there surrounding scan-and-go shopping, and this podcast interview helps to clear it all up!
