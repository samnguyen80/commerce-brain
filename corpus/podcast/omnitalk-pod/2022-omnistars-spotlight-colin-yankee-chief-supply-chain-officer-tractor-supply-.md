In this special OmniStars edition of the Omni Talk Retail Spotlight Series, brought to you in partnership with Quorso, Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga sit down with Tractor Supply Company EVP and Chief Supply Chain Officer Colin Yankee to discuss how his company continues to deliver great omnichannel results and financial returns that far outpace its peers.

Together they discuss:

- How he defines omnichannel retailing
- His lessons learned around supply chain coming out of the pandemic
- How he envisions the future of supply chain forecasting
- And how Tractor Supply thinks about every aspect of their operations, particularly down to the field level, as they continue to set an aggressive pace for innovation
