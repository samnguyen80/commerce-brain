In this week’s Fast Five podcast, sponsored by Microsoft, the A&M Consumer and Retail Group, Takeoff, and Sezzle, Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga discuss:

- Amazon's shrewd move and likely "deke" to close 68 physical stores.
- Lululemon finally getting into footwear.
- Dollar General trying to become everyone's new local bank.
- Walmart's extremely disappointing new fit technology it unveiled this past week.
- And close with a look at why Ryan Cohen's letter to the Bed Bath & Beyond Board of Directors may be the coolest thing we have seen in a long time.

There's all that, plus Blind Dutchmen, more Sesame Street references, and why ventriloquists don't have podcasts.
