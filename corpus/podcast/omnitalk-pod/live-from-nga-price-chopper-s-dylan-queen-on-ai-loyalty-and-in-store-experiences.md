In this interview from the NGA show floor, Dylan Queen, VP of Marketing and Operations at Queens Price Shopper, shares insights on how independent grocers can leverage technology and innovative strategies to compete in a saturated market. Queen discusses the role of artificial intelligence in retail pricing, the importance of personalized advertising and loyalty programs, and the challenges of implementing self-checkout and scan-and-go solutions. He also emphasizes the significance of exceptional customer service and unique in-store experiences, such as retail theater, to differentiate independent grocers from larger competitors.
