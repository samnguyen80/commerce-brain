You heard it here first: embedded finance will be one of the biggest, if not the biggest, trends to impact retail customer loyalty over the next decade.

So Omni Talk is pleased to announce that Alviere’s Tim Dardis, VP of Sales and Business Development, will joined Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga for the latest installment of their Omni Talk Ask An Expert Series to provide his expert opinion on what embedded finance is, how retailers should approach it, and why it just makes so much financial sense.

Collectively the three of them went deep on:

– The overall trend of retailers looking to grow their income from services as opposed to products
– A step-by-step guide on how retailers can crawl/walk/run to implement embedded finance within their organizations
– And, finally, why more and more retailers are already beginning to offer a full suite of banking services that customers can use as an alternative to traditional banks, all in the name of deeper customer loyalty
