In the latest edition of the Omni Talk Women's Retail Collective Podcast, Anne Mezzenga sits down with Fanatics Chief Strategic Retail Officer Gina De Caro Sprenger.

Gina is like no other woman in retail we've interviewed. She wanted to work in this industry so badly, she applied (and was hired) for a job operating the elevator at a luxury department store at age 14!

She definitely made her way to the top floor. From running the entire home business for Target to her current role leading the retail store efforts for every sports team franchise under the sun.

Listen to Gina's entire career story and stay until the end to hear about how she became a member of the "Church Choir."
