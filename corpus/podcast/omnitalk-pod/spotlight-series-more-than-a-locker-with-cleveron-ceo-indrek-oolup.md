It’s been a trend throughout the pandemic, but BOPIS, Click-and-Collect, whatever you’d like to call it, is here to stay. It’s time to get a solid solution, and unfortunately, it’s not as simple as setting up lockers inside or outside your store.

 In this podcast, Anne Mezzenga and Cleveron Co-Founder Indrek Oolup dive deep into the complete strategy required to set your enterprise or SME retail operation up for success.

Omni Talk Fast Five fans will recall the news of Cleveron’s robotic fulfillment kiosk pilot with Jewel-Osco which Anne and Indrek also cover in this interview.

Indrek shares the successes and opportunities they’ve seen so far from that effort, and how that’s played into the development of future Cleveron products, including a new product Indrek announces exclusively to Omni Talk listeners in this podcast.
