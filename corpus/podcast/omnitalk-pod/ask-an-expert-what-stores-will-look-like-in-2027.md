Roy Horgan, SES-imagotag’s Group SEVP Strategy, Marketing & Communications, joined Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga for the latest installment of their Omni Talk Ask An Expert Series to discuss what retail stores will look like in the future as they become “smarter.”

Collectively the four of them go deep on:

– The increasing cost pressures that will drive the industry towards smart store adoption
– The growing importance of electronic shelf labels (ESLs) in smart store of the future design
– The degree of expansion expected over the next 5 years
– And, most importantly, all the capabilities unlocked from a truly smart store when ESLs are combined with computer vision
