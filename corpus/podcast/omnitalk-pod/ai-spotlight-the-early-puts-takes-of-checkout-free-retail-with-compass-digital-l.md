In what had to be one of our favorite podcasts on AI from the NRF Innovation Lab, Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga sat down with Compass Digital Labs CTO, Scott Wu, to learn how his massive company, the Compass Group, is looking to bring computer-vision based checkout-free retail to food service and the many micro-markets it is opening at an astonishing clip.

In this podcast, Scott does an excellent job of relaying all the puts and takes that come with computer vision deployments, including how to view:

- New builds vs. retrofits
- Cameras vs. sensor fusion
- Operating on the edge vs. in the cloud
