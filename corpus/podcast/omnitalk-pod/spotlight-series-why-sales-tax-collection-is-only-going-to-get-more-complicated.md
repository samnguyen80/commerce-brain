Avalara's Meg Higgins and BigCommerce's Sharon Gee join Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga to discuss all the pressures the omnichannel evolution has placed on retailers' abilities to collect tax accurately at a local level.

In this podcast, Chris and Anne go deep with Meg and Sharon on:

– The increasing amount of digital access points consumers have by way of things like digital marketplaces, BOPIS, cross-border retailing and more
– All the complicated tax issues that arise for retailers in trying to offer these capabilities to their consumers
- What it takes to get in front of these issues and to keep the IRS off your back
– And, most importantly, how tax collection will only get more complicated in the future as AI takes hold and we can all "checkout anywhere"
