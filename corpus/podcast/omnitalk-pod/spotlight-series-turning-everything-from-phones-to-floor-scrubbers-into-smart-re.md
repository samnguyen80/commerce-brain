What devices in your store could be working harder for you?

That is the question we posed to Scandit CTO and Co-Founder Christian Floerkemeier in our latest Omni Talk Retail Spotlight Series Podcast. 

Chris Walton and Anne Mezzenga discuss with Christian:

- How everything from an iPhone to a floor scrubber can become a smart data capturing device within a retail store
- Tackling hiring and training challenges by capturing smart data on an employee's own or company provided mobile device
- Removing mundane work from the employee's plate so they can focus on more important tasks
- And dramatically improving inventory visibility and with improved ROI and customer satisfaction
