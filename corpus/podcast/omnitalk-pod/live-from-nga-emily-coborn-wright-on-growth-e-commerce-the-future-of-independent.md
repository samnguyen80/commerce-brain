In this exclusive interview from the NGA conference, Emily Coborn Wright, SVP of Growth Initiatives at Coborn's, shares her insights on the evolving grocery industry and the role of independent grocers. As a fifth-generation leader in the family-owned business, Coborn Wright discusses Coborn's early adoption of online grocery shopping and delivery, and how their e-commerce model has propelled them forward. She also delves into the company's growth initiatives, including their focus on liquor, fuel, franchise, and pharmacy. Coborn Wright highlights the importance of NGA's advocacy efforts and legislation in supporting independent grocers.
