 Today’s Top Headlines 

 1. OpenAI&#8217;s First Device: A Screenless AI Companion Smart Speaker 

 OpenAI&#8217;s first hardware device will reportedly be a portable, screen-free smart speaker designed as a humanlike AI companion, per Bloomberg. It features a camera, sensors, and mechanical parts letting it move, controlling smart-home devices while learning owner preferences from personal data. A 2027 launch is targeted. ( Read More )

 2. Costco Opens First Standalone Gas Station in Mission Viejo, CA 

 Costco opened its first standalone gas station in Mission Viejo, California, on June 24, member-only with 40 fueling positions and no attached warehouse. A second standalone site is planned in Hawaii. Costco&#8217;s fuel business is setting volume records and strengthening member loyalty, executives said on its Q3 earnings call. ( Read More )

 3. Decathlon Rolls Out Vusion&#8217;s Digital Shelf Labels at 700+ Stores 

 Decathlon has deployed Vusion&#8217;s digital shelf label platform at 700 stores in 54 countries, automating price updates via electronic shelf labels linked to its Cisco Meraki network. The rollout boosts store efficiency, nearly eliminates shelf-to-checkout price discrepancies, and improves the in-store customer experience. ( Read More )

 4. Amazon DSP Reveals Which Retailers Convert After Ad Exposure 

 Amazon Ads has enhanced Omnichannel Metrics to show which specific retailers record offline purchases after shoppers see Amazon DSP ads. Available to U.S. self-service and managed-service advertisers, the feature reports retailer-level sales and units using exposure-based attribution from panel and third-party data signals. ( Read More )

 5. Kroger Launches New Premium Wine Shop Concept in 14 Stores 

 Kroger is launching Wine Shop concepts in 14 Cincinnati- and Dayton-area stores, featuring more than 130 curated premium and imported wines, tasting bars and trained stewards. The grocer will assess customer response before expanding, as it seeks to strengthen its alcohol offering and improve the overall in-store experience. ( Read More )
