 Today’s Top Headlines 

 1. EU&#8217;s Ban on Destroying Unsold Clothing and Shoes Takes Effect 

 The EU&#8217;s new Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation, effective July 19, bans large companies from destroying or landfilling unsold clothing, footwear and accessories. Firms with over 250 employees and €50 million-plus turnover must favor donation, repair or reuse. Medium firms join in 2030; exemptions require proof. ( Read More )

 2. House Passes Common Cents Act, Sets Rules for Penny Rounding 

 The House passed the Common Cents Act, setting formal rules for retailers to round cash transactions to the nearest 5 cents as the U.S. phases out the penny. A Senate version awaits committee action. Retail and banking trade groups back the bill, citing legal risk and a confusing state-by-state patchwork. ( Read More )

 3. Grocery Outlet Deploys Facial Recognition at Its Bay Area Stores 

 Grocery Outlet has begun using SAFR Guard facial recognition software at several Bay Area stores, including San Francisco, Concord and Pleasant Hill, to flag shoppers on a shared shoplifting watchlist. Signs alert customers entering. SAFR says it doesn&#8217;t work with law enforcement, though privacy advocates warn of surveillance risks. ( Read More )

 4. Instacart Acquires Arpalus to Boost AI Shelf-Inventory Scanning 

 Instacart has acquired Arpalus, an AI startup whose smartphone app lets workers record shelf inventory via computer vision, hitting about 95% avg item accuracy. The tech will integrate with Instacart&#8217;s app and Caper Cart smart carts, helping its 600,000 in-store workers track availability and cut unnoticed out-of-stocks. ( Read More )

 5. QVC Group Wins Court Approval for Bankruptcy Restructuring 

 QVC Group, owner of HSN and QVC, won court approval for its prepackaged restructuring plan, cutting debt from about $6.6 billion to $1.3 billion as it exits Chapter 11. Vendors will be paid in full. The company gets a new $600 million credit line and plans to relist publicly under ticker QVCG, focusing on live social shopping. ( Read More )
