 Hello Omni Talk Fans! Is it just me or is Wonder starting to smell like another overhyped Marc Lore startup with too many ingredients in the cake and not enough time in the oven?

 In today’s Retail Daily Minute: 

 

- Wonder  closes a $650 million Series D at a $9 billion valuation, with reports the food-platform company is gearing up for an IPO as early as next year.

- Instacart  acquires Arpalus, an AI-powered shelf-scanning startup, to give its in-store shoppers real-time inventory data via smartphone and Caper Cart cameras.

- CVS Health  opens its first pharmacy-focused store in Houston as it accelerates a scaled-down, pharmacist-centered format nationwide.

 Today’s Top Headlines 

 1. Wonder Raises $650M, Eyeing an Early 2026 IPO at $9B Valuation 

 Wonder, Marc Lore&#8217;s food-tech firm owning Grubhub and Blue Apron, raised over $650 million at a $9 billion valuation, positioning for an early-2026 IPO. New backers include Cathie Wood&#8217;s ARK Invest firm. The company plans robotics and AI investment, plus Texas expansion, despite projected billions in cash burn before 2030. ( Read More )

 2. Instacart Acquires Arpalus to Boost AI Shelf-Inventory Scanning 

 Instacart has acquired Arpalus, an AI startup whose smartphone app lets workers record shelf inventory via computer vision, hitting about 95% avg item accuracy. The tech will integrate with Instacart&#8217;s app and Caper Cart smart carts, helping its 600,000 in-store workers track availability and cut unnoticed out-of-stocks. ( Read More )

 3. CVS Health Expands Pharmacy-Focused Store Concept to Houston 

 CVS Health has opened its first pharmacy-focused CVS Pharmacy in Houston, with two more area locations planned within a year. The roughly 3,000-square-foot format offers a full-service pharmacy and curated OTC products. Nearly 20 such stores are slated to open nationwide over 12 months, part of a broader CVS expansion. ( Read More )

 4. Walmart U.S. COO Kieran Shanahan to Depart; Kinnard Steps In 

 Kieran Shanahan is stepping down as Walmart U.S. COO after nearly 30 years, staying on through fiscal year-end in an advisory role. Kyle Kinnard, formerly Walmart International&#8217;s COO, takes over immediately, with Juan Galarraga promoted to Latin America regional GM amid continuing leadership changes under CEO John Furner. ( Read More )

 5. Gap Taps Hailey Bieber for 90s-Themed Denim Capsule Campaign 

 Gap launched &#8220;The Hailey Jean,&#8221; a 1990s-inspired denim capsule with Hailey Bieber, priced at $89 for two relaxed fits, featuring a nostalgic video and Times Square billboards. The campaign follows Gap&#8217;s recent marketing wins pairing fashion, music and culture, as denim fueled a tenth straight quarter of comp sales growth. ( Read More )
