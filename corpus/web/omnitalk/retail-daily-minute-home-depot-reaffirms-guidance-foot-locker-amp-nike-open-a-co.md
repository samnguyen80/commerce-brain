 Today’s Top Headlines 

 1. Home Depot Beats Q2 Estimates, Reaffirms Fiscal 2026 Outlook 

 Home Depot beat Wall Street estimates in fiscal Q2 2026, with revenue up 5.7% to $47.86 billion and comparable sales rising 1.7%, the strongest showing since 2022. Net income reached $4.77 billion, or $4.79 per share. CFO Richard McPhail cited frozen housing market conditions as the retailer reaffirmed full-year guidance. ( Read More )

 2. Foot Locker & Nike Unveil Crenshaw Community Retail Concept 

 Foot Locker and NIKE, Inc. will open The Crenshaw Rec by Foot Locker on August 22 in South Los Angeles, a first-of-its-kind store blending retail with community space. Built with Crenshaw leaders, Jordan Brand and Converse, it features an open gym, exclusive Crenshaw collections, local hiring and year-round programming. ( Read More )

 3. DoorDash Adds Barnes & Noble, Carter&#8217;s and Kohl&#8217;s to Platform 

 DoorDash has added Barnes & Noble, Carter&#8217;s and Kohl&#8217;s to its platform ahead of back-to-school season, delivering books, kids&#8217; apparel and school supplies in minutes. The retail additions mark DoorDash&#8217;s first large-scale books offering and first department store selection, spanning Kohl&#8217;s 1,100-plus stores nationwide. ( Read More )

 4. Target Bets on Upscale Grocery Revamp to Win Back US Shoppers 

 Target is overhauling its grocery aisles to win back shoppers, expanding food space more than 20% in nearly 150 remodeled stores and over 50% in 300 new locations. The retailer is prioritizing fresh produce and trendy items while cutting slow-moving pantry staples, betting the revamp will drive $2 billion in sales growth. ( Read More )

 5. Apple Proposes 15% Commission on Purchases Outside App Store 

 Apple has asked a federal court to approve a 15% commission on purchases made through external links in iOS apps, with 5% for small business developers and 10% for subscription renewals and partner programs. Epic Games called the proposed fees far outside the court&#8217;s guidance in the companies&#8217; years-long App Store dispute. ( Read More )
