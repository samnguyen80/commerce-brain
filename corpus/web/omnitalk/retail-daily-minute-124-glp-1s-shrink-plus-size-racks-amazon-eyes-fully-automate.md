 Today’s Top Headlines 

 1. GLP-1 Surge Pushes US Retailers to Cut Plus-Size Clothing Lines 

 As GLP-1 drugs like Ozempic reshape body sizes, retailers are quietly scaling back extended sizing. Plus-size shoppers report vanishing size tabs online and fewer in-store options, while brands that once championed inclusive sizing retreat. Critics call it an excuse to sideline larger customers as skinny culture returns. ( Read More )

 2. Amazon Eyes Fully Automated Delivery Stations in Robotics Push 

 Amazon is reportedly developing Project Tetromino, an internal initiative to build fully automated delivery stations, according to a planning document obtained by Business Insider. It could rely on Boxbot conveyors and AI-driven storage trays that sequence packages for vehicle loading. Amazon disputes the report details. ( Read More )

 3. Instacart Adds Petsense as Exclusive Same-Day Delivery Partner 

 Instacart is now the exclusive same-day delivery partner for Petsense by Tractor Supply, the pet specialty chain that operates 209 stores across 23 states. Shoppers can order pet food and supplies via the Instacart app or website with no markups and delivery in as fast as an hour. Petsense joins 2,200+ retail banners there. ( Read More )

 4. Footwear Slump Drives Dick&#8217;s to Slash Its Full-Year 2026 Outlook 

 Dick&#8217;s Sporting Goods missed Q2 earnings estimates and lowered its full-year Foot Locker outlook, citing a challenging athletic footwear and apparel marketplace. Comparable sales rose 4.9% at Dick&#8217;s stores, helped by World Cup demand, while Foot Locker proforma comps fell 3.6%. Shares sank 30%, the worst day since 2023. ( Read More )

 5. Kroger Combines Grocery and Prescription Delivery in One Order 

 Kroger and Instacart now let customers combine groceries and eligible prescriptions in a single delivery order through Kroger websites and apps. Available across nearly all Kroger banners, the service connects more than 2,200 pharmacy locations, offering added convenience with prescriptions securely delivered to customers. ( Read More )
