 The Hatch Restore 3 

 We thought it would be fun to tease Prime Day coming up in October with a new Product Pick of the Week, so let’s kick things off with Producer Ella’s first pick of the week: the Hatch Restore 3. We’ll let Producer Ella take it from here:

 “Let me tell you, this thing has genuinely changed my sleep for the better.

 I’m a huge white noise sleeper, so having all of those sounds built in is already a win, but what really sold me is the sunrise alarm. You can personalize your nighttime and morning routines, and instead of waking up to a jarring alarm, the light gradually gets brighter to mimic the sun coming up, paired with calming sounds to ease you into the morning. By the time you actually open your eyes, your room is already lit up and you feel way more ready to start the day.

 It’s definitely an investment when you think of an alarm clock, but in my opinion, it’s one of those products where once you have it, you’ll wonder how you ever slept without it. Producer Ella approved.

 I love this series because I’m a marketer’s dream. Give me a good product recommendation and I’m sold. I can’t wait to share the rest of the products I’ve been loving!”
