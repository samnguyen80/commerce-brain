 Today’s Top Headlines 

 1. Amazon Prime Day 2026 Forecast to Hit Record $26.3B in Sales 

 Amazon Prime Day 2026 (June 23–26) is forecast to drive a record $26.3B in U.S. online spend—up 9% YoY and surpassing combined Black Friday & Cyber Monday 2025. Discounts up to 23% expected on apparel & electronics. Mobile to drive 54% of sales; AI traffic to retail sites to double. ( Read More )

 2. Duluth Trading Taps Amazon Fulfillment for Marketplace Push 

 Duluth Trading Co. is partnering with Amazon in a wholesale model—letting Amazon handle fulfillment, Prime shipping, inventory, and returns for its marketplace products. The move targets Amazon shoppers already searching for Duluth brands, while nudging them back to its own site for a fuller assortment. ( Read More )

 3. Bed Bath & Beyond Launches a Nostalgic 21-Day Coupon Hunt 

 Bed Bath & Beyond&#8217;s 21-day &#8220;Legendary Coupon Hunt&#8221; (through July 13) invites customers to bring old BB&B coupons to Kirkland&#8217;s Home and BB&B + Container Store locations for 20% off plus a shot at a $100,000 home makeover grand prize—a nostalgia play tied to its broader turnaround strategy. ( Read More )

 4. Target Pilots New Roundel x DirecTV Deal to Boost Ad Relevance 

 Target is piloting a Roundel x DirecTV partnership, using first-party shopper data to serve relevant video ads across DirecTV&#8217;s home screen, live TV, and travel venues—with closed-loop attribution back to Target purchases. Roundel now tops $2B in value, with a goal to double that by 2030. ( Read More )

 5. StockX Enters Live Commerce With Auctions and Its SoHo Store 

 StockX is launching StockX Live this summer—a live auction and social commerce platform with timed bidding, giveaways, and direct buyer-seller engagement. The move coincides with the reopening of its first permanent U.S. retail store in NYC&#8217;s SoHo, offering ~300 curated deadstock products plus seller drop-off. ( Read More )
