 In this week’s Omni Talk Retail Fast Five sponsored by the   A&M Consumer and Retail Group ,  Mirakl ,  Ocampo Capital ,  Quorso  and  Veloq , Chris Walton was joined by J Recruiting Services Founder and CEO Jenn Hahn for a Fast Five that explored what it really takes for retailers to keep pace with rapidly evolving customer expectations.

 From Ahold Delhaize&#8217;s decision to hire former Amazon Fresh executive Claire Peters as its next U.S. CEO to Kroger&#8217;s announcement that its e-commerce business has finally reached profitability, Chris and Jenn examined how grocery retailers are rethinking leadership, operations, and digital strategy. They also debated Bed Bath & Beyond&#8217;s attempt to rekindle customer nostalgia through its iconic blue coupons and whether bringing Isaac Mizrahi back to Target is the type of move that can help reignite the retailer&#8217;s signature cheap-chic appeal.

 Plus, Rohlik Group Chief Strategy Officer Rita Kerbaj joins the show to discuss why online grocery profitability is becoming more achievable, how dedicated fulfillment centers are changing the economics of same-day delivery, and why grocers need to think of stores, automation, AI, and fulfillment as part of one connected operating system.

 • 00:04:42 – Ahold Delhaize Names Claire Peters as Next U.S. CEO • 00:11:18 – E-Grocery Sales Surge 28% Year-Over-Year • 00:18:07 – Kroger Says Its E-Commerce Business Is Finally Profitable • 00:28:51 – Rita Kerbaj on Online Grocery Profitability, Fulfillment & Same-Day Delivery • 00:35:12 – Bed Bath & Beyond Revives Its Iconic Blue Coupons • 00:39:17 – Target Taps Isaac Mizrahi as Creative Director at Large • 00:45:19 – Lightning Round: Economists, Prime Day, Jersey Mike&#8217;s & Presidential Picks

 Throughout the episode, Chris and Jenn unpack why winning the weekly grocery shop is becoming more important than ever, what separates successful e-commerce operators from the rest of the pack, whether nostalgia is a viable retail growth strategy, and how retailers can balance bold leadership decisions with the operational fundamentals that customers notice every day.

 There&#8217;s also plenty of fun mixed in, including conversations about Prime Day purchasing habits, Jersey Mike&#8217;s versus Chick-fil-A, favorite economists and historical figures, grocery delivery preferences, and producer Ella Sirjord weighing in on why online grocery is becoming an increasingly valuable part of her routine.

 Tap in for insights on grocery innovation, retail leadership, e-commerce profitability, customer experience, and the strategic decisions shaping the future of retail.

 Be careful out there,

 Chris, Jenn, Ella, and the OmniTalk team
