 Today’s Top Headlines 

 1. Ahold Delhaize USA Expands Omnichannel Commerce Platform 

 Ahold Delhaize USA is expanding its omnichannel commerce capabilities by enhancing its proprietary digital platform across brands, delivering a faster, more personalized shopping experience. The cloud-based platform strengthens e-commerce, streamlines fulfillment, improves customer engagement, and supports scalable future growth. ( Read More )

 2. Kroger Debuts AI Shopping Assistant for Smarter Grocery Trips 

 Kroger has launched an AI Shopping Assistant across its websites and apps, helping shoppers plan meals, discover products, and build personalized carts based on budgets, dietary needs, and occasions. The tool can turn recipe URLs or photos of shopping lists into carts, making grocery shopping faster and more convenient. ( Read More )

 3. Macy&#8217;s Takes Down Its Iconic Herald Square Red Bag Billboard 

 Macy’s has removed its iconic red shopping bag billboard from Herald Square after more than 60 years, ending a landmark that defined the flagship store’s skyline. The sign’s removal will make way for modern branding while revealing the historic “Million Dollar Corner” building hidden behind the billboard for decades. ( Read More )

 4. Meijer Brings Online WIC Grocery Shopping to Michigan Stores 

 Meijer has become Michigan’s first retailer to accept WIC benefits for online grocery purchases, allowing eligible families to shop through its app or website with curbside pickup or free home delivery. The initiative expands food access, streamlines grocery shopping, and improves convenience for more than 187,000 WIC participants statewide. ( Read More )

 5. NYC Municipal Grocery Stores Plan 30% Discount on Essentials 

 Mayor Zohran Mamdani says New York City-owned grocery stores will sell a core basket of produce, meat, seafood and pantry staples 30% cheaper, saving shoppers roughly $90 monthly. Five municipal stores, one per borough, are planned by 2029, starting at Hunts Point in the Bronx by late 2027, with locked-in monthly pricing. ( Read More )
