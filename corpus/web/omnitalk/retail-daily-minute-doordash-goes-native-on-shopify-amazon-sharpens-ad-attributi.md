 Today’s Top Headlines 

 1. DoorDash Launches Native Shopify Channel for Local Retailers 

 DoorDash has launched a native Shopify sales channel, allowing U.S. brick-and-mortar merchants to list products on DoorDash without separate onboarding or manual uploads. Catalogs and inventory sync in real time, helping local retailers reach more nearby shoppers, launch within days and offer convenient on-demand delivery. ( Read More )

 2. Amazon DSP Reveals Which Retailers Convert After Ad Exposure 

 Amazon Ads has enhanced Omnichannel Metrics to show which specific retailers record offline purchases after shoppers see Amazon DSP ads. Available to U.S. self-service and managed-service advertisers, the feature reports retailer-level sales and units using exposure-based attribution from panel and third-party data signals. ( Read More )

 3. Instacart Launches Same-Day Delivery From 2,400 Tractor Supply Stores 

 Instacart and Tractor Supply have launched nationwide same-day delivery from more than 2,400 stores. Customers can order pet supplies, livestock feed, tools, hardware, apparel and garden products through Instacart, with delivery in as fast as one hour and no markups, expanding convenient access across rural communities. ( Read More )

 4. Kroger Launches New Premium Wine Shop Concept in 14 Stores 

 Kroger is launching Wine Shop concepts in 14 Cincinnati- and Dayton-area stores, featuring more than 130 curated premium and imported wines, tasting bars and trained stewards. The grocer will assess customer response before expanding, as it seeks to strengthen its alcohol offering and improve the overall in-store experience. ( Read More )

 5. Nordstrom Names Brooke Bobb Vice President & Fashion Director 

 Fashion journalist Brooke Bobb will join Nordstrom next month as vice president and fashion director. Based in New York, she will shape seasonal trends, support luxury and designer growth, and connect merchandising, brands, stores, digital and marketing, drawing on roles at Harper’s Bazaar, Vogue and Amazon Luxury Stores. ( Read More )
