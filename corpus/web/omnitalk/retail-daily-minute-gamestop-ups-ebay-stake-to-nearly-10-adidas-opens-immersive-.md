 Today’s Top Headlines 

 1. GameStop Boosts eBay Stake to Nearly 10% as Cohen Pushes Deal 

 GameStop now holds nearly a 10% stake in eBay, roughly double its May position, according to an SEC filing, as CEO Ryan Cohen keeps pushing for a takeover after eBay rejected the company&#8217;s $56 billion bid. Cohen wants a collaborative deal but remains determined, leaving the decision to shareholders if talks stall. ( Read More )

 2. Adidas Opens Immersive Concept Store at Mall of America in Oct. 

 Adidas will open its first full-price Minnesota store, an immersive 9,161-sq.-ft. &#8220;brand concept&#8221; location at Mall of America this October. The store features four zones, including a customization area, a footwear-focused &#8220;City Shop,&#8221; and an Anthony Edwards shop, following Adidas&#8217; strong first-quarter 2026 revenue growth. ( Read More )

 3. US Grocery Unit Sales Keep Falling for a Fifth Consecutive Month 

 U.S. grocery unit sales fell nearly 2% year over year in June, marking a fifth straight month of decline, according to Bain & Company and NielsenIQ. The West saw the steepest drop at 3%, while the Northeast fared better at 1.3%. Rising costs, SNAP cuts, and shoppers trading down or buying less are driving the pullback. ( Read More )

 4. Amazon Business Hits $60 Billion in Estimated Annual Revenue 

 Amazon Business hit $60 billion in annualized gross sales in Q2 fiscal 2026, matching its projected full-year revenue. The program serves 11 million-plus organizations, added 1.8 million users this year, and saved customers over $1 billion in 2025. Amazon is rolling out dedicated delivery trucks for businesses in 13 states. ( Read More )

 5. Apparel Alliance Releases Version 2 of Supply Chain Taxonomy 

 An apparel alliance of Apparel Impact Institute, Cascale, Textile Exchange, and ZDHC Foundation has released Version 2 of its Supply Chain Taxonomy, fixing gaps in the 2024 framework. It serves as a shared language for textile, clothing, leather, and footwear sectors, boosting transparency and collaboration. ( Read More )
