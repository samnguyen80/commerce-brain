 Hello Omni Talk Fans! This week&#8217;s Fast Five was recorded live from my hotel room in Vienna, Austria, where I sweated enough to put Richard Simmons on a hot griddle to shame.

 But the real heat this week was the headlines.

 I had the pleasure of co-hosting with the one and only Jenn Hahn of J Recruiting Services , who brought her trademark talent expertise, sharp takes, and the stark reminder that 80% of her grocery shopping is now done online. (Yes, 80%.) And, you will find out why that matters in just one second.

 Because in this week&#8217;s Fast Five Podcast, Jenn and I discussed: 

 

- Ahold Delhaize&#8217;s new US CEO pick: Jenn and I went back and forth on whether hiring a former Amazon exec with no traditional US grocery background is a bold signal of change or a risky double-outsider bet at the worst possible time.

- E-grocery surging 28% year over year: I encouraged grocers to look to Sweden of all places before the situation becomes a five-alarm fire.

- Kroger&#8217;s e-commerce turning profitable for the first time: Is it a feel-good story or a warning sign? Here&#8217;s a hint: I was peering over the edge of a bridge, judging the height of the jump, by the time we finished talking about it.

- Bed Bath & Beyond&#8217;s coupon sweepstakes: I gave it a 99 out of 10 on the gimmick scale, while Jenn compared it to Jeep&#8217;s George Washington giveaway. We both liked it that much.

- Target names Isaac Mizrahi as Creative Director at Large:  I draw on a personal anecdote to tell you why going back to the well on a retread idea is the opposite of what Target needs right now.
 

 There&#8217;s all that, plus Rita Kerbaj from the Rohlik Group joins us for an excellent five-minute breakdown on what it takes to make online grocery profitable, i.e. dark stores, automation, same-day economics, all of it, aka the whole enchilada.

 And we also announce this month&#8217;s OmniStar in partnership with Quorso.  Congrats Gabe Disbrow, President & CEO of Leevers Supermarkets , who, according to Jenn, just wants everyone on his team to do epic sh*t.
