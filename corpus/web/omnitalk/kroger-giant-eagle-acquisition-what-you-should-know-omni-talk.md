 In this week&#8217;s OmniTalk Retail Fast Five, sponsored by the A&M Consumer and Retail Group ,  Mirakl ,  Ocampo Capital ,  Quorso  and  Veloq , Chris Walton was joined by recurring Fast Five favorite Ben Miller, independent retail analyst and former VP of Content at Shoptalk, to discuss how AI, grocery consolidation, and evolving retail technology are reshaping the industry&#8217;s competitive landscape.

 From Kroger&#8217;s $1.65 billion acquisition of Giant Eagle to Amazon&#8217;s $1 billion investment in embedding AI engineers directly within customer organizations, Chris and Ben explored what these moves reveal about the future of grocery retail and enterprise AI. They also debated Lowe&#8217;s expansion of marketplace selling through store associates, New Jersey&#8217;s proposed restrictions on AI-powered pricing and electronic shelf labels, and Amazon&#8217;s latest effort to build consumer trust through 365-day product price history.

 • 00:03:16 – Kroger Acquires Giant Eagle for $1.65 Billion • 00:12:46 – Amazon Invests $1 Billion in Embedded AI Engineering Teams • 00:17:37 – Lowe&#8217;s Brings Marketplace Products Into the Store Experience • 00:22:47 – New Jersey Targets AI Pricing & Electronic Shelf Labels • 00:32:22 – Amazon Adds 365-Day Price History to Alexa Shopping • 00:38:41 – Lightning Round: Rotisserie Chicken, World Cup, Wimbledon & More

 Chris and Ben also debate whether Kroger&#8217;s Giant Eagle acquisition is a sign of what&#8217;s to come for the grocery industry, why AI adoption is becoming more important than the AI models themselves, whether marketplace selling belongs in every retail format, and why electronic shelf labels have suddenly become one of the hottest topics in retail technology. 

 There&#8217;s also plenty of fun mixed in, including discussions about Costco versus Sam&#8217;s Club rotisserie chicken, World Cup fandom, Wimbledon traditions, decorative lawn ornaments, and producer Ella sharing why Lowe&#8217;s marketplace integration was her favorite headline of the week.
