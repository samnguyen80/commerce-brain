 Today’s Top Headlines 

 1. U.S. Customs Inspects Chinese-Linked Factories Across Vietnam 

 US customs officials inspected China-linked factories in Vietnam, examining records and production processes to gauge value-added content and potential software IP violations. This stokes fears of broader Vietnam tariffs amid tense trade talks, though no clear evidence of illicit Chinese transshipment has surfaced yet. ( Read More )

 2. Instacart Debuts Smart Caper Trolleys at Morrisons in Preston 

 Instacart launched AI-powered Caper smart trolleys at Morrisons Preston, marking its first UK retail partnership. The trolleys recognise items, weigh produce, show running spend totals, apply More Card loyalty savings and speed checkout, helping shoppers stay on budget while discovering products as they browse the aisles. ( Read More )

 3. TikTok Tests Amazon Prime-Style Membership Called Shop Plus 

 TikTok is reportedly testing a paid US membership called TikTok Shop Plus, modeled on Amazon Prime, per screenshots seen by Business Insider. Priced at $6, $10 or $15 monthly, it offers free shipping, discounts and coupons, aiming to stop shoppers from discovering products on TikTok but purchasing them on Amazon instead. ( Read More )

 4. NYC Municipal Grocery Stores Plan 30% Discount on Essentials 

 Mayor Zohran Mamdani says New York City-owned grocery stores will sell a core basket of produce, meat, seafood and pantry staples 30% cheaper, saving shoppers roughly $90 monthly. Five municipal stores, one per borough, are planned by 2029, starting at Hunts Point in the Bronx by late 2027, with locked-in monthly pricing. ( Read More )

 5. David&#8217;s Bridal Launches a Hybrid Outlet Shop in McAllen, Texas 

 David Bridal launched The Outlet Shop, a hybrid concept pairing full price bridal wear with discounted wedding, bridesmaid, prom and special occasion dresses inside its existing McAllen, Texas store. The retailer plans more outlet shop-in-shops nationwide this year, continuing its Aisle to Algorithm technology strategy. ( Read More )
