 Hello Omni Talk Fans! I returned from a two-week vacation, which meant I had time to do actual math on this week&#8217;s podcast. More on that in a second.

 This week I was joined by the wonderful Jenn Hahn of J Recruiting Services (aka our Resident Talent Expert), who was back in the guest host chair, and we covered a LOT of ground, including several real, honest-to-goodness disagreements that made this week&#8217;s podcast one of the most spirited episodes we have had in quite some time.

 Here&#8217;s a taste of what Jenn and I sunk our teeth into: 

 

- Kroger naming a chief e-commerce officer — Nate Faust, a Jet.com co-founder and former Walmart exec, is stepping in. Jenn thinks it&#8217;s a smart signal. I think the organizational structure is bizarre. What do you think?

- Best Buy&#8217;s incoming CEO betting on going smaller — Jenn&#8217;s buying, I&#8217;m selling. I even brought in some aforementioned math to back me up.

- Ahold Delhaize&#8217;s kiosks heading to Giant Food — Is a physical kiosk still the right tool for digital savings in 2026, or is this solving yesterday&#8217;s problem? Jenn makes the case for &#8220;bridge,&#8221; not &#8220;typewriter.&#8221;

- Amazon&#8217;s Alexa for Shopping&#8217;s wild numbers — 350 million shoppers, 40% higher order values. We debate whether Amazon can be the one to finally tip AI shopping from research into real transactions.

- TikTok testing a Prime-style membership — $20 billion of GMV moving through TikTok Shop is nothing to sneeze at, and Shop Plus might just be how they keep revenue from leaking to Amazon.
 

 There&#8217;s all that, plus Arber Sejdiji of Zenline AI joined us for 5 Insightful Minutes on why an AI agent is basically like &#8220;a new employee on day one,&#8221; and we also handed out this month&#8217;s OmniStar Award, in partnership with our friends at Quorso, to Ralph Woodward, SVP of Retail Operations at Bashas&#8217; , a true 40-year retail lifer with the stories to prove it.
