 Today’s Top Headlines 

 1. Saks Global Exits Bankruptcy as a New Exemplar Luxury Group 

 Saks Global exited bankruptcy as &#8220;Exemplar Luxury Group,&#8221; cutting debt 65% to $1.2B and closing roughly 100 stores, now running 49 locations. The retailer shut most of its off-price fleet, rebuilt vendor relationships past 700 partners, and ousted Richard Baker, targeting profitability within three years, a major retail shift. ( Read More )

 2. Prime Day Delivers Mixed Signals Despite Record $26.4B Sales 

 Amazon Prime Day 2026 hit $26.4B in U.S. online spend (+9.3% YoY), per Adobe, fueled by electronics, mobile, and AI-driven traffic. But Numerator found average order size and household spend fell 11% and 8% respectively, suggesting growth came from more orders, not bigger ones. Mobile shopping hit a record $14.2B in spend. ( Read More )

 3. Rhode Island First State to Mandate Self-Checkout Staffing Law 

 Rhode Island became the first state to mandate self-checkout staffing ratios, requiring grocery stores to assign one worker per three stations. Backed by UFCW, the law aims to curb theft and reduce strain on shoppers and staff, with fines up to $500 daily for violations. Other states are weighing similar shoplifting restriction. ( Read More )

 4. California&#8217;s SB 54 Packaging Law Now Binds Retailers Statewide 

 California&#8217;s SB 54 packaging law now has permanent regulations and active compliance deadlines, yet many retailers wrongly assume it only applies to manufacturers. Even exempt businesses must get formal state confirmation, as the law creates regulatory, contractual, and supply-chain risks for noncompliant retailers statewide. ( Read More )

 5. GameStop&#8217;s Cohen Turns Down $35B Pay Amid eBay Deal Focus 

 GameStop CEO Ryan Cohen withdrew his $35 billion pay package to focus fully on acquiring eBay, which rejected his cash-and-stock offer in May. Cohen plans to commit $500 million of his own money and envisions using GameStop&#8217;s 1,600 stores for live commerce fulfillment and content creation studios. ( Read More )
