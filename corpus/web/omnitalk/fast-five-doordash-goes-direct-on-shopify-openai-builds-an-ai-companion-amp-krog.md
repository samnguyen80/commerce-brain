 Hello Omni Talk Fans! Believe it or not, I yet again found two more people willing to argue with me on the internet. This week&#8217;s Fast Five features Alex Brakebill and Brandon Pezely from the A&M Consumer and Retail Group , and, while Brandon has guest hosted before, this was Alex&#8217;s very first rodeo behind the microphones. 

 And, he wasn&#8217;t afraid to speak his mind one bit.

 Because we got into it as we debated all the below on this week&#8217;s Fast Five Podcast: 

 

- DoorDash&#8217;s new native Shopify integration:  Alex, Brandon, and I all landed on different numbers on my 1-to-10 scale for how big a deal this is, and I&#8217;m still the most bullish one in the room (an 8 or 9, thank you very much).

- OpenAI&#8217;s first hardware device:  Jony Ive&#8217;s team is building a screenless AI speaker that moves around your house. Brandon looked up the Terminator timeline. I&#8217;m a soft sell. Alex already has an iPhone in his pocket and doesn&#8217;t see the point.

- The EU&#8217;s ban on destroying unsold clothing:  The new regulation is officially in effect now, and Alex and Brandon both think large fashion companies will find quieter ways around it than anyone wants to admit.

- Kroger&#8217;s new Wine Shop concept:  14 stores, 130+ curated bottles, and trained wine stewards. Brandon&#8217;s bullish, I&#8217;m skeptical it scales past a few Cincinnati-area locations, and we get into what this really says about Kroger trying to find its identity.

- Marc Lore&#8217;s Wonder gearing up for an IPO:  $650 million raised at a $9 billion valuation, and all three of us are asking the same question &#8212; does a nascent food hall company (with a potentially vaporware twist) really deserve a valuation higher than Wendy&#8217;s?
 

 There&#8217;s all that, plus Producer Ella joins us to talk about overconsumption in fashion, and we delve into Messi&#8217;s World Cup finale, homemade snow cones, the Avengers: Doomsday trailer, and a meteorite that allegedly contains &#8220;alien world chemistry.&#8221;
