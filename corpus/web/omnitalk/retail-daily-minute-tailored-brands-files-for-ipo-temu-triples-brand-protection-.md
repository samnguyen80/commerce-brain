 Today’s Top Headlines 

 1. Tailored Brands Files for an IPO Following Years of Turnaround 

 Tailored Brands, parent of Men&#8217;s Wearhouse, Jos. A. Bank, Moores and K&G, has filed to go public under ticker &#8220;MENW.&#8221; After its 2020 bankruptcy, the retailer&#8217;s turnaround grew net sales to $2.5 billion and net income to $217 million, regained market share, and set plans to open 500+ new stores over the next decade. ( Read More )

 2. Temu Triples Brand Monitoring, Tightens IP Enforcement Ratio 

 Temu&#8217;s 2026 IP Protection Report shows expanded enforcement: its monitored brand database tripled to 15,000+, proactive-to-reactive removal ratio rose to 331:1, and detection systems now cover 47 million images. The platform rejected 40%+ of new sellers, terminated 16,000+ stores, and deepened ties with 130+ IP groups. ( Read More )

 3. Amazon Dominates Late-Night Impulse Shopping, Survey Finds 

 A CouponFollow survey finds 56% of U.S. consumers impulsively shop on Amazon late at night, far outpacing other retailers. Overall, 75% have made unplanned late-night purchases, often driven by emotion or FOMO. Gen Z overspends most ($541/year), while clothing and beauty top purchase categories, especially among women. ( Read More )

 4. Ulta Beauty to Open $400 Million Times Square Flagship Store 

 Ulta Beauty is opening a 27,000-square-foot flagship in Times Square, its first-ever, paying $400 million for a 15-year lease. The four-level megastore aims to showcase upscale brands and experiential retail, helping Ulta compete with Sephora and elevate its image beyond its strip-mall roots as it expands internationally. ( Read More )

 5. Hasbro Enters Adult Crafting Market With Play-Doh&#8217;s Blooms 

 Hasbro is launching Blooms by Play-Doh, its first adult-focused Play-Doh line, letting users craft and preserve floral arrangements with kits starting around $25 at Amazon, Target and Walmart, plus TikTok Shop from July 16. The move taps rising adult interest in toys, which drove 35% of industry growth this year per Circana. ( Read More )
