 Today’s Top Headlines 

 1. Walmart Finally Opens Its Checkouts to Apple Pay and Google Pay 

 Walmart is ending its long holdout on tap-to-pay, announcing it will accept Apple Pay and Google Pay at Walmart and Sam&#8217;s Club starting August 24. Select locations go first, with all stores and clubs covered by year-end and fuel stations by mid-2027. The retailer had long pushed Walmart Pay and the failed CurrentC instead. ( Read More )

 2. Ross Stores Comps Soar 10% as Off-Price Share Shifts From TJX 

 Ross Stores posted a 10% jump in Q2 comparable sales, its second straight quarter of double-digit growth, as new, lapsed and existing shoppers drove traffic up 16%. Total sales rose 13% to $6.3 billion and net earnings climbed 68%. The gain contrasts with TJX&#8217;s 1% Marmaxx comp, and Ross lifted its 2026 store plans to 115. ( Read More )

 3. Home Depot Rolls Out 3-Hour Express Delivery to All US Markets 

 Home Depot has taken its Express Delivery service nationwide, promising eligible orders in three hours or less by using more than 2,000 U.S. stores as fulfillment hubs. Pro and DIY shoppers pay a $7 flat fee ($10 in Los Angeles), with no membership required, and executives say the majority now arrive in under one hour. ( Read More )

 4. Albertsons Says AI Shopping Assistants Lift Baskets by Up to 26% 

 Albertsons says its AI shopping tools are lifting online basket sizes almost immediately, with average order value up about 10% for standard conversational search and 26% for fuller assistants that handle recipes and dietary needs. The grocer is now merging Ask AI, Plan AI and Buy AI into a single streamlined assistant. ( Read More )

 5. TikTok App Code Hints at Venmo-Style Payments Sent Inside DMs 

 Hidden code in TikTok&#8217;s US iPhone app points to a peer-to-peer payments feature letting users send money inside direct messages, with recipients tapping to accept and senders attaching a note, Venmo-style. It would run on TikTok Pay, currently live only in Southeast Asia. TikTok says it isn&#8217;t testing this yet anywhere. ( Read More )
