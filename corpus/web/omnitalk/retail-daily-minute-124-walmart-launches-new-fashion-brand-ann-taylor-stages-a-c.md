Producer Ella is fresh out of college, has been with us about one year, and what I love about her is that she has a great love of product (something that often gets overlooked amid all the discussions about technology).

 So we are debuting something fun and new today. It is called “Producer Ella’s Product Pick Of The Week.” Each week Ella will pick a product that she loves and share it with all of you.

 Today we start with the Hatch 3 because who knew I needed a sunrise-simulating alarm clock but I did.

 In today’s Retail Daily Minute: 

 

- Walmart launches Scenario, a new under-$25 women&#8217;s fashion brand taking over shelf space from Time and Tru, as the retailer leans on fashion to offset its weakest comp sales growth in six years.

- Ann Taylor debuts &#8220;This Is Ann,&#8221; its first major marketing campaign since 2017, as parent company KnitWell Group looks to reconnect the heritage brand with career-minded shoppers.

- Stripe&#8217;s president predicts the traditional checkout page is dying as agentic commerce tools roll out across ChatGPT and Gemini.

 Today’s Top Headlines 

 1. Walmart Deepens Its Fashion Focus With a New Clothing Brand 

 Walmart is expanding its fashion ambitions with Scenario, a new women’s clothing brand for younger, trend-conscious shoppers. With most items priced under $25, the retailer hopes stylish, affordable apparel and accessories will strengthen clothing sales and help it compete more directly with Amazon and department stores. ( Read More )

 2. Ann Taylor Relaunches With Substack and Fall Fashion Campaign 

 Ann Taylor is mounting a comeback with “This Is Ann,” its first major integrated campaign since 2017. Targeting modern working women, the push highlights fall fashion, a New York Fashion Week pop-up and its Substack, The Desk, as the brand works to reconnect with loyal shoppers and attract a new generation of customers. ( Read More )

 3. Stripe Predicts AI Agents Will Replace Online Checkout Screens 

 Stripe predicts agentic commerce could eventually replace the traditional online checkout page, allowing AI agents to complete purchases directly from product pages. The company is already advancing this shift through Instant Checkout in ChatGPT, its Agentic Commerce Protocol and integrations with Google’s Gemini globally. ( Read More )

 4. Kroger Combines Grocery and Prescription Delivery in One Order 

 Kroger and Instacart now let customers combine groceries and eligible prescriptions in a single delivery order through Kroger websites and apps. Available across nearly all Kroger banners, the service connects more than 2,200 pharmacy locations, offering added convenience with prescriptions securely delivered to customers. ( Read More )

 5. Gap Inc. Brings Encore Members to New York Fashion Week Show 

 Gap Inc. is taking its Encore membership program to New York Fashion Week with six days of exclusive experiences from Sept. 9–14. Members across Gap, Old Navy, Banana Republic and Athleta can access runway shows, designer events, live music and cultural experiences, ending with a Gigi Perez performance across New York. ( Read More )
