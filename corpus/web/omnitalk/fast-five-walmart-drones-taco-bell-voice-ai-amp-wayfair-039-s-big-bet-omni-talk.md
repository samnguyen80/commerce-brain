 Hello Omni Talk Fans! This week&#8217;s Fast Five podcast featured not one but two co-hosts.

 I was joined by the always fanstastic Shelley Huff of The Interval (and former CEO of Serta Simmons, who also used to lead home for Walmart.com), and Chris Niesen, a former exec at Target, Party City, and Whole Foods , who now runs Peloton One Consulting and writes the Retail in Real Time substack.

 Here&#8217;s a fun fact too &#8212; Chris and I have known each other for 26 years, going all the way back to our Gap days when he used to call and ask me to send him more Easy Fit jeans when he was managing the Mall of America store.

 Yes, retail really is that small of a world, and it was great finally to get him on the show, despite him possibly oversharing about his fear of rabbits.

 Here&#8217;s a taste of what the three of us discussed on this week&#8217;s Fast Five Podcast: 

 

- Ocado CEO Tim Steiner  stepping down

- Albertsons  rebuilding how its merchants think

- Walmart&#8217;s  drone delivery expansion in Houston

- Taco Bell&#8217;s  voice AI closing in on 900 restaurants

- And  Wayfair  betting big on massive physical stores
 

 There&#8217;s all that, plus Simbe&#8217;s Caitlin Allen joined us for 5 Insightful Minutes on how shelf intelligence can give retailers heretofore unattainable insights, along with what Chris Niesen would build with adult Play-Doh, what Shelley likes to buy on Amazon at 2 AM, and whether the Cyclospora outbreak has changed any of our salad eating habits.
