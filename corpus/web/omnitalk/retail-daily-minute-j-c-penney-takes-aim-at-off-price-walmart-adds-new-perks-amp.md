 Today’s Top Headlines 

 1. J.C. Penney&#8217;s Campaign Takes Direct Aim at Off-Price Retailers 

 J.C. Penney is targeting off-price rivals with a new &#8220;retail rejuvenation&#8221; campaign framing discount shopping as regret rather than treasure hunting. A tongue-in-cheek ad and an Aug. 28-30 offer give shoppers $15 off $50 for dropping off regretted buys, as Penney fights its sliding sales against TJX, Ross and Burlington. ( Read More )

 2. Walmart+ Boosts Perks With Free Prints, Tire Repair and Banking 

 Walmart is boosting the value of its $98-a-year Walmart+ membership with three new perks: 25 free 4&#215;6 photo prints every month, one fee-free Money Center transaction each quarter covering check cashing, money orders, transfers or bill payments, plus free flat tire repair and a road hazard warranty on qualifying new tires. ( Read More )

 3. Buc-ee&#8217;s Eyes Its First Iowa Travel Center in Des Moines Suburb 

 Buc-ee&#8217;s is in the early stages of planning its first Iowa travel center in Urbandale, a Des Moines suburb near interstates 80 and 35. The city&#8217;s planning commission backed a land-use amendment for the 20-acre site, but rezoning and site plan approvals remain, and the Texas chain would face Casey&#8217;s, Maverik and Kwik Trip. ( Read More )

 4. Target Beauty Studio to Replace Ulta at More Than 600 Locations 

 With the Ulta Beauty shop-in-shop partnership ending in August 2026, Target will launch its own Target Beauty Studio this fall in the more than 600 stores that housed Ulta. The revamped space will carry over 60 new-to-Target prestige brands, giving the retailer full control of its beauty assortment and all of the sales. ( Read More )

 5. Meijer Lets Shoppers Book Vaccine Appointments Well in Advance 

 Meijer is letting customers book vaccination appointments at rx.meijer.com before doses arrive in inventory, covering flu, RSV, shingles, pneumonia and COVID-19. The Midwest retailer is also running free on-site clinics for workplaces and community groups, and all eligible recipients can now earn mPerks loyalty points. ( Read More )
