 In this week&#8217;s OmniTalk Retail Fast Five, sponsored by the A&M Consumer and Retail Group ,  Mirakl ,  Ocampo Capital ,  Quorso  and  Veloq ,  Chris Walton was joined by Shelley Huff, CEO + Co-Founder of The Interval, and Chris Niesen, Founder of Peloton One Consulting and author of the Retail in Real Time Substack, to unpack the biggest stories shaping retail today, from grocery automation and AI-powered merchandising to drone delivery, voice AI, and the future of physical stores.

 The trio examined what the departure of Ocado founder Tim Steiner means for automated grocery fulfillment, debated whether Albertsons&#8217; new AI merchandising platform will truly transform decision-making, and discussed Walmart&#8217;s rapid expansion of drone delivery across Texas. They also explored Taco Bell&#8217;s continued rollout of voice AI ordering and why Wayfair is doubling down on large-format stores despite a challenging furniture market.

 • 00:06:07 – Ocado CEO Tim Steiner to Step Down After 28 Years • 00:14:57 – Albertsons Scales AI-Powered Merchandising Intelligence • 00:21:54 – Walmart Expands Drone Delivery Across Houston • 00:29:38 – Five Insightful Minutes with Simbe&#8217;s Caitlin Allen • 00:36:45 – Taco Bell Nears 900 Voice AI Drive-Thrus • 00:44:51 – Wayfair Bets Big on Large Physical Stores • 00:51:32 – Lightning Round: Play-Doh for Adults, Late-Night Amazon Shopping, Ballpark Food & More

 Chris, Shelley, and Chris also discuss why automated grocery fulfillment still faces an economic challenge despite impressive technology, how AI success depends more on organizational adoption than the models themselves, why drone delivery is becoming a fulfillment strategy rather than a novelty, and what retailers can learn from Wayfair&#8217;s disciplined omnichannel expansion.

 There&#8217;s also plenty of fun mixed in, including debates over adult Play-Doh, late-night Amazon impulse purchases, produce safety, the best ballpark food, and producer Ella explaining why Taco Bell&#8217;s voice AI rollout was her favorite headline of the week.
