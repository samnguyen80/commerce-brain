 1. Amazon Fights New Mamdani-Backed NYC Delivery Worker Bill 

 A New York City bill backed by Mayor Zohran Mamdani would force Amazon to directly employ thousands of contracted couriers, ending third-party delivery arrangements that critics say shield it from liability. Sponsored by Council Member Tiffany Cabán, the Delivery Protection Act has majority support and could pass this fall. ( Read More )

 2. Walmart Gets First Symbotic SymMicro System in Back of Store 

 Symbotic has begun installing its first SymMicro back-of-store fulfillment system inside a Walmart store, with CEO Richard Cohen saying it should be running in about six months. A second site will follow shortly after, potentially triggering wider rollout under Walmart&#8217;s contingent pledge to purchase 400 automated systems. ( Read More )

 3. Primark Debuts Its First Minnesota Store Inside Mall of America 

 Primark enters Minnesota on Sept. 3 with a nearly 35,000-square-foot store at Mall of America, its 46th U.S. location across 15 states. The value retailer, which debuted in Indiana in July and opens a second greater Orlando site Aug. 20, will stock Vikings gear via its NFL tie-up alongside its signature budget pricing. ( Read More )

 4. Kroger Names Jet.com Co-Founder Nate Faust eCommerce Chief 

 Kroger has hired Jet.com co-founder and former Walmart e-commerce supply chain executive Nate Faust as its first chief e-commerce officer, effective Sept. 1. The appointment is the latest outside hire under CEO Greg Foran and follows a quarter in which Kroger&#8217;s online arm posted 19% growth and turned its first profit. ( Read More )

 5. Schnucks Launches AI Shopping Assistant, Interactive Weekly Ad 

 Schnuck Markets has launched two digital features: an AI shopping assistant built with VitalityIP that generates lists, meal ideas and nutrition guidance from over 6 billion lines of data, plus an interactive weekly ad allowing coupon clipping and cart additions. The 112-store grocer will keep offering printed ads too. ( Read More )
