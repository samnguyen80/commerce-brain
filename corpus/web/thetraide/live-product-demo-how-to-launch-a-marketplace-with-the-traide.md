## Understanding the end result: The Mochi Munch marketplace 
 The demonstration began with a look at "Mochi Munch," an example international snacks marketplace. This sample marketplace showcased what&#x27;s possible with Nautical Commerce, featuring:
 A professionally designed homepage with hero banners, navigation, featured categories, and popular products
- Organized category and collection pages for easy product discovery
- Seller storefronts with customizable branding and descriptions
- Multi-vendor checkout that allows customers to purchase from multiple sellers in a single transaction The impressive part? The entire marketplace was built using Nautical&#x27;s no-code tools and drag-and-drop editor.
 
 Mochi Munch storefront 

## Setting up your marketplace: The admin experience 
 The first step to setting up your marketplace is signing in to your dashboard and inputting your key business details. 

### Business details 
 Starting a marketplace requires just a few basic details:
 
- Company name and marketplace name
- Headquarters address
- Default time zone and currency 

### Customizing your storefront 
 To customize your storefront, you need to take a few key actions, including: 
 
- Upload your logo and set your color palette
- Choose your fonts (with direct integration to Google Fonts)
- Configure your navigation structure with simple drag-and-drop functionality
- Set up your domain name when you&#x27;re ready to launch The drag-and-drop editor allows marketplace owners to build and modify their homepage and other pages without coding knowledge. You can add components like featured product sections, category showcases, or seller highlights with just a few clicks, and changes appear instantly without requiring rebuilds or publishing delays.

### Product configuration 
 To publish an effective marketplace storefront, you need clean product data with which to populate the store. 
 In the admin dashboard, operators can set up their product architecture through “product settings.” This is where they can: 
 
- Create categories for hierarchical organization of products
- Define collections for arbitrary groupings (like "Best sellers")
- Set up product attributes (like flavor, size, or country of origin)
- Create product templates that enforce consistent data across similar products This structured approach ensures high-quality product data, while giving marketplace operators control over what information sellers must provide.

### Shipping and payment setup 
 The next step is setting up shipping and payment details. 
 When it comes to shipping, operators can establish their shipping rules under “settings.” In this section, you can: 
 
- Define your shipping model (hybrid vs. fulfilled by marketplace vs. fulfilled by sellers)
- Control your shipping rates (based on seller order total vs. marketplace order total)
- Set your shipping zones and rates
- Define warehouse locations for marketplace and sellers To set up payments, head to the apps marketplace. You can integrate with payment gateways like Stripe with minimal configuration.

## The seller experience 
 One of our favorite parts of the Nautical platform is how it creates a streamlined experience for sellers joining your marketplace.

### Seller onboarding 
 Nautical makes it simple for vendors to join your marketplace:
 
- Sellers can apply directly through a customizable application form
- Marketplace operators review and approve applications
- Vendors accept your terms and commission structure through a digital agreement in the product 

### Seller account setup 
 Once approved, sellers can quickly set up their presence:
 
- Configure payment accounts for receiving payouts
- Define shipping options or use marketplace-defined shipping zones
- Customize their storefront with logos, banners, and descriptions
- Add staff accounts for team collaboration 

### Product management 
 Sellers have flexible options for adding products:
 
- Direct product creation through an intuitive interface
- Bulk CSV import for large catalogs
- Integration with existing Shopify stores
- Support for product variants (like different pack sizes) The platform enforces the product templates and attributes defined by the marketplace, while giving sellers the freedom to manage their inventory effectively.

## The customer experience 
 From a buyer&#x27;s perspective, Nautical provides a seamless shopping experience:
 
- Intuitive browsing across categories and collections
- Ability to shop from multiple vendors in a single session
- Unified checkout process with straightforward shipping options
- Streamlined payment processing The multi-vendor checkout is particularly notable. Customers can purchase products from different sellers without completing multiple transactions, creating a frictionless experience that encourages larger basket sizes.

## Fulfilling and paying out orders 
 Once an order is placed, Nautical provides robust tools for marketplace operations behind the scenes:
 
- Order management with visibility into fulfillment status
- Automated calculations for seller payouts
- Commission tracking based on marketplace agreements
- Detailed statements for transparency with sellers
- Built-in analytics for tracking marketplace performance The platform handles complex financial orchestration automatically, including calculating commissions, managing adjustments, and processing payouts to sellers.
