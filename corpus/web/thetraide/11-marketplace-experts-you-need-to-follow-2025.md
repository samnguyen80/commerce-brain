 Anyone who’s launched a marketplace knows—going at it alone can be challenging. The good news? The internet is full of marketplace experts who generously share their wisdom. Whether you’re bootstrapping a startup, looking to scale, or simply hoping to stay on top of industry updates, following seasoned ecommerce and marketplace pros can fast-track your journey to success. With so much experience available online, both free and paid, why not take advantage of it?
 Below, we’ve rounded up some of our top picks, organized by medium, so you can dive right into their expertise. From podcasts and social media to books and communities, these are the marketplace experts you don’t want to miss in 2025.

## Top Marketplace Experts on Social Media

### Jason Greenwood, Founder, Greenwood Consulting
 
 Jason Greenwood is a trusted name in ecommerce and a seasoned consultant for B2B and DTC companies. His LinkedIn feed is filled with insights on ecommerce trends and strategy tips that marketplace founders and business owners alike can use to stay ahead. On his podcast, Ecommerce Edge , he interviews industry leaders and covers everything from online retail trends to marketplace growth tactics, making it a must-follow for marketplace operators looking to stay informed on the larger ecommerce landscape.
 Where to Find Him: LinkedIn , The Ecommerce Edge Podcast 

### Jason “Retailgeek” Goldberg, Chief Commerce Strategy Officer at Publicis
 
 Jason Goldberg is an excellent source for up-to-date retail news, sharing the latest on industry shifts and retail data analysis on his LinkedIn and Twitter accounts. He also has a podcast, The Jason and Scot Show , where he and his co-host, Scot Wingo, share more detailed thoughts, making him an ideal follow for anyone wanting to keep their finger on the pulse of ecommerce news and understand its implications for marketplaces.
 Where to find him: LinkedIn , Twitter , and The Jason and Scot Show 

### Adi Thacker, GM and VP, Monetization and Ads at Poshmark
 
 Adi Thacker specializes in monetization strategies and ads, with direct insights on how to leverage these tools in marketplaces. His posts on LinkedIn provide marketplace operators with the latest approaches to monetization, revenue strategy, and platform optimization. Adi’s expertise is ideal for those looking to understand marketplace ad strategy and how to maximize revenue streams.
 Where to Find Him: LinkedIn 

## Top Marketplace Experts Who Wrote a Book

 Tom McFadyen leads McFadyen Digital— an agency focused on helping companies grow their ecommerce and marketplace initiatives. His book, Marketplace Best Practices, dives into marketplace strategy and practical frameworks for building profitable online marketplaces. For operators looking for a comprehensive approach to marketplace creation, his work is foundational.
 Read the book : Marketplace Best Practices  

### Lisa Gansky, Co-Founder, Mesh Ventures 
 
 Lisa Gansky ’s influential book on the Mesh Economy presents a strong argument for the future of the sharing economy. Her insights are particularly relevant for marketplace founders interested in the intersections of community, sustainability, and profit.

### Andrew Chen, General Partner at Andreessen Horowitz
 
 Andrew Chen ’s book tackles growth strategies for digital products, making it a crucial read for marketplace startups. His work on network effects provides a deep understanding of user acquisition and engagement strategies, which are essential for marketplace success.
 Read the book : The Cold Start Problem.  Andrew also has a newsletter .

## What Does a Marketplace Expert Do?
 Marketplace experts bring strategic insights, practical advice, and sometimes even hands-on help to marketplace startups and businesses alike.
 They can guide founders on everything from platform design and user experience to growth strategies and monetization. The best marketplace experts have often launched their own platforms or consulted with multiple marketplace startups, so they bring experience-based insights to each situation.

## How Can a Marketplace Expert Help My Business?
 Experts can offer perspective on current market trends, customer acquisition, and revenue streams. They might suggest partnerships, technology solutions, or operational efficiencies that could drive significant improvements in growth and engagement. For new founders, tapping into this expertise can help avoid costly mistakes and reduce time-to-market.

## Trends in Digital Marketplaces for 2025
 Experts agree that the marketplace landscape will continue to evolve. Some key trends include:
 Niche Marketplaces : Vertical marketplaces targeting specific industries will continue to grow.
- AI and Automation : From product matching to customer service, marketplaces are increasingly leveraging AI for better user experiences.
- Community-Led Growth : Communities are powerful tools for marketplaces, and founders are creating spaces where users can connect, share, and learn from each other. As you explore the world of marketplaces, consider following some of these experts to stay updated on these trends and make the most of your business potential.
