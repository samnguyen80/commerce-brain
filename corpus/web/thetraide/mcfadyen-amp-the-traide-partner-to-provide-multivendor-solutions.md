 Vienna, VA – June 20, 2023 – McFadyen Digital , a leading marketplace strategy and implementation agency, today announced it has formed a strategic partnership with Nautical Commerce to enable customers with complex B2B and B2C use cases to launch a highly customizable marketplace with confidence. As a complete multi-vendor commerce platform that provides commerce, fintech, and logistics capabilities in one solution, Nautical’s forward-thinking technology and strategic approach to implementation allow customers to build unique digital solutions with more flexibility and speed. It also eliminates the need for customers to invest in more than one platform. 
 McFadyen Digital’s global team has over 25 years of commerce success and over 15 years of marketplace expertise. With McFadyen’s guidance, customers are empowered to maximize their return on investment and build a scalable, profitable ecommerce experience. 
 “We’re thrilled to be working with McFadyen to help customers on their digital transformation journeys. At Nautical, business enablement is our top priority, and that goes beyond the technology itself. Proper guidance and strategy is a significant piece of the puzzle to ensure success in digitizing multi-vendor commerce,” said Ryan Lee, CEO and founder of Nautical Commerce. “I am confident that McFadyen’s expertise in business planning and management will be an invaluable asset alongside Nautical’s headless, composable technology. I look forward to partnering with McFadyen on many successful projects to come.”
 “Nautical Commerce is an intriguing option in the marketplace platform space for those who prioritize flexibility and composability. Their cloud-agnostic microservices-based approach means those seeking a modern customization architecture have almost limitless options for their multi-vendor commerce needs,” remarked McFadyen Digital CEO & Author, Tom McFadyen. “This partnership allows us to offer our clients another reliable platform right-sized to their needs.”
 The formal partnership represents a step forward in multi-vendor commerce by providing businesses with more accessible solutions to launch and scale their operations with less risk. 
 About Nautical Commerce
