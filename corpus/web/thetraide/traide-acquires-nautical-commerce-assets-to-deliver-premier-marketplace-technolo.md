 Today, I&#x27;m excited to share that Traide has acquired the technology assets of Nautical Commerce, a significant milestone in our mission to make multi-vendor commerce accessible to every business.
 ‍

### Why This Matters
 Nautical Commerce built a powerful, enterprise-grade marketplace platform trusted by businesses across B2B, B2C, and digital commerce. Their technology — spanning multi-vendor order management, seller onboarding, marketplace payments, and headless commerce infrastructure — represents years of deep investment in solving the hardest problems in marketplace operations.
 ‍
 At Traide, we share that same vision: empowering businesses to launch, operate, and scale marketplaces with speed and confidence. By bringing Nautical&#x27;s proven technology into the Traide platform, we&#x27;re combining the best of both worlds — Nautical&#x27;s robust commerce engine with Traide&#x27;s commitment to accessibility, flexibility, and an exceptional operator experience.
 ‍

### What This Means for Customers
 For existing Nautical Commerce customers: Your platform, your data, and your operations are in good hands. Continuity of service is our top priority. You&#x27;ll continue to receive the same level of support you&#x27;ve come to expect — and over time, you&#x27;ll benefit from accelerated product development, new features, and a team that&#x27;s deeply invested in your success.
 For Traide customers and future marketplace operators: This acquisition significantly strengthens our platform capabilities. You&#x27;ll benefit from battle-tested marketplace infrastructure that has powered multi-vendor transactions at scale, along with expanded integrations, improved seller management tools, and a faster pace of innovation.
 ‍

### Our Vision Going Forward
 The marketplace model is reshaping commerce. Businesses of every size — from startups to enterprises — are recognizing that multi-vendor platforms unlock new revenue streams, expand product assortments, and create better experiences for buyers and sellers alike.
 With this acquisition, Traide is positioned to be the platform that makes this transformation possible for everyone. We&#x27;re investing in:
 Platform reliability and performance — building on Nautical&#x27;s proven infrastructure
- Ease of use — ensuring marketplace operators can launch and manage their platforms without a dedicated engineering team
- Flexibility — supporting headless, composable architectures that let businesses build the marketplace experience their customers deserve
- Ecosystem growth — expanding our integrations, partnerships, and community resources ‍

### Looking Ahead
 This is just the beginning. Over the coming weeks and months, we&#x27;ll share more about our product roadmap, integration plans, and the exciting features we&#x27;re bringing to market.
 ‍
 If you&#x27;re an existing customer with questions, our team is here for you — reach out to your account manager or contact us directly. If you&#x27;re exploring the marketplace model for your business, there&#x27;s never been a better time to get started.
 ‍
 Thank you for being part of this journey. We&#x27;re building something special, and we&#x27;re just getting started.
 ‍
 James Throsby Founder & CEO, The Traide
