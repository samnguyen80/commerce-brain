 We are absolutely thrilled to bring the massive success of our London workshops back to our home city! Welcome to Deep Dive MCR, our first-ever dedicated, completely free growth workshop in Manchester. We’re on a mission to celebrate the North’s incredibly vibrant digital scene, and we’re bringing our signature hands-on, highly collaborative formula right to your doorstep.
 Deep Dive MCR is the ultimate interactive space for passionate Northern brand founders, operators, and C-suite leaders. We’ve ditched the static slideshows to build an intimate environment centred around real, collective problem-solving. Through structured roundtable sessions, you’ll get to sit down with fellow brand builders to work through current bottlenecks, share hard-won victories, and swap trusted advice.
 We’ll dive deep into the specific growth levers that move the needle: from maximising customer lifetime value and lowering acquisition costs to fine-tuning a high-performing tech stack. You&#x27;ll learn the tactical plays being used by today&#x27;s most dynamic innovators to supercharge revenue and ROI.
