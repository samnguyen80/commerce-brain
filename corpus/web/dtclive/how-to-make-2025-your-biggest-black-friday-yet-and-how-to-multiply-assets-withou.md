## Make 2025 your biggest Black Friday yet with insights from Sprii
 Black Friday has shifted. Shoppers aren’t just hunting for discounts anymore, they want interaction, exclusivity, and a bit of entertainment too. 
 That’s why Live Shopping has become such a powerful way to stand out.
 We caught up with our friends at Sprii , who see thousands of Black Friday campaigns run through their platform every November. We dug into their brand-new 2025 Black Friday Playbook . Inside, they’ve broken down the five proven plays you can copy to win Black Friday (and Black Month).
 Their Black Month numbers from the previous years give a glimpse of just how big this has become:
 💶 €53M revenue generated 
 📦 2.6M products sold 
 📺 20K+ campaigns delivered 
 What stood out most in our conversation was how early the most successful brands start their Black Friday Live Shopping campaigns. 
 So, what’s in the 2025 Playbook?
 
- How to start building hype weeks before the big day
- The Live Shopping formats that cut through Black Month noise

## How to multiply assets without burning more hours
 What actually moves the needle is having a system that multiplies what you already have and makes iteration easier.
 Here’s a framework we use:

### Tools
 
- CapCut and Descript for fast edits, captions, remixes
- Canva or Figma for statics, resizing, batch edits
- Airtable or Notion for pipeline and creative tracker
- Motion or Triple Whale for creative-level reporting
- Google Drive or Dropbox with clear naming conventions 

### Multiply Volume
 
- One UGC clip can turn into 5–10 assets with different hooks, CTAs and overlays
- Keep a running hook and CTA bank refreshed weekly
- Organise your library by clip ID, hook/CTA variations and performance history
- Use pre-built CapCut or Canva templates for quick swaps
- Pull statics from video by freezing frames and adding headlines or CTAs 

### Reporting and Iteration
 
- Automated dashboards for weekly reporting
- Tag performance: Winner, Iterate, Kill
- Variation system: 3–5 new versions for each winner with a new hook, CTA or style
- Weekly review to analyse results and brief the next round This is how you scale volume without burning more hours. If you don’t have a system like this in place, you’ll always feel behind on creative.
