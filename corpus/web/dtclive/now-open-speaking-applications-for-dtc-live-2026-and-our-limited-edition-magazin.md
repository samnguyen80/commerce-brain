# Now open: Speaking applications for DTC Live 2026 and our limited edition magazine

 

 
 

 
 

### Want to speak at a DTC Live event in 2026?
 If you’re a DTC founder, marketer or operator with practical insight to share, we’re opening up speaking opportunities across the DTC Live 2026 calendar.
 We host a mix of intimate learning sessions, flagship conferences and podcast conversations , with formats to suit different styles and experience levels.

### Deep Dives | London 
 Focused, small-group sessions built for real discussion and clear takeaways. 
 12 Feb | 4 Jun | 16 Jul | 12 Nov 
 Formats include fireside chats and expert panels, ideal for going deep on a specific challenge or strategy.

### Connect | Flagship Conferences 
 Our largest events, bringing the DTC community together for high-energy learning and connection. 
 London (12 Mar | 15 Oct) | Manchester (7 May) | Los Angeles (10 Sep) | New York (15 Sep) 
 Formats include fireside chats and panels with founders and industry leaders.

### DTC Live On Air | Podcast 
 In-person podcast recordings hosted in London and Manchester . 
 🗓️ Scheduled flexibly If you’re interested, just get in touch and we’ll find a time that works to record together.

### Why speak at DTC Live?
 
- Connect with a powerful network of founders, marketers and operators
- Share your expertise with an audience that cares
- Build your personal brand and increase industry visibility
- Multiple formats so you can choose what suits your style Interested? 
 Drop a note to amelia@dtclive.com and we’ll be in touch with next steps.

## The must attend events of 2026
 The new year is fast approaching, and we’re kicking things off with some seriously exciting DTC Live experiences. 
 If you’re looking to level up your 2026 plans, meet the right people, and get inspired for what’s ahead, these are the moments you don’t want to miss.

### Deep Dive LDN Annual Planning Workshop
 12th February | Premium central London location 
 A one-day, high-energy workshop for fast-moving DTC brands, where you’ll connect with ambitious leaders and potential partners, hear what’s actually working right now, learn directly from operators scaling in today’s market, enjoy a high-end central London setting with a proper lunch, and chat with our team about how we can support your next stage of growth.
 Register your interest 

### DTC Live Connect LDN
 12th March | London 
 DTC Live Connect LDN is a full-day, high-impact gathering of founders, marketers and operators, bringing together sharp talks, real insights, industry-leading partners, meaningful conversations, and a buzzing central London atmosphere where you can learn what’s working right now, meet the people shaping the future of ecommerce, and leave with ideas, connections and momentum for the year ahead.
