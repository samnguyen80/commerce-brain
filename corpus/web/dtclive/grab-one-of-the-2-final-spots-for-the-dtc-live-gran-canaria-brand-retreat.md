## BFCM might feelfar away… But it’s closer than you think
 Talking about Black Friday and Cyber Monday in August might feel early. But with the biggest shopping weekend of the year creeping up fast, now is the time to make sure your operations are ready for the rush.
 BFCM can bring huge spikes in traffic but also exposes cracks in the customer experience if fulfilment is not seamless. Nearly 70% of online shoppers abandon their carts and this can be even higher during peak trading.
 We spoke to our fulfilment friends at Sprint Logistics for their top tips on keeping customers converting.

### 5 Fulfilment fixes to keep BFCM customers converting
 1. Give Real-Time Delivery Estimates Shoppers want clarity. Sprint Logistics integrates Warehouse Management Systems and carrier networks to provide live tracking and accurate delivery times. Pro tip: put BFCM cut-off dates front and centre on product pages and at checkout.
 2. Be Upfront About Shipping Costs Nothing kills a checkout faster than surprise fees. Offering free shipping thresholds or showing costs early in the journey can make a big difference. Sprint’s flexible carrier network helps keep options competitive without eating into margins.
 3. Keep Inventory Data Accurate Selling something that’s actually out of stock? That’s a guaranteed trust-buster. Sync your sales channels, update stock in real time, and let customers know exactly what’s available, Sprint’s eCommerce fulfilment service is built for this.
 4. Promise (and Deliver) Speed Same-day dispatch, next-day options, and localised fulfilment can be the edge you need. With multiple fulfilment centres across the UK and Europe, Sprint helps brands offer faster shipping without the stress.
 5. Make Returns Hassle-Free BFCM shoppers want reassurance. A simple returns process and clear policies can build trust and increase conversions. Sprint’s reverse logistics service keeps it smooth from start to finish.

### Post-BFCM? Don’t just move on 
 Sprint suggests digging into your fulfilment data after the rush, what products caused the most cart drops? 
 Where did delays happen? 
 This insight is essential when planning for the next peak.
 Usually people associate reducing cart abandonment just with better marketing but it’s about better logistics. 
 Get your fulfilment right, and you can turn more clicks into completed checkouts this BFCM.

## Why Meta won’t scale your &#x27;New&#x27; ads (And what to do instead)
 Meta’s Andromeda update may be killing your ad performance, and if your “new” ad looks anything like the old one, Meta won’t scale it.
 Here’s why that happens, and how to fix it:

### Same vibe = same signal 
 Meta doesn’t look at fonts or colours, it looks at scroll patterns, hook structures, and engagement loops. If your new ad feels like the old one, Meta routes budget back to your original winner. Small tweaks won’t cut it, you’re not testing new creative, you’re echoing the old signal. 

### Micro-iterations are getting punished 
 This is the mistake: new headline, new cut, same concept. To Meta, that’s not a new idea, it’s noise. It’s not glitching, it’s just smarter now. Andromeda prioritises content variation that breaks prediction models, not surface-level edits. 

### Scale comes from message variation, not versioning 
 You only need one message to win, but you need to package that message in radically different formats.

### What works right now:
 
- Raw UGC with human urgency
- Skits or rants to reset scroll inertia
- Testimonial mashups that stack proof
- Us vs Them comparisons to provoke emotion
- Bold stat carousels that create logic-based desire
- Founders explaining value directly to camera
- Educational explainers that unlock curiosity Each one gives Meta a different behavioural pattern to optimise around.

### If you’re still tweaking winners, you’re falling behind
 Surface edits aren’t strategy. 
 Brands relying on old systems are getting capped. Meta is rewarding signal variance, your creative process has to evolve.

### Strategic implementation plan
 
- Identify your top-performing message
- Scrap the micro-edits
- Rebuild 6–8 distinct formats around the same message
- Track which unlocks spend
- Kill anything that stalls early New results need new signals. Same concept, same outcome.  Not sure where you stand heading into peak season?
