# How to Get Orders from Checkout to Dispatch with Zero Manual Touches

 

 
 

 
 There is a great sense of momentum when a new order pops up on your Shopify dashboard. The customer converted, and the payment cleared.
 But what happens next?
 In many growing eCommerce businesses, the order sits in a queue, waiting for a team member to review it, check inventory levels, print a shipping label, and route the information to the warehouse.
 This is the processing gap : the time between when a customer pays and when the warehouse actually starts picking the item. When order volumes are low, managing this gap manually is standard practice. But as order volume scales, this manual review process can become a bottleneck that impacts delivery times and increases administrative workload.
 The most effective way to scale efficiently is to bridge that gap through zero-touch fulfilment .

## Understanding the Processing Gap 
 If your team is physically clicking buttons to move an order from your storefront to your shipping department, it introduces hidden inefficiencies into your daily operations.
 
- Slower shipping speeds: Customers have come to expect rapid delivery. If an order sits in a digital queue for several hours waiting for administrative approval before it reaches the warehouse floor, that adds unnecessary time to the delivery window.
- Higher risk of human error: When a staff member is manually processing hundreds of orders a day, the likelihood of assigning the wrong shipping carrier, routing the order to the incorrect 3PL, or missing a specific customer note naturally increases.
- Tying growth to headcount: If order processing requires manual human effort, your capacity is directly tied to the size of your team. This makes it difficult to double your sales volume without also doubling your administrative staff. 

## What is Zero-Touch Fulfilment? 
 Zero-touch fulfilment means that from the moment a customer clicks "Buy" to the moment the shipping label is generated at the warehouse, no human being has to manually touch, edit, or route the order data.
 The data flows instantly across your systems based on pre-set logic.
 By implementing order fulfilment automation, you remove the friction of manual review. Your warehouse gets the information immediately, your customer gets their package faster, and your back-office team is freed from repetitive data entry.

## Building an Automated Order Processing Workflow 
 To achieve a zero-touch setup, you need to replace manual human decisions with digital rules. Upgrading to a Retail-first ERP System like Brightpearl with a built-in automation engine allows you to do exactly this.
 Instead of relying on staff to manually connect the dots, Brightpearl manages the workflow in the background. Here is how it streamlines your delivery:
 
- Instant order routing: The moment an order clears checkout, Brightpearl reads the customer&#x27;s location and inventory availability. It automatically routes the order to the warehouse or 3PL closest to the customer, which can reduce both shipping times and freight costs.
- Automated allocation: The system automatically allocates the exact stock required for the order, updating your available inventory across all your other sales channels in real-time to prevent overselling.
- Smart shipping rules: Instead of a human assessing the best shipping method, Brightpearl&#x27;s tools evaluate the package&#x27;s weight, dimensions, and destination. It then instantly tags the order with the correct, most cost-effective shipping carrier and service level.
- Exception handling: The system also manages exceptions. If an item is out of stock, the automation engine can be set to automatically split the order, sending the available items to dispatch immediately while creating a backorder for the rest, without requiring human intervention. 

## Reallocating Your Staff for Growth 
 When you automate the processing gap, the impact on your daily operations is immediate.
 Your customer service and operations teams no longer have to spend their time copying and pasting tracking numbers, hunting down inventory, or untangling complex shipping rules.
 By utilising Brightpearl , you decouple your processing capacity from your administrative workload. This allows you to reallocate your team&#x27;s time toward high-value tasks that software cannot do: improving the customer experience, optimising your supply chain, and focusing on brand growth.
