 Connect NYC

 September 15, 2026

 Crossing the pond to the city that never sleeps, Connect NYC is our newest, fastest-growing event, and it is already creating an incredible buzz across the US East Coast. This is the ultimate playground for brand builders looking to scale internationally, tap into the American market, and connect with global heavyweights.
 We bring the signature high-energy, premium yet casual DTC Live formula to New York City, uniting influential brands with world-class tech platforms and visionary investors. Our stage has already featured inspirational stories from pioneering brands like Tommy Hilfiger, AKT, Stratia Skincare, Goop and Bokksu, alongside cutting-edge tech platforms that are actively reshaping the digital retail landscape.
 Whether you want to learn the playbook of brands that have conquered the US market, meet key partners who can streamline your tech stack, or get your brand noticed by influential investors, Connect NYC delivers. We keep the schedule light on lectures and heavy on active networking, giving you ample space to chat directly with fellow founders during our interactive breaks. 
 You’ll walk away with actionable insights you can implement tomorrow, a pocketful of exclusive partner deals, and an invaluable network of transatlantic eCom friends. 
 Join us in NYC for a day of lightning-fast insights, premium connections, and celebratory drinks.
