 Trends, Tactics & Transformation Retreat

 October 27, 2026

 Welcome to our Trends, Tactics & Transformation Retreat, the ultimate sun-soaked getaway in Gran Canaria designed to help you step away from the everyday and focus on what your brand truly needs to thrive. Over three incredible days, we gather a curated cohort of passionate brand founders, operators, and C-suite leaders for our third annual retreat. All your accommodation and costs are fully covered, and you only have to take care of your flights.
 We’ve swapped dry, crowded seminars for an intimate planning experience under the Spanish sun. One entire day is dedicated to a high-impact, hands-on strategy workshop, where you’ll dive into the specific growth levers shaping the future of retail. The rest of your time is built around genuine relationship-building. Whether you’re brainstorming during interactive roundtables, recharging during wellness sessions, or connecting over local food, it’s all about real conversations with fellow brand builders who understand the journey.
 This exclusive, premium getaway is open only to brands within our DTC Live community. It’s the perfect, distraction-free environment to gain strategic clarity, showcase your solutions, and walk away with a lasting network. Just genuine peer support, collaborative learning, and an unforgettable experience.

## Your Brand Retreat questions answered 

 We know that taking four days out of your business is a major commitment. If you can’t find the answer you’re looking for, or you want to chat through whether the next retreat is the right fit for your brand, reach out to our team via email or socials.

 
 

### Who will I be networking with during the retreat? 
 You’ll be joining a curated group of just 20 ambitious brand leaders, founders, tech innovators, and investors. It’s a rare opportunity to build deep, meaningful connections with peers who are facing the same scaling hurdles as you, away from the distractions of the office.

 

 

### What is the cost to attend? 
 We cover the entire cost of your stay, including luxury accommodation, all meals, workshops, and curated social events. The only investment required from your side is the cost of your flights to the destination.

 

 

### What kind of strategic workshops are included? 
 Our sessions are led by expert partners and focus on the technical and strategic side of growth. You&#x27;ll learn more about the eCom tech that actually supports scale and master marketing best practices needed to nurture and retain a loyal customer base.

 

 

### Is there time for relaxation or is it all work? 
 Balance is central to the experience. We believe the best thinking happens when you’re recharged, so we include rejuvenating wellness activities and dedicated downtime to ensure you leave feeling inspired rather than exhausted.
