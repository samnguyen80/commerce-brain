# Back to School Product Knowledge Basics - Why Associate Training Matters
 
 Back-to-school season is here—and for outdoor, footwear, and lifestyle retailers, that means a surge in store traffic and shopper urgency.

 Parents and students are on the hunt for specific gear, from comfortable shoes for gym class to durable backpacks.

 But here’s what often happens: A shopper walks in, overwhelmed by all the options, and asks an associate for help.

 The response?

 An uncertain suggestion.

 And just like that, your brand misses its moment to stand out.

 Many associates represent your brand to customers in-store—otfen without properly understanding your products.

 And during busy shopping seasons, like back-to-school, that gap matters.

## Product Knowledge Is a Competitive Advantage

 Back-to-school shoppers don&#x27;t just browse – they compare, ask questions, and look for associate guidance.

 They’re curious about what works best, what lasts longest, and what’s worth the price tag.

 That’s where product knowledge turns into sales performance.

 Here’s why thorough training on your brand matters:

 
 Customers trust informed associates. 40% of associates say a confident recommendation lands with customers.
 
 When your product’s value is clearly explained, customers are far more likely to buy.

 

- Associates represent your brand. 
 
 Think of sales associates as your frontline ambassadors.

 Without proper training, they can unintentionally misrepresent your brand or overlook it entirely.

 

- Knowledge builds confidence. 
 
 Associates who deeply understand a product can effortlessly convey its benefits, increasing the likelihood of a sale.

 Fact: 63% of shoppers walk into stores undecided, and their purchases are often influenced by the associates say in those moments.

## The Risk of Being Absent from the Recommendation

 Educating associates about your products for Back-to-School season is like equipping a runner with the perfect pair of shoes for a marathon—essential for success.

 What brands risk without associate training:

 

- Defaulting to competitor recommendations. 
 
 When associates don’t know your product, they’ll recommend the one they’re most familiar with—even if yours is the better fit.

 

- Damage to reputation. 
 
 Poorly informed associates may unintentionally pass on incorrect information, leaving customers dissatisfied with their purchase and your brand.

 This doesn’t have to be your reality.

 By investing in the right tools, you give the associates selling your brand to not only know your product but the ability to feel confident talking about them - and your brand becomes the one they recommend.
