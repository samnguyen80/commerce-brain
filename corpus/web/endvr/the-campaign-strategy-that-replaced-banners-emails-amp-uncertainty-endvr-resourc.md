# The Campaign Strategy That Replaced Banners, Emails, & Uncertainty
 
 Before becoming Senior Client Success Manager at ENDVR, I spent 11 years as Marketing Manager for Mountain Hardwear Canada. A significant amount of my time (& budget) was spent on co-op marketing - developing and executing marketing plans specifically for wholesale partners.

 As Marketing Manager I was tasked with driving sell-through, building retailer relationships, and ultimately, delivering measurable results.

 Retailers have varying marketing resources, expertise, and budgets.

 Trust me, I get it.

 Just trying to provide timely and relevant co-op marketing materials, training, and guidance required tons of coordination.I&#x27;d throw everything at it: digital ads, web banners, social posts, branded emails – sometimes even sales incentives, although I still cringe at the thought – such a painfully manual task!

 But at the end of the day, I&#x27;d ask myself, &#x27;Did any of this actually drive foot traffic?&#x27; And even if it did, how could I prove those customers bought our brand?

 The truth is, attributing sales to my co-op marketing efforts across a diverse retail network felt impossible.

 Then, we began using ENDVR. I shifted my discretionary co-op marketing funds into sales incentives - whether nationwide, or targeted to specific retail partners.

 Suddenly, I had a powerful tool that simplified my workload and gave me the data I needed to impress my leadership team.

 Weekly meetings with leadership moved from “ Here is a mock up of the email we did with x-retailer ” to “ this past week we paid $x in cash rewards to sales associates, which attributed to $x in total sales, x number of units sold, for an ROI of 35 : 1, at all of our key accounts across the country. ” Access to this type of data helped my team and I understand what was working and where to focus our future efforts. I no longer felt the glazed over looks of our sales team when presenting past season efforts at Sales Meetings because I was able to present concrete data: “this is exactly how our marketing spend supported sell-through.” Needless to say it didn’t take long for me to realize that ENDVR gave me a direct way to influence and measure what happens on the store floor.

 The data I had at my fingertips helped me to become a more results-driven marketer.

 So, if you&#x27;re ready to move beyond the "maybe" and start seeing definitive proof of your impact, it might be time to explore how a platform like ENDVR can transform your approach, just like it did mine.
