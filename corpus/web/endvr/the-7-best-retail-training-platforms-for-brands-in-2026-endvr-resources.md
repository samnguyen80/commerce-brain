# The 7 Best Retail Training Platforms for Brands in 2026
 
 "Retail training platform" describes two different products, and buying the wrong one wastes a year.

 Employer-side tools serve retailers educating their own workforce: onboarding, store procedures, compliance, and product content from the retailer&#x27;s perspective. Axonify, Rallyware, and Bigtincan mostly live here.

 Brand-side tools serve brands reaching the associates who sell their products at retailers the brand doesn&#x27;t own. The buyer is different, the rollout is different, and so is the measure of success. ENDVR, Myagi, and SPARC live here.

 We build ENDVR, so read our entry knowing that, and the rest knowing we&#x27;ve sourced claims from each vendor&#x27;s own materials. Most tools on this list aren&#x27;t actually competing for the same buyer, which makes an accurate map more useful to you than a ranking.

## Brand-side tools

### 1. ENDVR

 ENDVR delivers brand education to retail associates&#x27; phones as 2-4 minute modules with rewards attached, and completion averages 89% across active campaigns. The structural difference from every other tool on this list: education sits beside sales incentives with AI-verified receipts, so brands see the line from module completion to confirmed sell-through, by store and region.

 Education on ENDVR is one of five things brands run from the same portal, alongside Sales Incentives , market research through Frontline Insights, display verification, and consumer cashback. Associates already on the network (140,000+ across 15,000+ stores) find your content without any retailer-side rollout.

 Best for: brands in sporting goods, outdoor, footwear, apparel, and electronics that want education tied to measurable sell-through.

 Pricing: per engaged store, with your first stores free. Details .

 Watch out for: ENDVR is retail-facing. Internal head-office learning and HR compliance stay on your LMS.

### 2. Myagi

 Myagi, acquired by Rallyware in 2023 and now sold as "Myagi by Rallyware," pioneered brand-to-retailer content sharing: brands publish product education, retail partners subscribe their teams to it. Its network model in sports and outdoor overlaps ENDVR&#x27;s category territory most directly of anything on this list.

 The main difference is what happens after the lesson. Myagi measures learning engagement; connecting it to sales depends on the retailer&#x27;s own data. Our detailed comparison is here .

 Best for: brands with strong content libraries wanting broad distribution to partner retailers.

 Watch out for: no per-sale verification, so proving sell-through impact requires outside data.

### 3. SPARC Retail

 SPARC connects brands and associates in sports and outdoor with education modules and engagement campaigns. It&#x27;s a lightweight, affordable way to get content in front of associates, with self-reported sales claims rather than verified receipts. Full comparison .

 Best for: smaller sports and outdoor brands starting with associate engagement.

 Watch out for: self-reported data limits ROI reporting.

## Employer-side tools

### 4. Axonify

 Axonify is the reference product for enterprise frontline learning: adaptive microlearning, communications, and task management for workforces in grocery, big-box retail, and logistics. If you&#x27;re a retailer educating thousands of your own employees, it belongs on your shortlist.

 For brands, the relevant question is different: your product story inside a retailer&#x27;s Axonify competes with every operational priority the employer loads into it, if the retailer surfaces brand content at all. Brands typically pair whatever retailer systems exist with a direct channel to associates. Our comparison from the brand&#x27;s side is here .

 Best for: enterprises running learning programs for their own frontline workforce.

 Watch out for: it&#x27;s the employer&#x27;s system, on the employer&#x27;s priorities.

### 5. Rallyware

 Rallyware combines learning, incentives, and task management for large workforces, with deep roots in direct selling and enterprise retail. Its core buyers employ the learners and rollouts are enterprise projects, though its 2023 Myagi acquisition (covered above) added a brand-side training product. Our comparison .

 Best for: direct-selling companies and enterprise retailers.

 Watch out for: enterprise sales cycles; not designed for brands reaching third-party associates.

### 6. Bigtincan

 Bigtincan (which absorbed Brainshark) arms customer-facing teams with content and readiness tools. Retailers like Nike use it for their own retail teams, according to Bigtincan&#x27;s own materials. It&#x27;s a content and enablement suite for teams you employ, with strong document and coaching features.

 Best for: retailers and brands enabling their own stores and internal teams.

 Watch out for: wholesale-channel associates at independent dealers sit outside its model.

### 7. Mindtickle

 Mindtickle is sales readiness for B2B revenue teams: call scoring, role-play, certification paths, CRM integration. Some consumer brands use it for internal sales organizations. It&#x27;s excellent at that job and not built for retail floor associates at all; it earns its place here because it ranks for the category searches and buyers keep evaluating it for the wrong job.

 Best for: B2B sales teams and internal revenue enablement.

 Watch out for: category confusion. If your learners work at your retail partners, look brand-side.

## How to choose

 One question sorts the whole list: who employs the people you&#x27;re educating? 

 Your own employees (store teams, inside sales): Axonify or Rallyware at enterprise scale, Bigtincan for content-heavy enablement, Mindtickle for B2B revenue teams.

 Associates at retailers you sell through: you need a brand-side tool, and the follow-up question is what proof you need. If engagement metrics satisfy your leadership, Myagi and SPARC are credible options. If you need education connected to verified sell-through by store, that combination is the specific thing ENDVR was built to do.

 A useful test before any demo: ask the vendor to show you, in their own reporting, how a brand would know that education on SKU X changed sales of SKU X. The answers separate the categories fast.
