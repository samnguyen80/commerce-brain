# How ENDVR’s New Sales Rep App Helps You Show Up Smarter - Every Store, Every Visit
 
 Sales reps manage dozens of stores and campaigns; ENDVR’s new rep app is built to make that job easier.

 This rep-first experience is tailored to the realities of managing store relationships, supporting campaigns, and driving performance across territories of all sizes. 80% of purchases still happen in-store, reps play a large role in bridging the gap between brand strategy and in-store execution. ENDVR’s new app gives them everything they need to make that connection stronger, faster, and more effective.

## How It Works

 The ENDVR Sales Rep App delivers on its promise by streamlining workflows for both brand managers and the reps on the ground.

 Here’s how it benefits each:

### For Brand Admins

 This app gives brands a smarter, more scalable way to support reps and improve in-store performance.Getting your reps on the app is simple: reps can request access via the app, and brand admins approve those requests and set each rep’s visibility (all stores, specific stores, or groups) in the brand portal .

 Once your reps are on the platform, you gain instant visibility into what’s happening at the store level.

 No more waiting for monthly reports.

 You’ll see which stores are performing well, where engagement is slipping, and where your support could make a difference.

 Reps can launch campaigns and training directly to their assigned stores, ensuring every location stays aligned and supported.

 Just as important, the app helps strengthen your retailer partnerships.

 Reps can use it to stay connected with store teams, share product updates, check on merchandising, and offer timely support.

 It reinforces your brand’s commitment to supporting the store team.

 That level of connection matters; 27% of sales associates say that open communication with brands and better access to product education directly helps them close more sales.

### For Reps

 ENDVR gives reps a better way to manage their stores, track performance, and stay focused on what matters.

 
 Unified Dashboard & Brand Visibility: 
 
 See everything in one place—from how many stores are linked to your account to which campaigns are active or completed.

 The dashboard makes it easy to manage all the brands you represent and access brand-specific data, all with a single login.

 No more switching between accounts or tools.

 

- Real-Time Campaign Insights: 
 
 Get instant feedback on how your stores are engaging with incentives, training, and other campaigns.

 If something isn’t gaining traction, you’ll know early enough to adjust.

 If it’s working, you can pinpoint what’s driving results and double down where it matters.

 

- Store-Level Performance & Reports: 
 
 Every store has its own profile with recent activity, sales, and participation stats.

 Visual reports make it easy to spot trends, track progress, and identify stores that might need extra support.

 You can tailor your visits based on real insights—like following up where training is lagging or celebrating stores that are outperforming.

 

- Full Territory Oversight: 
 
 Whether you&#x27;re managing one brand or several, the app gives you complete visibility across your stores and campaigns.

 Everything you need to stay organized, informed, and effective is right at your fingertips.

## Closing & Next Steps

 If you want to improve in-store execution, getting your reps on the ENDVR Sales Rep App is the place to start.

 Invite your team, assign their stores, and they’re ready to go—with real-time data, campaign tools, and store insights in their pocket.

 Built for the floor, this app helps reps work smarter and show up stronger.

 And for you, it means better visibility, more consistent execution, and a stronger connection to what’s happening in every store.
