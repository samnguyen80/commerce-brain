# The Best B2B Wholesale Platforms for Brands in 2026
 
 If you&#x27;re building out your wholesale channel, you&#x27;ll run into the same short list of B2B platforms: NuORDER, JOOR, Elastic Suite, Brandboom, and a handful of others. They all do a version of the same job: getting your product into stores. Digital catalogs, line sheets, virtual showrooms, order capture.

 What none of them do is the second half of the job: getting your product out of stores. That&#x27;s sell-through , and it&#x27;s the number your retail partners actually grade you on when the next pre-book conversation comes around.

 This guide covers both halves. First, an honest map of the sell-in platforms and which one fits which kind of brand. Then the sell-through layer that turns a good ordering stack into a complete wholesale system.

 Full disclosure: we build ENDVR, the sell-through tool at the end of this list, and we partner with several of the companies on it: Brandscope , RepSpark , and Locally all connect with ENDVR today. Every company on this list does a different job than ENDVR does, so there&#x27;s no rigged scoring here. Every platform below earns its place in the right stack.

## B2B ordering platforms and digital showrooms

### NuORDER by Lightspeed

 NuORDER is one of the most widely adopted B2B ordering platforms in retail. Brands build digital catalogs and virtual showrooms, retailers browse and place orders, and buyers at major department stores use it as their standard ordering interface. Since joining Lightspeed, it also sits closer to the POS data retailers run on.

 Best for: established brands managing hundreds of retail accounts who want one system for catalogs, order capture, and buyer collaboration.

 Watch out for: it manages the order only. Once the product ships, visibility ends at the loading dock.

### JOOR

 JOOR is the dominant player in fashion and luxury wholesale. Digital showrooms, virtual market appointments, integrated payments through JOOR Pay, and a network that includes most major fashion retailers. If your buyers work in apparel, they likely already have JOOR logins.

 Best for: fashion, luxury, and contemporary brands whose buyers expect to order through it.

 Watch out for: strongest in fashion; brands in hardgoods or sporting goods will find fewer of their buyers on the network.

### Elastic Suite

 Elastic Suite is the B2B ordering platform closest to ENDVR&#x27;s home territory: outdoor, snow, sporting goods, and footwear. Digital catalogs, dealer whiteboarding, and pre-book workflows built around seasonal buying cycles. Many of the same specialty brands that run sales incentives on ENDVR take pre-books through Elastic.

 Best for: outdoor and sporting goods brands running seasonal pre-book programs with specialty dealers.

 Watch out for: like every ordering platform, it tells you what dealers bought. Whether any of it sold is a separate question.

### Brandboom

 Brandboom is the lightweight option: fast line sheets, shareable presentations, and order collection without enterprise onboarding. The free tier makes it the default starting point for emerging brands.

 Best for: smaller brands and lean teams who need professional line sheets and order capture without a big rollout.

 Watch out for: fewer enterprise features as your account list grows into the hundreds.

### Brandscope

 Brandscope is the leading B2B ordering platform in Australia and New Zealand, built around how ANZ wholesale actually works: sales agents building assortments and capturing orders for independent retailers. Brandscope and ENDVR work together . As their own line puts it: Brandscope gets you in the door, ENDVR moves you off the shelf.

 Best for: brands selling into the ANZ market through agents and independent retail.

### RepSpark

 RepSpark provides B2B e-commerce for brands with strong roots in golf and lifestyle apparel: digital catalogs, rep-driven ordering, and marketplace discovery for retailers. RepSpark also connects with ENDVR , pairing its ordering workflow with sell-through data from the sales floor.

 Best for: golf, apparel, and lifestyle brands whose reps drive the order book.

## Wholesale marketplaces

### Faire

 Faire is a different animal: not a tool your brand licenses, but a marketplace where hundreds of thousands of independent retailers discover and order products, with net-60 terms and free returns lowering the risk of trying a new brand. For makers and emerging brands, it can be the fastest path to wholesale distribution.

 Best for: emerging brands and makers building an independent-retail footprint from scratch.

 Watch out for: commission on orders is meaningful, the retailer relationship belongs partly to the marketplace, and it skews toward gift, home, and boutique categories more than technical or specialty retail.

## Store locator and local inventory software

### Locally

 Locally solves a different problem in the same channel: showing online shoppers which nearby stores carry your product, in stock, right now. Brands embed Locally&#x27;s dealer locators on their own sites and turn "where can I buy this?" traffic into in-store visits. Locally and ENDVR partner to highlight stores with knowledgeable, engaged staff directly on the locator.

 Best for: brands with real dealer networks who want their own web traffic driving in-store sales instead of leaking to marketplaces.

## How to build the stack

 Match the tools to the jobs, in the order the product moves:

 Taking orders. Pick the ordering platform your buyers already live on: JOOR for fashion, Elastic Suite for outdoor and sporting goods, NuORDER for broad retail coverage, Brandboom to start lean, Brandscope for ANZ, RepSpark for rep-driven categories.

 Finding new doors. Faire if you&#x27;re emerging and targeting independents; your ordering platform&#x27;s marketplace features once you&#x27;re established.

 Directing consumer demand. Locally, so the shoppers researching your product online end up at the dealers who stock it.

 Driving sell-through. ENDVR, so the associates in those doors know your product, recommend it first, and every verified sale feeds your dashboard. This is the layer that protects everything the rest of the stack worked to build, because dealers re-order what sells, and how fast product sells through decides your next season more than any showroom.

## Frequently asked questions

 What&#x27;s the difference between NuORDER and JOOR? 
Both are enterprise B2B ordering platforms with digital showrooms and order management. The practical difference is network: JOOR is strongest in fashion and luxury, where most major buyers already use it, while NuORDER has broader coverage across general retail and, through Lightspeed, closer ties to retailer POS systems. Most brands choose whichever their key buyers already order through.

 Do I need a B2B ordering platform and a sell-through tool? 
They do different halves of one job. The ordering platform digitizes sell-in: catalogs, orders, invoicing. A sell-through tool works the other side: making sure the product that shipped actually sells, and giving you the data to prove it. Brands that run both know within weeks, instead of seasons, whether a launch is landing.

 Is Faire a replacement for a B2B ordering platform? 
For emerging brands, it can be the starting point. As your account list grows, most brands add a dedicated ordering platform for their established dealers and keep Faire for new-door discovery, because the commission economics favor moving repeat business onto your own system.

 How do brands measure sell-through in doors without EDI? 
Historically: rep visits, surveys, and gut feel. The newer approach is receipt-level verification through the associates themselves: every incentive claim on ENDVR is an AI-verified receipt, which doubles as store-level sell-through data.
