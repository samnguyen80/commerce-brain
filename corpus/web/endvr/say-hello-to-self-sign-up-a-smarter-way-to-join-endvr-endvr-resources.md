# Say Hello to Self Sign Up - A Smarter Way to Join ENDVR
 
 We are proud to announce the launch of our new Self Sign Up options, that allows brands to join the platform instantly - no calls, no commitments.

 This update makes it easier than ever for brands to drive in-store sales through sales incentives, digital education, retail ops tools, and (soon) customer cashback.

 Now, any brand can create an account and go live with pay-as-you-go campaigns in minutes - furthering ENDVR’s mission to connect brands, retailers, sales associates, and customers in driving in-store sell-through.

### A Major Step Toward Frictionless Retail Enablement

 With self-signup, ENDVR opens the door to brands of all sizes- from emerging DTC brands entering wholesale to established leaders looking to scale retail initiatives.

 By removing barriers to entry, brands can now activate campaigns faster and with more flexibility, giving their teams greater control over in-store marketing. “With self-signup, we’re making it radically easier for brands to activate the retail network that already exists around their products,” said Stephen Gendron , CEO and Co-Founder of ENDVR . “This is about speed, access, and giving every brand the tools they need to drive performance inside wholesale retail stores. ” 

### Benefits for New Customers

 Brands joining ENDVR through self-signup can now access the platform with ease - and grow their retail strategy step by step. Here’s how it works - three simple steps designed to grow with you: Step 1: Join the Network (for Free) 

 Start by creating your brand profile and becoming visible across your retail network.

 At no cost, you can: - Set up a brand profile and upload a brand video to be shared with store staff - Share brand updates via your newsfeed - Invite your reps and retail partners to join - Establish your presence on the ENDVR platform with zero upfront commitment Step 2: Launch & Learn (Pay-As-You-Go) 

 When you&#x27;re ready to take action, you can launch your first paid campaign.

 This flexible option allows you to: - Test messaging and incentives to see what resonates with store staff - Start small and scale based on performance - Learn and adapt as you gather data and feedback from the store floor - Join the many top brand partners who began with just one campaign Step 3: Scale for Impact (Annual Plan) 

 Once you’ve seen the value, the annual plan helps you take things further by unlocking: - Access to preferred pricing - Enterprise-level tools, features, and custom insights - Deeper ROI tracking and strategic campaign optimization support Whether you&#x27;re just starting to build your retail network or looking for a smarter, more scalable way to grow, ENDVR gives you the flexibility and control to move at your own pace.

### What It Means for Existing Brands

 As a valued brand already using ENDVR, this update doesn’t change how you operate on the platform - it strengthens the network around you.

 By making it easier for new brands to join, self-signup adds more activity, visibility, and momentum across the platform.

 With more brand content flowing into the network, retail staff will be more engaged and will login more frequently, engaging with more product education, and interacting with the platform more consistently.

 That means even greater exposure and impact for the campaigns you already run. What it means for you: 

 The more active the ENDVR network becomes, the more opportunities your brand has to stand out on the store floor.

 You’ll benefit from: - A more engaged audience of retail associates already interacting with brand content - Increased daily platform usage from your store network - A stronger, more dynamic ecosystem that makes every campaign more valuable What stays the same: - Your tools, campaigns, and workflow are unchanged - Our customer success team is still here to support your growth and help you get the most out of the platform Whether you&#x27;re new to ENDVR or already part of the network, there’s never been a better time to take action.

 Sign up today to start driving sales with pay-as-you-go campaigns, or share the link with fellow brands to help grow a more active, engaged retail community for everyone.
