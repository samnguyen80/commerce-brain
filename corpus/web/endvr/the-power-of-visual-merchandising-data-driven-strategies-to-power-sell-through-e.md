# The Power of Visual Merchandising: Data-Driven Strategies to Power Sell-Through
 
 Stores are more than places to shop. They set the stage for discovery and connection, shaping how customers experience a brand.

 Visual merchandising, when done right, transforms passive browsing into active engagement, directly influencing purchasing behavior.

 Well-maintained displays can increase sales by up to 540%. 70% of purchasing decisions occur at the point of purchase. Poorly executed displays don&#x27;t just fail to inspire. They leave revenue and customer loyalty on the table.

## The Numbers Are Clear

 The connection between strategic visual merchandising and revenue growth is undeniable:

 
 Visual elements like layout, lighting, and product placement influence 91.3% of consumer purchase intent.

- Well-executed displays can increase sales by 30%.

- Premium placement, such as eye-level positioning, accounts for 52% of all retail sales.

- Stores that refresh their displays every two weeks experience 23% higher foot traffic than those with static setups.
 

## Experiential Merchandising Powers Results

 For outdoor brands, creating immersive, experience-driven displays makes a measurable impact.

 Experiential merchandising involves creating displays that provide a sensory experience for the customer—interactive camping scenes, climbing walls, adventure-themed vignettes.

 Brands that leverage experiential merchandising report 18% longer dwell times and 12% higher conversion rates.

 The in-store experience must mirror the thrill of the outdoors. Displays become more than decoration. They become silent salespeople.

 A North Face store featuring tent setups and adventure-themed vignettes saw a 27% increase in sales for the displayed products.

 Visual storytelling isn&#x27;t optional. It&#x27;s a revenue driver.

## 3 Strategies to Elevate Outdoor Retail Displays

### 1. Optimize Product Placement with Precision

 Science confirms that 62–90% of product assessments hinge on color and placement. Outdoor brands should prioritize:

 

- Eye-level positioning for high-margin items (52% of sales occur at eye level)

- Thematic groupings that tell a story, like Hunter&#x27;s boot-and-bag pairings or Specialized&#x27;s cycling lifestyle displays

- Interactive shopping zones , such as in-store camping scenes, which increase product dwell time by 15% and boost accessory sales
 

### 2. Leverage Technology for Consistency and Agility

 Visual merchandising software helps digitally map displays, streamline real-time updates across global teams, and minimize miscommunication.

 But technology alone doesn&#x27;t maintain displays. The associates on the floor do.

### 3. Maintain Relentlessly

 Cluttered displays repel customers. Regular updates are non-negotiable.

 

- Retailers refreshing displays every two weeks see 19% higher repeat visits.

- Use inventory turnover data to identify slow-moving items and rotate stock strategically.
 

## Displays Support Associates. Associates Power Sell-Through.

 Here&#x27;s the truth: displays don&#x27;t sell products. People do.

 When a customer walks into a store and asks for a recommendation, your sell-through depends on what happens next. The associate standing in front of them shapes what gets considered, what gets tried, and ultimately what gets bought.

 The best displays in the world won&#x27;t move product if the associate doesn&#x27;t know the story behind them.

 Visual merchandising is the bridge between product and passion. But the associate is the one who walks the customer across it.
