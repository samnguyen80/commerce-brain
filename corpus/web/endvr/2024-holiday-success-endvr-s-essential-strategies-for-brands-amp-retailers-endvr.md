# 2024 Holiday Success: ENDVR’s Essential Strategies for Brands & Retailers
 
 As the holiday season approaches, the retail landscape is already beginning to buzz with excitement and opportunity.

 It&#x27;s a time of year that holds immense potential for brands and retailers as they navigate a complex, ever-evolving environment shaped by shifting consumer behaviour, economic uncertainty, and the growing demand for personalized and sustainable shopping experiences.
