 For over three decades, Dragon Alliance has been at the forefront of the eyewear industry, known for its innovation and premium quality. With a history rich in innovation and a dedication to pushing the boundaries of eyewear technology, Dragon Alliance is a brand for individuals who want to forge their own path and see the world on their own terms.
 

## Overcoming Training Challenges
 Despite their esteemed position, Dragon Alliance faced a significant challenge with the traditional training methods of retail staff at their wholesale retail spaces. Relying on face-to-face training, they struggled to measure its effectiveness.

 " We focused mainly on face-to-face training, but it&#x27;s really hard to quantify the ROI. It&#x27;s tough to understand if people are actually absorbing the information. "
 This gap in measuring engagement led the brand to explore new alternative solutions, but transitioning to online platforms had its own problems.

 " We dabbled with some online educational platforms, but they were really clunky and desktop-based. We literally saw virtually zero uptake. "
 

## Finding the Solution with ENDVR
 In pursuit of a better digital education solution, Dragon Alliance turned to the ENDVR retail sales enablement platform. With its tailored features, ENDVR emerged as the perfect choice for revolutionizing training initiatives.
