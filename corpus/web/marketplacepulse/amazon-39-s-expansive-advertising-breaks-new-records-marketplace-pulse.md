# Amazon's Expansive Advertising Breaks New Records

 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 Amazon&rsquo;s advertising business hit another milestone in Q2 2025, capturing 9.36% of the company&rsquo;s total revenue – the highest share ever recorded. At $15.69 billion in quarterly revenue, advertising continues to be Amazon&rsquo;s fastest-growing segment, and at its current trajectory, could breach the symbolic 10% threshold as soon as this year.

 The progression is both predictable and remarkable. Since Q1 2021, when advertising represented just 5.88% of revenue, Amazon has systematically transformed every corner of its platform into paid inventory. The vast majority of its advertising revenue comes from in-search advertising, where search results have become an increasingly ad-saturated experience with fewer and fewer organic results visible amidst the myriad of paid placements over time.

 

 Yet Amazon&rsquo;s advertising ambitions extend far beyond stuffing more ads into search results. As the company noted in its Q2 earnings call, the advertising network now reaches &ldquo;an average ad support audience of more than 300 million across our own properties&rdquo; in the U.S. alone, spanning Prime Video, Twitch, Fire TV, live sports, and third-party websites. Recent partnerships with Roku and Disney&rsquo;s real-time ad exchange demonstrate further how Amazon sees off-platform growth as vital to its continued advertising success.

 The numbers dwarf historical context: Amazon made more in advertising during Q2 2025 ($15.69 billion) than its entire revenue in Q2 2012 ($12.83 billion). The trailing twelve months of advertising income now exceeds Amazon&rsquo;s total 2012 revenue, illustrating the transformation from online bookstore to advertising behemoth.

 This growth comes with both cost and opportunity for sellers. A growing reliance on advertising for visibility pushes customer acquisition costs ever-higher, and for many sellers, total Amazon fees now exceed 50% of revenue . Yet the expanding reach presents opportunities to access significantly larger audiences from the same, mostly stable advertising platform, particularly as Amazon ventures further into connected TV and off-platform inventory.

 While key rivals Google and Meta still command larger absolute advertising revenues, Amazon&rsquo;s quarterly growth rates frequently outpace both. In Q2 2025, Amazon&rsquo;s 22% advertising growth maintained its position as the company&rsquo;s fastest-growing segment for the second consecutive quarter, and fourth time in the past six quarters. Only AWS comes close to matching this consistency.

 The broader context re-emphasizes Amazon&rsquo;s strategic evolution. Services income – advertising, third-party seller services, subscription services, and AWS – now constitute almost 60% of Amazon&rsquo;s income, up from 50% in 2021 . As Amazon steadily and purposefully shifts from retailer to infrastructure provider , advertising revenue has become essential to subsidizing the rest of the retail operation.

 Without the high-margin advertising revenue, Amazon&rsquo;s retail business would be increasingly unprofitable . This dependency makes the trajectory toward 10% of revenue not just likely, but necessary for Amazon&rsquo;s long-term retail strategy.

 Amazon&rsquo;s advertising business will exceed $60 billion in 2025 – a figure that will place it among the world&rsquo;s largest media companies by revenue. For sellers, this means navigating an increasingly pay-to-play environment. For Amazon, it represents the transformation into what it has long aspired to be: not just a retailer, but the infrastructure powering commerce itself.
