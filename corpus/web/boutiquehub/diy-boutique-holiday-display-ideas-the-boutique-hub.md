# DIY Boutique Holiday Display Ideas
 

 
 
 
 
 

 

 
 
 The Holiday season is an opportunity for your boutique to stand out from the crowd. This week’s post features creative projects to help you create an inspired space for your customers to shop. These DIY boutique Holiday display ideas are proof positive that you don’t have to spend a fortune to make your boutique look like a million bucks.

## Disco Snowman 

 Can you think if anything more fun and enticing than a disco ball snowman? What a fun and creative way to spice up your store for the holidays! This easy to follow tutorial walks you through the steps to create this trendy little guy. 

 

## Paper Bag Snowflakes 

 These easy and cheap paper bag snowflakes can fill up a space with festive and chic vibes in no time. You can add these to your dressing room mirrors to add a holiday touch, or to any blank space on a wall around your store. Click on the image to follow the video tutorial. 

 

## Vintage Kitsch Deer + Matching Door Decor 

 These vintage looking deer are too cute to pass up and can be used year after year! These can be a fun way to add some ambiance to your window displays, or your exterior decor. Click on the image to read the blog post tutorial for the DIY deer. 

 

## Grand Entryway 

 Looking for something more eye-catching and bold? This grand entryway makes a big statement and is sure to invite new customers in. You can easily create something similar yourself by wrapping long garland around your store&#8217;s exterior and securing with hooks and wire. Then, add lots of ornaments in different sizes, mix colors and let them hang at different lengths. You can customize this to match your store&#8217;s aesthetic! 

 

## Bows Galore! 

 Adding bows to different displays around your store is the perfect way to add a trendy and holiday-themed vibe to your decor this year. This can be as simple as adding bows to a simple wrathe like this picture below, or even decorating your doors to look like giant bows. It&#8217;s simple yet so festive and cute! 

 

 

## Giant Christmas Candles 

 Vintage candles are super popular right now, so making a giant version of them for your store just makes sense! These are made from PVC pipes and duct tape, and the best part? You can re-use them for decor when it&#8217;s your boutique&#8217;s anniversary! 

 

## DIY Scroll Garland 

 There&#8217;s nothing like a handmade garland to warm up your space during the holidays. This easy-to-make and chic decoration inspired by Anthropologie makes for a perfect backdrop for your window display, or even a photo backdrop for your holiday events! 

 Birds Party | DIY Anthropologie Inspired Scroll Garland 

## DIY Yarn Tree

 These DIY yarn trees created by Hollstrom Home add a whimsical touch to your holiday decor and can be customized to match the theme of your shop using your choice of yarn! 

 Hallstrom Home | Yarn Tree DIY with Twinkle Lights 

## Candy Cane Garlands

 Another classic in the roundup! Candy cane garlands are a fun addition to add throughout your walls or even as a photo backdrop. This one is easy, classic, and festive! 

 The Wonder Forest | Candy Cane Garland 

## Balloon Ornaments 

 These balloon ornaments make for such a charming holiday banner. Using just a few supplies, this DIY decoration can be completed in no time! 

 The Wonder Forest | Balloon Baubles 

## Christmas Window Drawings 

 One of our favorite and easy ways to utilize window space in your boutique is to use a white liquid paint pen or a chalk pen. Let your artistic side lead you and you&#8217;ll be amazed at how lovely it will look! 

 Photo by Song Bird Blog via Pinterest 

## Pom Pom Tree 

 This pom pom would be a perfect fit for your checkout counter, beside a mannequin, or an empty space needing some attention. It&#8217;s is easy to make and it can bring in a pop of color that your space needed!

 Aww Sam | Pom Pom Tree 

## Christmas Tree Mannequin 

 This one takes a little more effort and skill, but wow, she&#8217;s a beauty! This glamourous decor is sure to attract customers! 

 Home Depot | Christmas Tree Dress
