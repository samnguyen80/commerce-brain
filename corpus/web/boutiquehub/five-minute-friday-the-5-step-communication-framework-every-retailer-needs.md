# Five Minute Friday: The 5-Step Communication Framework Every Retailer Needs

 What if one of the biggest things holding your boutique back has nothing to do with inventory, marketing, or sales?

 It&#8217;s communication .

 When your staff, vendors, and customers aren&#8217;t on the same page, productivity suffers—and when productivity suffers, profitability does too. In this episode, Sara Burks breaks down the communication process that can help you eliminate those roadblocks: ask questions, listen to the answers, learn from them, implement a plan, and communicate it clearly and consistently.

 From your team and vendor relationships to customer expectations, getting everyone aligned can make a major difference in how your business operates.
