# What is Pink Friday 2026? Everything You Need to Know About the Support Small First Movement
 

 
 
 
 
 

 

 
 

### What is Pink Friday?

 Since 2020, Pink Friday has been a nationwide movement held on the Friday before Black Friday , created to encourage shoppers to discover and support small retailers first during the holiday season.

 In 2026, Pink Friday will take place on Friday, November 20th, 2026. 

 More than just a shopping day, Pink Friday is about celebrating the heart of local economies. It’s a chance for small business owners to showcase their stories, connect with their communities, and remind shoppers why supporting small matters.

 The official tagline, #SupportSmallFirst , gives retailers an opportunity to shine a spotlight on their brand, host events, and rally their community before the holiday rush begins.

 

### The History & Impact of Pink Friday

 Pink Friday was founded in 2020 by The Boutique Hub , with a simple but powerful idea: what if shoppers supported small businesses before turning to Black Friday deals at big-box stores?

 In its first year, hundreds of retailers across the U.S. joined the movement. Within just a few years, that number grew to thousands of small businesses nationwide —from Main Street boutiques to online shops.

 Every November, Pink Friday helps small retailers:

 
 Drive early holiday sales and start the season with momentum

- Educate shoppers about the value of buying small and local

- Build community pride by turning shopping into a celebration
 

 The impact goes beyond sales. Pink Friday has become a cultural movement that reminds people why small businesses matter: they create jobs, support schools and events, fuel local economies, and give neighborhoods their character.

 

### Why Pink Friday Matters

 

- Supports local economies – shopping small keeps dollars in your community.

- Celebrates diversity & creativity – independent retailers offer unique finds you won’t see at mass retailers.

- Empowers business owners – each purchase helps small business owners thrive.
 

 Pink Friday isn’t just about sales—it’s about making intentional choices that create lasting impact.

 

### How to Participate in Pink Friday 2026

#### If You’re a Shopper:

 

- Mark your calendar for November 20, 2026.

- Support small first – visit your favorite boutiques, local shops, and small online retailers. Browse our online nationwide map to check out participating retailers near you. 

- Spread the word – share your Pink Friday finds on social media using #SupportSmallFirst and #PinkFriday .

- Invite friends and family to shop alongside you and choose local over big-box.
 

 

#### If You’re a Small Business:

 

- Sign up to participate – Join Pink Friday 2026 here .

- Plan an event or promotion – host an in-store party, online special, or community giveaway.

- Access free marketing tools – use Pink Friday graphics, banners, and guides to get the word out.

- Engage your audience early – promote across email and social channels leading up to November 20th.
 

 

### Why Retailers Love Pink Friday

 Thousands of small businesses have participated since the event began. Retailers say it helps:

 

- Kick off the holiday season with momentum

- Drive new customers to support small

- Stand out from big-box competition

- Strengthen community connections
 

 Participation is 100% free for all types of small business retailers.
