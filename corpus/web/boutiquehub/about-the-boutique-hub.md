## We Believe In Community > Competition.

 Since 2013, The Boutique Hub has become the world's largest community dedicated to empowering independent retailers.

 We connect retail store owners, e-commerce entrepreneurs, wholesale brands, product makers, and industry experts in one powerful network designed to spark growth and innovation in the ever-evolving retail landscape.

 Through daily business strategy, hands-on training, exclusive wholesale resources, live events, and a powerful voice advocating for independent retailers, The Hub equips small business owners to scale smarter, stay competitive, and thrive in their communities.

## How One Big Idea Transformed An Industry…

 
 
 The Boutique Hub started in 2013 with one simple goal: to bridge the gap between independent retailers and the resources they need to succeed. What began as a small idea to connect shoppers with unique boutiques quickly evolved into something much bigger. Through challenges and early setbacks, The Boutique Hub became a community-driven movement centered on “community over competition,” empowering small business owners with education, tools, and a network that truly has their backs.

 Today, The Boutique Hub continues to change lives, proving that small businesses can make a big impact.

 

 
 
 

 

 

 
 
 
 
 

 
 

### Meet Our Founder & CEO:

 

## Ashley Alderson

 

#### The Dreamer Behind The Boutique Hub

 
 With a background in business, marketing, and economic development, Ashley turned her passion into a reality in 2013, founding The Boutique Hub. Rooted in the mantra “community over competition,” The Hub provides education, resources, and support to thousands of retailers globally. 
 
Outside of work, Ashley is a wife to CFO Eric Alderson, a mom to three amazing kids, and a lover of jewelry, horses, and basketball. As a cancer survivor, she brings a unique perspective on resilience and perseverance to everything she does. Whether she’s riding horses with her kids or playing old lady basketball, Ashley’s heart remains dedicated to helping others chase their dreams and build a life they love.
