 We’re constantly working on our product to make your daily workflow more productive and efficient and your experience with your B2B Wave eCommerce platform effortless. Here are our latest product updates.

### Storefront 

##### Quotes
 We improved the quote feature’s look and feel to make it more user-friendly. 
 
 

##### Contact form
 You can now add Captcha to the contact form on your website for guest visitors. Captcha helps prevent spam form submissions that clutter your inbox. 
 
 

### Admin Panel 

##### Order tracking
 We added the option to show who submitted each order in the order list. With this new feature, you can now tell the difference between orders submitted by sales reps versus those placed directly by admins or customers. 
 
 

##### Product dates
 We’ve updated the date column on the product list so you can track when it was created and updated. That’s especially useful for companies where multiple people and/or integrations update product details. 
 
 

### Imports/exports 
 1. You can now remove parent customer from customers import. 2. You can import payment options when importing customers. 3. You can import images and reference code for product variants. 4. You can import shipping option and additional custom fields when importing orders (admin panel). 5. You can import extra fields. 

### API 
 You can now expose customer admin fields to the API/customers endpoint. 

### Admin Panel 
 Customize your admin panel using CSS and JavaScript to change font sizes and set default values on any form. 

### Integrations 

##### Quickbooks Desktop
 1. We’ve updated the Quickbooks Desktop integration. and you can now update product inventory and prices from Quickbooks Desktop to B2B Wave. 2. You can also import products from Quickbooks Desktop into B2B Wave using the default category:SKU format that Quickbooks provides. B2B Wave will separate the SKU from the category name and will update the product accordingly. 3. We’ve enabled adding customer comments to invoices and sales order memos. We’ve also made it possible to automatically update the order status on B2B Wave after an order has been exported to Quickbooks Desktop. 3. We now support both sales tax and NON-sales tax Quickbooks Desktop accounts. 

##### R+L Carriers
 You can now use an online service called R+L to get live shipping rates from R+L Carriers. 

##### Brightpearl
 We’ve made it possible to define the sales channel on Brightpearl based on specific rules. And we’ve also enabled importing products, categories, and prices from Brightpearl to B2B Wave. 

### Mobile App 
 ‍ 1. Sales reps can now sort products by name, SKU number, and create date. In addition to that, they can also view the barcode, and they can switch on “List for quick ordering” and add products to an order using the +/- buttons.
 
 2. We fixed the sync issues, and now you can sync with your customers and categories without issue.
