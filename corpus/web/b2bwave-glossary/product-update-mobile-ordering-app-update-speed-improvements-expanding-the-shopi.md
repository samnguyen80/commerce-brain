 We’re constantly working on our product to make your daily workflow more productive and efficient and your experience with your B2B Wave eCommerce platform effortless. Here are our latest product updates.

### Mobile App 

##### Extra fields
 On the product view page, you can now see all product extra fields that were created from the Admin panel.

##### Improved speed
 We’ve increased the scrolling speed on the mobile ordering app. The speed is now 10x faster compared to the previous method!

### Admin Panel 

##### New filtering capabilities
 1. We improved the filtering of products through categories by making it faster and better. 2. We&#x27;ve also added the option to filter customers based on province/state and language.

##### Translations
 Company description, brands, and PDF catalog descriptions can now be translated into multiple languages.

### Integrations 

##### QuickBooks Desktop
 It’s now possible to export sales rep details to sales orders/invoices: When a customer places an order that gets sent to QuickBooks Desktop , if there is a linked sales rep, the sales order will contain the sales rep details.

##### Shopify
 It&#x27;s now possible to update the order status (Fulfilled) from Shopify to B2B Wave, as well as to automatically create a product on B2B Wave when a new product is created on Shopify.

##### ChannelAdvisor
 It’s now possible to export order details to ChannelAdvisor through B2B Wave API.

### Imports/exports 

##### Speed improvement for product imports
 We’ve improved product imports, and the process is significantly faster now.
