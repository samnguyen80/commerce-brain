 B2B Wave Bulletin is our regular update that highlights recent product improvements and updates we’ve made to the B2B Wave cloud-based B2B eCommerce platform so you can easily stay up-to-date with what’s new.

### Admin panel 

##### YouTube video embed button for CKEditor
 Users can now use the &#x27;YouTube Embed&#x27; button to easily add YouTube videos to their product descriptions, pages, and news feeds.

##### CSS syntax highlighting
 CSS now contains a syntax highlighter (in Settings/Profile/Advanced/Custom CSS) to help admins spot syntax errors or inconsistencies.

##### Easily attach files to customers
 By defining an extra field of type file for customers, you can easily attach files to their records.

##### Add liquid template for notify order document
 Admins can now easily modify the &#x27;Notify Order Document&#x27; email template, which is used when sending invoices.

##### Support for translating product name and description
 B2B Wave now supports adding product names and descriptions in more than one language.

### Reports 

##### Sum by currency in product sales reports
 In case your price lists are in multiple currencies, product sales reports will show sums broken down by currency.

### Imports/exports 

##### Product price adjustment import or export
 Admins can now import or export a spreadsheet of their product price adjustments.

##### Update customer preferred products with Excel files
 When importing or exporting customers, you can now import and export their preferred products.

### Integrations 

##### Shopify
 Product quantities will now be updated on B2B Wave when products are created or modified in Shopify.

### Storefront 

##### Zero-priced products to show “Contact Us”
 Admins now have the option of showing “Contact Us” instead of pricing information on products that are zero-priced.
