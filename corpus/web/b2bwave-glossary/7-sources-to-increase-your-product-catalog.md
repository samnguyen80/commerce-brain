 Finding reliable suppliers and new products to sell can be challenging, so we have compiled a list of some high-quality sources for finding suppliers effortlessly. 

## 1. Maker’s Row 
 At Maker’s Row, you can browse through 11,000 U.S. manufacturers, both small and large, to find products that fit your specific product catalog needs. You can also ask them to help you find the right suppliers to save time.
 
 

## 2. eSources 
 eSources offers verified suppliers from multiple countries, both for traditional wholesale trade and for dropshipping. They market themselves as a platform solely for UK wholesalers, but they can also be used by US, Canadian, and other wholesalers because suppliers on its platform do ship to every country in the world. The company that owns eSources guarantees the quality of its suppliers, stating that they do conduct some level of due diligence before adding them to their platform.
 
 

## 3. Wholesale Deals 
 WholesaleDeals is a UK-based website, similar to eSources, except that they collect suppliers who are already selling on Amazon and eBay and actively searching for wholesalers. The suppliers are from the UK, USA, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Ireland, Canada, Hong Kong, China, Australia, and several other countries.
 
 

## 4. MFG 
 MFG claims to be the world&#x27;s largest contract manufacturing marketplace and manufacturers directory. In other words, if you want to find and buy directly from a manufacturer, this website is a great place to start. If you want to produce something to sell, they can do that also.
 
 

## 5. Liquidation.com 
 If you’re interested in finding short-term opportunities, you can do that here by monitoring liquidating stock that is sold at great prices. This website is one among many similar ones that offer such products in more than 500 categories.
 
 

## 6. Thomasnet.com 
 The Thomas Network platform provides the tools and information you need to find and select suppliers for industrial products and services.
 
 

## 7. WorldwideBrands 
 WorldwideBrands offers more than 8,000 verified suppliers from the USA, Canada, UK, Hong Kong, and China for both traditional wholesale as well as dropshipping.
