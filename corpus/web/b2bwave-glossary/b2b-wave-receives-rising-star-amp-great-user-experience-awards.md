 B2B Wave, the leading B2B eCommerce solution for small businesses, recently received two awards from FinancesOnline: The “Great User Experience” Award and the “Rising Star” Award for 2017. 
 We are happy to announce that our cloud-based B2B eCommerce solution, B2B Wave , was recognized by a leading business software review platform for our intuitive design and rich functionalities. FinancesOnline granted our software the 2017 Great User Experience Award and the Rising Star Award after thorough testing of our software’s capabilities and usability.
 The Great User Experience Award for top eCommerce software highlights the ease of use and feature set of a business solution. In our case, the FinancesOnline review team cited our software for its capability to provide personalized customer access and a platform for quick orders and reorders. Likewise, our product catalog functionality is considered an important tool for connecting to would-be buyers. Our software can generate beautiful product catalogs in just two clicks.
 Meanwhile, the Rising Star Award is granted to a type of business software that is enjoying a fast-growing user base despite being only recently launched. The review team cited this as a clear indicator that users find our software valuable to their eCommerce business. In addition, our clients find our system quick to set up, thereby boosting their productivity and overall revenues. We are honored to be considered as one of the major brands in our niche by a leading business software review site.
