 We’re constantly working on our product to make your daily workflow more productive and efficient and your experience with your B2B Wave eCommerce platform effortless. Here are our latest product updates.

### Mobile app 

##### Admin users can now place orders on the app‍
 With this new update, both sales reps and admins can place orders through the mobile ordering app on behalf of their customers.

### Admin panel 

##### New dashboard for changing titles and terms that appear on your store
 Admin users can now easily change the texts that appear on the user interface. They can now customize their B2B Wave account to use their industry’s terminology. This is available for all the languages that are used in a B2B Wave account.
 
 

### Integrations 

##### Shopify
 Product variant images are now properly imported from Shopify to B2B Wave and are linked to the right product variants.

### API 

##### Flexible updating of the extra fields on the order details
 Additional fields that are added by users to the orders can now be updated from the API and through integrations.

##### Order status ID and name visible on the API
 When updating the order status through the API, users can now use both the status name and/or the status ID. This is used for not having to look for the status ID or statuses like “Invoiced”&#x27; or “Sent.”

### Imports/exports 

##### Products
 When importing products and images, you will be able to see the status of the image imports. For example, you’ll be able to see which images are still pending to be imported and what is currently being imported. This is useful for users importing large numbers of products.

### Storefront 

##### Sort products based on available quantity
 When customers are browsing products on the storefront, they can now sort based on the available quantity of a product, in addition to the existing sorting options. The products with the highest quantity will appear first.
