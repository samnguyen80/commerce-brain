 We’re constantly working on our product to make your daily workflow more productive and efficient and your experience with your B2B Wave eCommerce platform effortless. Here are our latest product updates.

## B2B Wave + Veeqo integration 
 
 Exciting news: We’re thrilled to announce that B2B Wave now integrates with Veeqo, a leading shipping and inventory management software (US & UK only). Now, you can effortlessly manage shipping details, update inventory, and keep customers in the loop about their orders in one seamless integration. Dive into the thrill of the B2B Wave and Veeqo integration and explore how it works.
 Sounds interesting? Contact our support team to learn more about our Veeqo integration.

## Improved security 
 We’re happy to introduce Two-Factor Authentication (2FA), an extra layer of protection for both customers and admin users on B2B Wave. Double up on protection and take control of your account’s security. With two-factor authentication, your data stays safer, ensuring only authorized access. Discover how you can enhance the security of your account .

## Storefront 
 ‍

### Force default product view
 You can now force how products appear on your storefront to create a seamless buying experience for your customers. Whether you opt for the structured simplicity of a list view or the visually engaging grid view, you can “lock” the default product layout to the one that works best for your customers and business.
 ‍

### Product filters with extra fields
 Enhance navigation on your B2B storefront and offer customers a more personalized shopping experience with our new product filters feature. Learn more about it here. 
 
 

### Quick order
 Streamline customer experience for faster and more efficient ordering through the Quick Order menu. Specifically, now customers can:
 Search and place orders for product variants.
- Use quantity autocomplete for products for which you’ve set a minimum quantity. ‍

### Show Order Admin extra fields on your store
 Opt to show Order Admin extra fields on your storefront’s order view page, which is accessible through the Order history menu. Ensure all crucial information is readily available for customers and improve their post-purchase experience.
 ‍

### Filter available products
 Create an elevated buying experience for customers by:
 
- Allowing them to filter products based on their availability through your B2B store’s category pages. 
- Sort products by what’s available first. ‍

### Add +/- buttons to the product grid view
 Opt to show +/- buttons in the product grid view as a streamlined and error-resistant method for adjusting quantities.
 ‍

### Control news visibility
 Manage news articles’ privacy settings for a personalized and targeted approach to your content distribution.
 ‍

## Integrations 
 ‍

### Brightpearl
 Optimize stock levels efficiently with our warehouse module, allowing you to update individual warehouse quantities separately in B2B Wave from Brightpearl.
 ‍

### Mailchimp
 We’ve made updates to streamline the Mailchimp interface experience on our platform:
 
- See unique emails under the Customers field when creating new audiences to export to Mailchimp through B2B Wave. 
- Experience a more refined user interface with larger result boxes when creating new audiences. Check out how you can connect B2B Wave with Mailchimp .
 ‍

### QuickBooks Desktop
 Transform your workflow with our upgraded QuickBooks Desktop connection by:
 
- Filtering which customers and products you want to import to B2B Wave; this way, you minimize errors and ensure data accuracy.
- Importing customer price levels, an update that allows you to maintain personalized pricing strategies without wasting extra time. ‍

### QuickBooks Online
 Experience greater efficiency with our QuickBooks Online integration improvements:
 
- Update and synchronize product pricing automatically using QuickBooks webhooks for seamless price management.
- Tag line items with specific QuickBooks (QB) Classes for enhanced accounting accuracy.
- Set the invoice shipping date as the order delivery date and ensure precision and alignment with the actual order fulfillment. ‍

### ShipStation
 We’ve upgraded our ShipStation integration to allow you to:
 
- Enable automatic order creation on ShipStation when you set a specific order status. 
- Use the correct weight metric (e.g., kilos, pounds), if set, when creating orders on ShipStation. ‍

### Square and Authorize.net
 Allow customers to remove their saved cards from the “Customers Details” page, ensuring heightened payment security and privacy.
 ‍

### Xero
 Set a custom due date for invoices using a special order extra field named “ Xero Invoice Due Date ,” ensuring customer adherence to agreements and streamlining your administrative processes.
 ‍

### Zoho Inventory
 Enjoy a smoother connection with Zoho inventory with the following updates:
 
- Enjoy faster product imports from Zoho inventory to B2B Wave.
- Import product descriptions effortlessly from Zoho inventory to streamline the product management process.
- Ensure data accuracy with improved category and SKU matching when importing products from Zoho inventory.
- Create sales orders in Zoho inventory and set the salesperson field, if configured on B2B Wave, for a better sales workflow.
- Opt to commit stock from a specific warehouse when creating sales orders for better inventory management.
- Configure the B2B Wave and Zoho inventory integration to allow stock updates from a single warehouse.
- Create sales orders faster through reduced API calls from B2B Wave. ‍

## Customers 
 ‍

### Order payment for disabled users
 Allow disabled customers to settle outstanding invoices and recover revenue from inactive accounts.
 ‍

### Independent sub-customers’ privacy 
 Opt to assign sub-customers to different privacy groups than their parents for a more flexible and tailored customer management experience.
 ‍

### VAT number validation
 Validate the VAT number of EU customers through B2B Wave to minimize errors and ensure accurate invoicing.
 ‍

## API 
 ‍

### API key generation
 Enhance control, security, and flexibility with our new multiple API keys feature. Generate new keys, determine which API endpoints they can access, and restrict them for use by specific IP addresses. Learn more about multiple API key generation .
 
 

### Products
 Get more out of B2B Wave’s Products API with these updates: 
 
- Refine precision with your product imports using the newly added manufacturer_id , package_quantity , and package_quantity_measurement_unit_id fields.
- Unset product privacy groups through our API with a simple, straightforward process. ‍

### Customers
 Include and set payment_options , privacy_groups , and locale easily through our Customers API, ensuring adaptability to diverse customer needs.
 ‍

### Orders
 Experience an elevated order creation process through B2B Wave’s API. With our latest improvements, you can: 
 
- Define shipping_option_id and shipping_costs , if provided.
- Include total_volume in the orders response
- Include t otal_weight in the orders response . When returned, this field also appears on Zapier. ‍

### Categories
 Utilize the new privacy_groups , subcategories_private , and is_private fields and easily manage product categories through our powerful API.
 ‍

## Admin panel 
 ‍

### Products
 Enjoy increased control in managing product statuses by choosing to override product status rules. Also, you can opt to specifically allow manually applied product statuses to overhaul the “Backorder” and “Out of stock” status rules. Learn more about product status rules .
 ‍

### Customers
 Filter customers based on their assigned privacy group and manage your diverse buyer base more efficiently.
 ‍

### Orders
 We’ve upgraded our Orders menu to allow for smoother navigation and enhanced functionalities through these updates:
 
- Allow order creation directly from the Admin Panel for convenience, seamless transactions, and improved customer service for buyers who prefer placing orders through email or phone.
- Start order numbering from a specific digit (150, rather than the default 1) to align order numbers with your existing systems and improve order management processes.
- Add, change, or remove coupons directly from the order’s page easily and gain flexible and efficient control over discount management.
- Choose to display available product quantity when editing orders for increased accuracy in inventory management.
- Choose to prevent invoice creation for unsubmitted orders and align billing seamlessly with finalized transactions. 
- Recover deleted orders quickly through our “ Restore Order ” feature and minimize the impact of accidental deletions on your order management workflow while preserving data integrity.
- Track orders effortlessly by filtering orders using their ShipStation order number. ‍

### Profile settings
 Customize your B2B portal’s contact form email settings and set communication channels and their recipients to ensure that you promptly respond to customer inquiries.
 ‍

## Sales Reps Portal 
 We’ve got a few exciting updates on the sales reps portal front:
 
- Give sales reps managers access to their assigned sales reps’ customers and orders. 
- Allow sales reps to edit ordered products or add products to their assigned customers’ orders for more flexibility.
- Choose to enable sales reps to export customers.
- Opt to allow sales reps to view their customers’ unsubmitted orders, giving them real-time visibility. Learn more about setting up sales reps’ accounts .
 ‍

## Suppliers 
 ‍

### Order messaging
 Allow configured suppliers to send messages directly to buyers about their orders, improving communication, transparency, and efficiency throughout the ordering process.
 ‍

## Payments 
 ‍

### Payment due date for bank transfers
 Set a payment due date for new orders for customers who select the bank/wire transfer option. In addition, you can quickly identify overdue payments and keep track of your settled transactions with the ability to filter your orders based on their payment status. Enjoy more predictability in your cash flow and improved efficiency with order processing.
 ‍

## Imports/exports 
 ‍

### New orders email notifications
 Add or update who can receive email notifications for new orders easily using the email_new_orders column in the customers sample import file , the existing customers export file , or via the column matching functionality . Read all about importing and exporting customers .
 ‍

## Reports 
 We’ve refined the details of our reports. Now, you can:
 
- Filter the Product Sales and Customer Product Sale s reports by delivery date for more targeted results.
- Enjoy a thorough overview of order activity on your B2B store with the freshly introduced Order Summar y and Order Summary by Status Change reports. ‍

## Email templates 
 ‍

### New customization option
 We’ve introduced another customization option in our email templates. Specifically, you can add order.admin.fields.* to the list of variables for email templates, which corresponds to Order Edit (admin) extra fields.
