 We’re constantly working on our product to make your daily workflow more productive and efficient and your experience with your B2B Wave eCommerce platform effortless. Here are our latest product updates.

### The iOS Mobile app is here 🎉
 The B2B Wave app is now available for iPhones and iPads. Your sales reps can directly create orders and view products from their iOS devices.

### Storefront 

##### Improved user interface
 We improved the product list view for devices with smaller screen sizes to make it easier to view the products.

##### Request payment later
 You can now allow your customers to submit an order and send them a payment request at a later stage. The payment needs to be an online payment by card.

### Imports/exports 

##### Custom currency on order exports
 If you&#x27;re selling in multiple currencies, you can now set the order currency on the order export.

### Admin Panel 

##### Automatic product deactivation
 We&#x27;ve made it possible to automatically deactivate products and completely hide them from customers when those products are not available. 

### Integrations 

##### QuickBooks Online
 If B2B Wave couldn&#x27;t create a product on QuickBooks Online through the integration , we will now show detailed error information so you can understand what happened.

##### QuickBooks Desktop
 1. Same as for QuickBooks Online errors, when a sales order, estimate, or invoice cannot be created, we&#x27;ll show detailed error information so you can understand what happened. 2. You can now create sales orders with and without sales tax. 3. We&#x27;ve made it possible to import products from QuickBooks Desktop to to B2B Wave. 4. B2B Wave&#x27;s order status can now change when an order is exported to QuickBooks Desktop. 5. You now have the option to honor QuickBooks Desktop prices when creating sales orders, estimates, and invoices.

##### Xero
 Same as for our QuickBooks integrations, if B2B Wave couldn&#x27;t create an invoice on Xero through the integration, we will now show detailed error information so you can understand what happened.

##### ShipStation
 If an order gets a tracking number, B2B Wave will now automatically change the order status and send an email with the tracking number to the customer.

##### Brightpearl
 You can now automatically create an order when it gets submitted and add it to a specific channel, and with a specific order status.

##### ChannelAdvisor
 1. You can now automatically update the shipping option and the tracking number from ChannelAdvisor to B2B Wave. 2. You can also automatically update the product quantity from ChannelAdvisor to B2B Wave. 3. You can automatically update one or more price lists from ChannelAdvisor to B2B Wave.

##### Stripe
 It&#x27;s now possible to connect multiple Stripe accounts and assign a different Stripe account to each customer. This is particularly useful when you have different accounts for different countries.
