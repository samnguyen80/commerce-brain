# Christian Fiorelli
 

 

 
 
 SVP eCommerce 

 

 
 
 Alexander Wang 

 

 
 
 Christian Fiorelli is the Senior Vice President of Ecommerce & CRM at Alexander Wang, where he leads global digital strategy, innovation and CRM. 

 With over 15 years of experience in fashion and luxury retail, Christian combines data-driven insights with a deep understanding of brand storytelling to elevate the customer experience across digital channels. Prior to joining Alexander Wang, he held regional and global leadership roles at Giorgio Armani, Max Mara and YOOX Net-a-Porter, gaining deep expertise across both the Americas and APAC markets.
