## A Member of the Largest Global Ecommerce Events Portfolio
 

 

 
 
 CommerceNext is now part of CloserStill Media, a global events company specializing in high-value, content-driven events and building and nurturing B2B professional communities. As one of the fastest-growing global events businesses operating best in class events throughout the United States, Europe and Asia, CloserStill Media delivers across a range of industry verticals.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 

### Ecommerce Expo UK
 Is the UK’s largest and most established ecommerce event, with over 300 exhibitors and 11,000 visitors. Ecommerce Expo UK is collocated with Technology for Marketing, The UK’s definitive martech event.

 

 

 

 
 

 
 
 
 

### E Show Barcelona and E Show Madrid
 Is Southern Europe’s largest ecommerce events, deliver over 400 exhibitors and 15,500 visitors across the 2 shows. (Madrid: 300 exhibitors, 8000 attendees; Barcelona: 300 exhibitors, 7500 attendees). CloserStill also runs a 1-2-1 meeting event in Spain for the ecommerce sector called eForum.

 

 

 

 
 

 
 
 
 

### Ecommerce Expo Asia
 Is the region’s largest ecommerce event taking place annually in October at Marina Sands Hotel delivering more than 3,000 ecommerce professionals. CloserStill Asia also produces the Retail Executive Summit in April, a C-level event for retail professionals.
