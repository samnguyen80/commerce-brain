# Leslie Emmons Burthey
 

 

 
 
 SVP Marketing 

 

 
 
 TeePublic 

 

 
 
 SESSION : 

 Level Up the Foundations: Search, Social & Video

 Leslie Emmons Burthey is VP of Marketing at FabFitFun, the lifestyle membership creating personalized experiences that introduce you to the best the world has to offer. Leslie focuses on growth, content, CRM, and consumer insights. Leslie was previously the VP of Marketing at Winc (formerly Club W). Prior to Winc, she gained consumer product experience at Christian Dior and Ralph Lauren. She has an MBA from Stanford, and an undergraduate degree from the Wharton School.
