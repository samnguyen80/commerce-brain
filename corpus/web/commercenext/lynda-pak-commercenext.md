# Lynda Pak
 

 

 
 
 SVP & Divisional CIO 

 

 
 
 The Estée Lauder Companies 

 

 
 
 Lynda Pak is a Senior Vice President, Global Brand and Corporate Function Technology, at the Estée Lauder Companies. Lynda joined ELC in 2016 and has been in her current role since 2020, driving technology strategy and operations for all ELC brands and functions. With more than 20 years of both e-commerce/omnichannel retail business and IT experience, she has served on the executive leadership teams at Bed Bath and Beyond (BBB), Borders Group (BGI) and other leading companies in the consumer products and retail industries. During her 14+ years as a consultant, Lynda led large, complex ERP-based transformation programs to drive business value for Fortune 100 clients.
