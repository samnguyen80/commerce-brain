# Key Topics
 Shaping the Agenda
 

 
 From deploying AI, forecasting in chaos, to navigating tariffs, rethinking assortments and redefining loyalty in an era of algorithmic discovery, we’re digging into the mechanics of leading retail growth through complexity.
 

 
 
 
 
 Agentic AI in Retail 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 Explore how next-gen agentic AI is transforming retail by moving beyond automation to take proactive actions&#8211; optimizing pricing, assortments, marketing and customer experiences in real time &#8211;helping retailers adapt faster and lead with intelligence.
 

 

 

 

 
 
 
 Loyalty & Retention 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 As acquisition costs rise, loyalty is being redefined. Learn how brands are building deeper connections and turning customers into lifelong advocates.
 

 

 

 

 
 
 
 Ecommerce Tech Innovations 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 Kick off with a high-energy startup showcase where YOU choose the winner. Then watch the latest retail technology come to life in our brand-new Live Demo format—an up-close look at the innovations shaping commerce&#8217;s next chapter.
 

 

 

 

 
 
 
 Omnichannel Transformation 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 Shoppers move seamlessly across channels and your brand must too. See how retailers are creating unified experiences that win both online and in-store.
 

 

 

 

 
 
 
 Customer Acquisition 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 Winning new customers has never been more competitive or costly. Learn how marketers are rethinking their strategies to drive sustainable growth and turn browsers into buyers.
