# Eddie Patzsch
 

 

 
 
 VP Revenue 

 

 
 
 Optimove 

 

 
 
 Eddie Patzsch is a revenue leader and former founder with experience building and scaling organizations across enterprise SaaS and product-led growth companies. He is the VP Revenue at Optimove, where he oversees global new business, customer growth, partnerships and GTM Engineering. 

 Prior to Optimove, Eddie founded PartnerPortal.io, a B2B SaaS platform that was acquired in 2023 by Insight Partners. He has also held revenue roles at Groupon, Yext, Searchmetrics and AdRoll.
