###### What is RepSpark's Insignia solution for product customization?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 RepSpark's Insignia solution enables brands and retailers to customize products with logos, artwork, and color choices directly during checkout. Users can upload their design, select garments, choose logo placement (such as left chest or sleeves), and preview the final product. Insignia offers full creative control with no restrictions on logo placement or color, making it ideal for events, corporate gifting, golf courses, and more. Note: Insignia is best suited for brands seeking maximum flexibility; those needing strict compliance with licensor rules should consider the Licensing solution instead. 

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### How does RepSpark's Licensing solution work for custom orders?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 The Licensing solution automates compliance for brands selling licensed merchandise. Users select from pre-approved logos and product assortments, and the system automatically filters options to ensure only licensor-approved products are available. It enforces rules for logo placement, color combinations, and product guidelines, and calculates royalty fees at checkout. This is especially useful for brands working with organizations like the NFL, NCAA, or MLB. Note: Licensing restricts creative options to licensor guidelines; brands seeking unrestricted customization should use Insignia. 

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### What are the main differences between Insignia and Licensing in RepSpark?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 Insignia provides full creative control for product customization, allowing users to upload any logo, choose placement and colors, and preview designs without restrictions. Licensing, on the other hand, enforces licensor rules—restricting logo placement, color combinations, and product selection to ensure compliance, and automatically calculating royalties. Choose Insignia for flexibility and Licensing for compliance with third-party brand requirements. Note: Brands needing both flexibility and compliance may need to use both solutions depending on the order type. 

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### How do I place a custom order using RepSpark's Insignia solution?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 To place a custom order with Insignia: 1) Upload your logo or artwork, 2) Select your preferred garments and colors, 3) Choose logo placement (e.g., left chest, sleeve), 4) Preview your design, and 5) Finalize and submit your order. The process is designed to be intuitive and can be completed during checkout. Note: Insignia does not enforce licensor compliance; for licensed products, use the Licensing workflow. 

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### How do I ensure compliance when selling licensed products through RepSpark?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 RepSpark's Licensing solution automates compliance by filtering product and logo options to only those approved by licensors, enforcing placement and color rules, and calculating royalties at checkout. This ensures all orders meet licensor requirements and reduces the risk of non-compliance. Note: Creative options are limited to licensor-approved choices; for unrestricted customization, use Insignia.
