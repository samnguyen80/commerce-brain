# Sun Mountain Partners with RepSpark to Streamline Its Wholesale Ordering Experience 

 
 
 
 by Sawyer Frank on August 12, 2026
 

 
 Built on more than 45 years of trust, quality, and reliability, Sun Mountain will use RepSpark's digital wholesale platform to make ordering faster, more transparent, and more efficient for its reps and retail partners. 

 
 (Missoula, MT & Anaheim, CA) – Sun Mountain , a golf equipment brand that has combined quality and heritage to craft premium golf gear for over 45 years, has partnered with RepSpark to modernize its wholesale ordering experience.

 By joining the golf industry's leading B2B e-commerce platform, Sun Mountain is giving its sales reps and retail partners a faster, more transparent, and more efficient way to do business.

 Sun Mountain designs and manufactures golf bags, travel bags, carts, and apparel built on trust, quality, and reliability. Every product is engineered with purpose for golfers who demand performance in all conditions.

 "Trust, quality, and reliability guide everything we do at Sun Mountain," said Grant Lordan, Senior Director of Marketing and Brand Strategy for Sun Mountain. "RepSpark helps us extend those same principles to the way we support our reps and retail partners, making the ordering process faster, more transparent, and more efficient."

## 

#### The TQR Standard 

 Sun Mountain's legacy is one of firsts. The brand invented the first stand bag and engineered the patented ClubGlider kickstand travel bag, changing how golfers carry and travel with their gear. That spirit of innovation continues across every category the company designs and manufactures today.

 The results speak for themselves: 87 of Golf Digest's top 100 courses in the United States carry Sun Mountain, and the brand is trusted by players across the PGA TOUR, LPGA Tour, and DP World Tour.

 Since 1981, the company has focused on building equipment golfers can rely on, and that consistency has built a reputation rooted in Trust, Quality, and Reliability, both in what the brand makes and in how it stands behind it.

 The TQR Standard represents Sun Mountain's commitment to building products golfers can trust in any condition. 

 Key Highlights of the Sun Mountain Approach: 

 
 
 Trust : Confidence before you ever step onto the course, backed by more than 45 years of building gear golfers count on.

 

- 
 Quality: Premium materials, purposeful construction, and relentless attention to detail that you can see and feel.

 

- 
 Reliability: Gear that performs round after round, season after season, and travel after travel, without compromise.

 

#### Empowering Wholesale Growth 

 Through RepSpark, Sun Mountain offers retail buyers a frictionless B2B purchasing experience. Buyers can access Sun Mountain's digital catalogs to view line sheets, check available inventory, and place seamless wholesale orders. 

 The partnership gives Sun Mountain's reps and retail partners the modern tools they need to order with confidence, extending the brand's promise of trust, quality, and reliability to every step of the buying experience.

 "Sun Mountain is one of the most respected and innovative names in golf, with a reputation built over four decades of doing things the right way," said Meghann Butcher, CEO of RepSpark. "Their commitment to trust, quality, and reliability is exactly the standard today's retailers value. We're thrilled to provide the B2B technology that helps Sun Mountain serve its partners faster and more efficiently than ever."
