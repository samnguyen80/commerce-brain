### 
 FAQs
 

 
 
 
 

## 
 Common questions answered.
 

 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### What is RepSpark?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 RepSpark is the #1 B2B wholesale ecommerce platform in golf, trusted by 250+ brands and connected to 100,000+ retailers worldwide. Founded in 2007 and headquartered in Anaheim, CA, RepSpark gives brands the tools to manage and grow their wholesale channel while giving retailers a seamless, intuitive buying experience. The platform supports seasonal pre-book and at-once ordering, custom embroidery through its Insignia customization engine, event microsites for tournaments and corporate programs, and more. 

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### What is AIMS360?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 AIMS360 is the ERP built for consumer brands and the people who make and sell physical product. For more than 40 years it has run apparel, footwear, accessories, beauty, wellness, home, baby and pet brands on one platform, processing orders from every channel including wholesale, DTC, EDI, and retail POS through one omnichannel workflow, alongside inventory, production, warehouse and 3PL, retailer EDI to 350+ partners, and accounting. Whether you sell wholesale to PGA Tour Superstore, DICK’S Sporting Goods, and Scheels or ship direct from Shopify, AIMS360 handles the size-and-color matrix of apparel and the lot-and-expiration tracking of CPG in a single system, so growing brands scale without stitching tools together. 

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### What is the RepSpark + AIMS360 integration?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 A direct API integration connecting RepSpark's digital wholesale selling platform with AIMS360's apparel ERP. RepSpark powers the buyer-facing experience — digital catalogs, curated assortments, microsites, and order entry — while AIMS360 serves as the operational source of truth for inventory, production, costing, fulfillment, invoicing, and reporting.

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### How does inventory sync between AIMS360 and RepSpark?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 AIMS360 calculates available-to-sell inventory by date, factoring in future-dated orders and incoming work-in-progress. RepSpark surfaces that calculation through the integration, giving sales reps and buyers accurate availability by ship date — not just units on hand.

 

 

 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### Who is the RepSpark + AIMS360 integration for?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 Apparel brands selling wholesale — including golf, resort, licensed, custom, and fashion apparel — that want to connect a professional digital selling experience with a purpose-built apparel ERP.

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### Can AIMS360 handle orders from channels outside RepSpark?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 Yes. Orders from other channels, including EDI, flow into AIMS360 alongside RepSpark orders, giving brands one omnichannel workflow for picking, fulfillment, invoicing, and reporting across every order source.

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### What results have brands seen with the integration?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 Full Turn Custom Apparel eliminated manual order re-keying, saves 2–4 hours weekly on inventory availability alone, and scaled into three lines of business without adding home-office headcount in five years.

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### Does the integration support custom and pre-book selling models?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 Yes. Brands use the connected stack to run evergreen at-once selling against live inventory, time-windowed pre-book campaigns through RepSpark, and highly customized programs supported by AIMS360's production, costing, and fulfillment workflows.
