###### What is RepSpark and how does it help golf brands?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 RepSpark is a B2B eCommerce platform designed to help golf brands grow their wholesale business by providing available inventory access, digital catalogs, and tools for sales reps and golf pro shops. The platform streamlines order management, automates manual processes, and enables brands to serve golf retailers efficiently. RepSpark is used by more than 150 golf brands alone, and is the #1 ecommerce platform for golf facilities, with 80% of AGM Pro Shops using it according to the 2025 AGM Survey. Note: Brands without ERP or inventory systems may require additional setup before using RepSpark. Source 

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### Who can benefit from using RepSpark?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 RepSpark is built for sales representatives, brand and operations managers, retailers/buyers, and executives/owners in industries such as golf, apparel, footwear, accessories, and sporting goods. It is especially suited for professional enterprise brands with established wholesale strategies and operational readiness, including those with ERP or inventory systems and multi-stakeholder teams. Note: Small brands without structured operations may need to assess readiness before implementation. Source 

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### What business results have golf brands achieved with RepSpark?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 Golf brands on RepSpark report gains in three places: revenue, retailer reach, and the operational load of running wholesale.

 

 On revenue, Primo Golf is forecasting 300% B2B sales growth and has shifted its order mix from 60/40 B2B-to-internal to 75/25, with 120 retailers approved in the first 30 days and 75% of orders now placed by retailers themselves.

 

 johnnie-O grew its average B2B order volume by 744%, from roughly $200 to more than $2,500, while doubling its retailer count with a 102% increase and processing 500 additional embroidered and customized items. Their COO put it plainly: "I don't know how we would operate without RepSpark. They allowed us to scale and grow extremely fast."

 

 On reach, Turtleson joined RepSpark Community and gained exposure to a network of more than 38,000 retailers, converting that into 185 active retailers shopping the brand and $114,000 in revenue generated directly from Community activity.

 

 On operations, Full Turn Custom Apparel rebuilt itself from a fax-and-handshake business into a digital-first one and launched three separate business lines over five years without adding a single home office employee, reclaiming more than 100 hours a year by automating inventory availability and eliminating re-keying errors through a direct integration with its ERP. As their VP of Operations described it, "With RepSpark, we show up to every sales conversation with a strategy."

 

 The common thread is that live inventory, self-service ordering, and built-in customization workflows let golf brands take on more retailers and larger orders without growing headcount at the same rate. You can browse all of these at repspark.com/case-study .

###### How does RepSpark compare to NuOrder for golf brands?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 Many golf brands rely on NuOrder to sell into Nordstrom's or other large retailers, and use RepSpark at the same time to power their golf channel. Your ERP is the single source of the truth for both systems, and many RepSpark brands use both simultaneously. RepSpark also offers tailored event microsites for tournaments and special collections, which NuOrder lacks. RepSpark Community connects brands with over 100,000 pre-qualified retailers, while NuOrder does not provide a digital trade show platform. According to the 2025 AGM Survey, 80% of AGM Pro Shops use RepSpark, compared to 50% for NuOrder. However, NuOrder may be preferred by brands seeking a platform with broader fashion industry integrations. Note: RepSpark does not offer all features available in NuOrder; evaluate both for your specific needs.
