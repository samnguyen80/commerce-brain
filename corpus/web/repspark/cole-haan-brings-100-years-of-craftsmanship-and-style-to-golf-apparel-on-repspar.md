# Cole Haan Brings 100 Years of Craftsmanship and Style to Golf Apparel on RepSpark 

 
 
 
 by Sawyer Frank on August 13, 2026
 

 
 Premium golf apparel from a global lifestyle brand now available to retailers through RepSpark. 

 
 (New York, NY & Anaheim, CA) – Cole Haan , a global lifestyle brand renowned for nearly a century of craftsmanship, design, and innovation, now offers its golf apparel collection to retailers on RepSpark , the golf industry's leading B2B wholesale eCommerce platform. As part of the Catapult Brand Group portfolio, Cole Haan on RepSpark gives retailers a streamlined, digital-first way to discover and order from a collection designed for the modern golfer who moves seamlessly between the course, the office, and everyday life. Golf shops, specialty retailers, and pro shops will now have an ordering experience for Cole Haan's golf apparel collection, backed by accurate available inventory and the operational infrastructure of the broader Catapult and RepSpark partnership. Cole Haan's golf collection pairs refined, sophisticated styling with performance-driven fabrics, comfort, and versatility. Designed for people who are always on the move, the collection features elevated pieces that transition seamlessly from the course to off-course adventures.

## 

#### Nearly a Century of Craftsmanship 

 Cole Haan was founded in 1928 on a commitment to craftsmanship, design, comfort, and innovation. What began as an iconic footwear brand has evolved into a global lifestyle brand with a presence in more than 500 stores across 100+ countries. In Spring 2026, Cole Haan introduced a new golf apparel collection, bringing the same design philosophy and performance mindset that defined its footwear heritage to golf styling. The result is a sophisticated, modern approach to golf fashion that honors the brand's legacy while meeting the needs of today's golfer. The apparel is now available to retailers on RepSpark. Rooted in a commitment to innovation and refined style, Cole Haan blends timeless elegance with functional performance, creating pieces designed for those who refuse to compromise on either.

#### Key Highlights of the Cole Haan Approach 

 
 
 Refined, Sophisticated Styling: Elevated design language that stands apart on and off the course.

- 
 Performance-Driven Fabrics: Advanced materials that deliver comfort, mobility, and durability. 

- 
 Versatile Design: Pieces that transition seamlessly from golf to office to casual lifestyle. 

- 
 Heritage of Innovation: Nearly 100 years of design expertise and craftsmanship applied to modern golf. 

- 
 Global Credibility: A trusted brand with established presence in 100+ countries and 500+ retail locations. 

 

#### Elevating the Retail Experience 

 Through RepSpark, Cole Haan offers retail buyers a frictionless B2B purchasing experience. Buyers can access Cole Haan's digital catalogs to view line sheets, check available inventory, and place seamless wholesale orders for golf apparel. Cole Haan gives pro shops a premium, trend-driven apparel offering that attracts discerning golfers and elevates the retail experience, all within one digital destination. "Cole Haan represents exactly what premium golf retail should be," said Meghann Butcher, CEO of RepSpark. "With nearly a century of design expertise, a global reputation for quality, and a collection that bridges golf and lifestyle, Cole Haan gives retailers a sophisticated partner that drives margin and customer loyalty. We're excited to bring Cole Haan's complete offering to golf retailers through the Catapult Brand Group and RepSpark partnership."

#### About Cole Haan 

 Cole Haan is a global lifestyle brand founded in 1928 and known for nearly 100 years of craftsmanship, design, and innovation. With a presence in more than 500 stores across 100+ countries, Cole Haan creates sophisticated, performance-driven products for people who move seamlessly between the course, the office, and everyday life. Cole Haan Golf Apparel pairs refined styling with advanced fabrics and comfort, delivering elevated pieces that transition from the course to any adventure. Learn more at colehaan.com.

#### About Catapult Brand Group 

 Representing a curated portfolio of premium brands, Catapult simplifies the buying experience by providing retailers with a single, trusted partner for product sourcing, sales support, and marketing resources. By combining industry expertise with strategic partnerships and a customer-first mindset, Catapult empowers brands to expand their reach, strengthen retail relationships, and create meaningful connections across the golf and sport industries.

#### RepSpark Systems 

 RepSpark is the leading provider of digital solutions for B2B wholesale commerce. Designed to be personalized, intuitive, and powerfully intelligent, RepSpark’s platform drives brands to connect and collaborate with their buyers to place orders, create digital line sheets, and analyze key data 24/7. RepSpark brands leverage technology to drive sales growth and better serve their retailers. Founded in 2007, RepSpark is headquartered in Anaheim, CA.
