# Foxden Fairways Brings Its Family Golf Apparel to Retailers on RepSpark 

 
 
 
 by Sawyer Frank on August 27, 2026
 

 
 Clubs, resorts, and retailers can order Foxden Fairways' elevated golf apparel for young golfers and the whole family through RepSpark. 

 
 (Atlanta, GA & Anaheim, CA) – Foxden Fairways , a golf apparel brand that brings classic country-club style to young golfers and their families, is available to order on RepSpark , the golf industry's leading B2B wholesale e-commerce platform. As part of the Catapult Brand Group portfolio, Foxden Fairways on RepSpark gives retailers a streamlined, digital-first way to discover and order its collection. Clubs, resorts, and specialty retailers will now have an ordering experience backed by accurate available inventory and the operational infrastructure of the broader Catapult and RepSpark partnership. Foxden Fairways was built on the idea that golf is best when it's shared. The brand pairs timeless country-club aesthetics with modern performance fabrics across polos, skorts, dresses, layers, hats, and more, all designed to move easily from the course to everything that follows. Foxden makes it easy for parents and kids to share their love of the game on and off the course with matching styles sized for the whole family. “It’s incredibly meaningful to see Foxden being worn by families at some of the country’s great golf destinations. We have a deep appreciation for the places, people, and traditions that make this game special, and we’re excited to work alongside Catapult and RepSpark as Foxden continues to grow,” said Graham Cohen, Founder, Foxden Fairways.

## 

#### Golf, Made for the Whole Family 

 Foxden Fairways started with a father who discovered a passion for golf and wanted to share it with his children. When he looked for apparel that offered young players the same quality and style as adult gear, he found few options for the youngest golfers. That gap became the brand's mission: to design elevated apparel that lets kids and parents enjoy the game together, crafted with the same care and performance as the pieces adults wear, at a more approachable price. The brand was inspired by the beauty of the course, from sandy dunes and tall pines to a simple breeze along the fairway. Foxden celebrates time spent outdoors together, and what began as a solution for one family has grown into a collection for families everywhere.

#### Key Highlights of the Foxden Fairways Approach 

 
 
 Built for Young Golfers : Elevated golf apparel that pairs classic country-club style with modern performance and comfort.

- 
 Coordinated Family Looks: Youth sizing from 2T through 13–14, with select styles offered in men's and women's sizes.

- 
 Proven on the Course: Already found at premier clubs, resorts, and PGA TOUR events nationwide.

- 
 Custom Programs: Clubs and organizations can create fully custom polos, skorts, hoodies , and sweaters in their own colors and designs, starting at a 50-piece minimum. 

 
 Elevating the Retail Experience 

 Through RepSpark, Foxden Fairways offers retail buyers a frictionless B2B purchasing experience. Buyers can access Foxden Fairways' products, check available inventory, and place seamless wholesale orders. Foxden gives pro shops and resorts a differentiated family and youth apparel option, plus custom club programs, all within one digital destination. "Foxden Fairways fills a real need in golf retail with elevated apparel made for the next generation of players and their families," said Meghann Butcher, CEO of RepSpark. "It's exactly the kind of fresh, differentiated brand clubs and resorts are looking for, and we're thrilled to bring it to retailers through the Catapult Brand Group and RepSpark partnership."

#### About Foxden Fairways 

 Foxden Fairways is a golf apparel brand bringing elevated, timeless style to the next generation of golfers and their families. Inspired by the traditions and beauty of the game, Foxden combines classic country-club aesthetics with modern performance fabrics across collections designed for boys, girls, and adults. With a focus on quality, comfort, and coordinated family style, Foxden Fairways creates apparel made to be enjoyed on the course and wherever the game takes you. To learn more, visit www.foxdenfairways.com.

By combining industry expertise with strategic partnerships and a customer-first mindset, Catapult empowers brands to expand their reach, strengthen retail relationships, and create meaningful connections across the golf and sport industries.
