## 
 Frequently Asked Questions
 

 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### How robust is the RepSpark community's functionality? What features does it offer for managing listings, transactions, and interactions between buyers and sellers?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 The functionality of RepSpark Community is robust, offering comprehensive features for managing listings, transactions, and buyer-seller interactions. Key features include uploading a public catalog to showcase your product assortment, displaying retailer requirements, and receiving leads from new retailers who want to buy your product. Additionally, brands can upload their logo, an image, and provide detailed descriptions to give retailers a preview of their offerings.

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### How intuitive is the RepSpark community interface for both buyers and sellers? 

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 The marketplace interface is intuitive and user-friendly, designed to provide a seamless experience for both buyers and sellers.

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### What types of analytics are available for my Digital Storefront in Community?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 The Digital Storefront in the Community offers detailed analytics, including impressions, clicks on your brand description, and the number of new retailer requests. These insights help you optimize your storefront's performance and engagement.

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### Does RepSpark manage the buyer requests?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 No, RepSpark does not manage buyer requests. Brands are responsible for reviewing, approving, or denying retailer requests based on their specific requirements and following their onboarding process. RepSpark does not have the ability to deny or approve retailer requests.

 

 

 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### What is the process for accepting new buyers? Is there a process for vetting sellers and ensuring quality?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 Each brand is responsible for setting its own process for accepting new buyers and vetting sellers to ensure quality. It is recommended to clearly display your requirements on your brand details page and designate a person to review all requests and properly onboard new retailers.

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### What marketing opportunities are available within RepSpark Community?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 RepSpark Community offers extensive marketing opportunities. New brands are featured to retailers in the marketplace, shared on social media, press releases, newsletters, and can pitch their brand in a monthly retailer webinar. Additionally, brands can purchase a Featured Brand spot for prominent placement in the marketplace for a month. Further marketing options are available, and details can be found on the pricing page .

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### How can new retailers discover my brand In RepSpark Community?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 New retailers can discover your brand through multiple channels, including search functionality, product categories, brand categories, industry filters, and sections highlighting new, featured, and trending brands.
