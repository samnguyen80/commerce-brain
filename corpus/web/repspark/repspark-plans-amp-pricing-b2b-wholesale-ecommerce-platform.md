###### What features are included in the Accelerate plan? 

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 The Accelerate plan is designed for emerging brands and successful DTC brands starting their wholesale journey. It includes digital catalogs, order management, basic analytics, support for up to 3 users (with additional available), unlimited B2B users, live chat, knowledgebase, training webinars, and access to RepSpark Pay. Add-ons like Event Microsites and Insignia Integration for custom decoration are available. Note: Multi-division support, advanced analytics, and multi-currency are not included in this plan. 

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### What features are included in the Professional plan?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 The Professional plan is suited for growing businesses needing more sophisticated tools. It includes everything in Accelerate, plus ERP integration, advanced analytics, inventory management, support for up to 10 users (with additional available), multi-currency, product-level multi-language support, virtual showroom, and a one-hour training session. Add-ons such as Event Microsites (5 included) and Insignia Integration are available. Note: Multi-division support is not included in this plan. 

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### What features are included in the Enterprise plan?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 The Enterprise plan is tailored for large organizations or those with complex needs. It includes all features from Professional, plus custom branding, unlimited user access (20+ in base), extensive platform integration, priority support, advanced tools like virtual showroom and marketplace functionalities, multi-division support, product customization tools, and professional services. Event Microsites (10 included) and extended retailer newsletter inclusion are also part of this plan. Note: This plan is best suited for organizations with established infrastructure and multi-stakeholder buy-in.

 To receive a customized quote, schedule a call with the RepSpark team . Pricing is tailored based on your company size, integration needs, and feature requirements. Note: For highly customized enterprise solutions, pricing may vary significantly depending on scope.

###### What security and compliance certifications does RepSpark have?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 RepSpark is SOC 2 Type II certified, PCI DSS compliant, and aligns with GDPR requirements. These certifications ensure rigorous controls for data security, payment card data protection, and privacy for individuals in the European Economic Area.
