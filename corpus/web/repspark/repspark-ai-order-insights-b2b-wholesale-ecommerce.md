## 
 RepSpark AI FAQs
 

 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### What is RepSpark AI?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 RepSpark AI is the embedded intelligence layer inside RepSpark Flow , our latest software release. It surfaces actionable order insights inside the platform, including expiring drafts, incomplete orders, unusual patterns, and revenue risks.

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### What are AI powered order insights?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 AI powered order insights automatically analyze visible order activity and highlight what needs attention, so reps and buyers can act faster without digging through reports or spreadsheets.

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### Do I need to set up dashboards or train my team on a new tool?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 No. RepSpark AI is built into the workflows your team already uses. There are no separate dashboards to build and no extra tool to manage.

 

 

 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### Who sees these insights?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 RepSpark AI is designed to become role aware. Buyers see what affects their carts and orders. Reps see what affects their accounts and follow ups.

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### How does this feature reduce order mistakes?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 By flagging missing quantities, incomplete orders, unusual order patterns, and other issues early, RepSpark AI helps teams catch preventable problems before orders are submitted.

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### Is RepSpark AI available today?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 Yes. RepSpark AI launched with RepSpark Flow as part of the broader modernization of our platform.

 

 Existing RepSpark brands will gain access to our AI tools when they migrate to Flow. New customers will be able to use the features when they come onboard today.
