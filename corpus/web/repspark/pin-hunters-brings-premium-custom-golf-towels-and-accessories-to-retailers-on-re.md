# Pin Hunters Brings Premium Custom Golf Towels and Accessories to Retailers on RepSpark 

 
 
 
 by Sawyer Frank on August 20, 2026
 

 
 Fully customizable golf products designed for tournaments, corporate programs, and pro shops now available through RepSpark. 

 
 (New York, NY & Anaheim, CA) – Pin Hunters, a fast-growing premium golf accessories brand founded in 2022, now offers its complete line of custom golf towels, headwear, and accessories to retailers on RepSpark , the golf industry's leading B2B wholesale eCommerce platform. As part of the Catapult Brand Group portfolio, Pin Hunters on RepSpark gives retailers a streamlined, digital-first way to discover and order from a customizable collection designed for pro shops, tournaments, member events, and corporate gifting programs. Golf shops, specialty retailers, pro shops, and tournament directors will now have an ordering experience for Pin Hunters' custom towel and accessory collection, backed by seamless customization capabilities and the operational infrastructure of the broader Catapult and RepSpark partnership. Pin Hunters' collection pairs elevated design with premium materials and unlimited personalization options. Designed for retailers and event organizers looking to deliver distinctive, on-brand products, the collection features customizable towels, headwear, apparel, and accessories that stand out on and off the course.

## 

#### Built for Customization and Impact 

 Founded in 2022 by Matthew Paul, Pin Hunters was created to bring thoughtful design and unique personalization to golf retail. What started as a passion project has rapidly evolved into a trusted partner for pro shops, tournaments, and corporate programs seeking premium, customizable golf products. "There's a big difference between putting a logo on a product and creating merchandise people actually want to keep," said Matthew Paul, founder of Pin Hunters. "We want every piece we create to feel connected to the course, tournament, or organization behind it. RepSpark makes it easier for golf buyers to access that level of customization without making the ordering process more complicated." Pin Hunters approaches custom merchandise as more than a place to put a logo. Designs are built around the identity of the club, tournament, or event — incorporating elements such as local landmarks, traditions, signature colors, and course history to create products golfers actually want to keep and use. Recent projects have included custom tournament merchandise for Colonial Country Club, with towel designs inspired by recognizable elements of the Charles Schwab Challenge, including its tartan champion's jacket and signature baby-blue champion's vehicle. With no setup fees, free artwork with unlimited mockups, and original designs backed by high-quality materials, the brand empowers retailers and event organizers to create distinctive products that reflect their brand identity. The full product line is now available to retailers on RepSpark. Rooted in a commitment to customization and quality craftsmanship, Pin Hunters delivers premium golf accessories that make every event, retail offering, and corporate program stand out.

#### Key Highlights of the Pin Hunters Approach 

 
 
 Fully Customizable Product Line: Towels, headwear, and accessories tailored to each retailer or event.

- 
 Original Designs & Custom Patterns: Distinctive artwork and design options that elevate brand presence.

- 
 Free Artwork with Unlimited Mockups: Zero-friction design collaboration with no setup fees.

- 
 Premium Materials: High-quality construction designed to perform and last.

- 
 Retail-First Focus: Built specifically for pro shops, tournaments, member events, and corporate gifting.

- 
 Fast Turnaround: Streamlined production for time-sensitive retail and event needs.

 

#### Elevating the Retail Experience 

 Through RepSpark, Pin Hunters offers retail buyers a frictionless B2B purchasing experience with built-in customization. Buyers can access Pin Hunters' digital catalogs to view design options, request custom mockups, check available inventory, and place seamless wholesale orders for golf towels and accessories. Pin Hunters gives pro shops and tournament organizers a premium, customizable offering that drives retail differentiation and customer loyalty, all within one digital destination. "Pin Hunters represents exactly what modern golf retail should be," said Meghann Butcher, CEO of RepSpark. "By combining premium quality with zero setup fees and unlimited customization, Pin Hunters removes barriers and empowers retailers to build distinctive product offerings that strengthen customer relationships. We're excited to bring Pin Hunters' complete customization capabilities to golf retailers through the Catapult Brand Group and RepSpark partnership."

#### Part of the Catapult Brand Group Portfolio 

 Pin Hunters is one of the premium brands represented by Catapult Brand Group, a premier sales and marketing agency representing some of the most iconic names in golf and lifestyle. Catapult's full portfolio is available to retailers through RepSpark, including Cole Haan Footwear & Apparel, Bombas, Foxden Fairways, Knockaround, Fair Harbor, Brumate, Native, Superfeet, Pin Hunters, Wyld1, Speaqua, Ellesse, SMF Prints, and Surface Sunscreen. 

 Catapult has integrated its dedicated ERP, Apparel Magic, with RepSpark so that inventory, pricing, and order data flow seamlessly across its ERP and wholesale eCommerce platform. 

 Beyond standard wholesale ordering, Catapult and RepSpark are transforming the tournament and corporate gifting space through multi-branded microsites, allowing golf shops and tournament directors to curate event merchandise from across the Catapult portfolio in a single, cohesive digital shopping destination. 

#### About Pin Hunters 

 Pin Hunters is a premium golf accessories brand founded in 2022 and dedicated to bringing elevated design and personalization to the course. Specializing in custom golf towels, headwear, apparel, and accessories, Pin Hunters empowers pro shops, tournament directors, and corporate programs to create distinctive, on-brand products with zero setup fees and unlimited design support. Backed by high-quality materials and original artwork, Pin Hunters makes it easy to stand out. Learn more at pinhuntersgolfco.com. 

#### About Catapult Brand Group 

 Representing a curated portfolio of premium brands, Catapult simplifies the buying experience by providing retailers with a single, trusted partner for product sourcing, sales support, and marketing resources. By combining industry expertise with strategic partnerships and a customer-first mindset, Catapult empowers brands to expand their reach, strengthen retail relationships, and create meaningful connections across the golf and sport industries.

#### RepSpark Systems 

 RepSpark is the leading provider of digital solutions for B2B wholesale commerce. Designed to be personalized, intuitive, and powerfully intelligent, RepSpark’s platform drives brands to connect and collaborate with their buyers to place orders, create digital line sheets, and analyze key data 24/7. RepSpark brands leverage technology to drive sales growth and better serve their retailers. Founded in 2007, RepSpark is headquartered in Anaheim, CA.
