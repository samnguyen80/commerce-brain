# RepSpark Named to Inc. 5000 List for Fifth Consecutive Year 

 
 
 
 by Tim McLain on August 11, 2026
 

 
 Distinguished achievement follows strategic alignment with PGA of America, the Association of Golf Merchandisers and investment from Headlight Partners 

 

 ANAHEIM, Calif.—August 11, 2026— RepSpark , the leading B2B ecommerce platform serving specialty brands and retailers across sport, outdoor, golf and lifestyle apparel related industries, has been recognized on the 2026 Inc. 5000 list for the fifth consecutive year. The recognition reflects RepSpark's position as the dominant wholesale commerce infrastructure across specialty retail, reinforced by recent strategic alignment with the PGA of America and AGM, as well as a $22 million investment from Headlight Partners . 

 Fewer than 10% of Inc. 5000 honorees earn Honor Roll status by making the list multiple years, and only 4% of all companies that have ever appeared on the Inc. 5000 have achieved it five times. Less than half of software and technology companies survive five years, making RepSpark's consecutive recognition in a high-failure industry segment a marker of exceptional execution and market durability. 

 "The RepSpark team at large has built software that actually reflects how wholesale operates, paired with the service and support brands and retailers need to scale," said Meghann Butcher , Co-Founder and CEO of RepSpark. "The fact that we're back on this list, year after year, while less than half of software companies survive five years, speaks to both the durability of our model and the real value we deliver." 

 RepSpark powers B2B wholesale ecommerce for more than 250 brands and 100,000 retailers worldwide across golf, outdoor and lifestyle apparel, with capabilities including digital ordering, product customization, event microsites, digital catalogs, accounts receivable management and AI-powered order insights. The PGA of America backs RepSpark as the wholesale infrastructure its PGA Professionals depend on, while the AGM's retailer-manager scholarship program emphasizes RepSpark's commitment to professional development in specialty retail. 

 "The support from PGA of America, Headlight Partners and AGM validate what our numbers already showed, RepSpark is the standard golf wholesale operates on," said Butcher. "Our Inc. 5000 recognition proves the model works, and now we're doubling down on faster product development, deeper integrations and the support that keeps our customers ahead." 

 RepSpark's five consecutive Inc. 5000 honors came from building wholesale software that actually succeeds. 

### About RepSpark 

 RepSpark is a leading B2B wholesale ecommerce platform helping brands and retailers across sport, outdoor, golf, and lifestyle apparel grow stronger wholesale relationships through digital ordering, product customization, event microsites, digital catalogs, and integrated wholesale operations. RepSpark serves 250 brands and 100,000 retailers worldwide, powering more than $4 billion in annual B2B transactions. RepSpark is a five-time Inc. 5000 company. Learn more at repspark.com .
