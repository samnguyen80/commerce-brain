 Frequently Asked Questions
 

 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### What is a digital line sheet for wholesale brands?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 A digital line sheet is an interactive, always-current product catalog that wholesale brands share with retail buyers to present collections, capture orders, and sync inventory in real time. Unlike a static PDF, a digital line sheet lives online, updates automatically when products or pricing change, and allows buyers to place orders directly — without a follow-up call or email from a rep. 

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### How is a RepSpark digital line sheet different from a PDF catalog?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 A PDF catalog is a static document — once it is published, it cannot reflect inventory changes, pricing updates, or product additions without a new version. A RepSpark digital line sheet is connected to your live product and pricing data, so every buyer who opens it sees the current collection at their specific wholesale price. Buyers can also place orders directly within the line sheet, eliminating the manual order-entry step that follows every PDF catalog review. 

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### Can independent sales reps use RepSpark digital line sheets for multiple brands?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 Yes. RepSpark is designed with multi-line reps and independent showrooms in mind. A rep logs into a single RepSpark dashboard and accesses digital line sheets for every brand they represent — each with that brand's current inventory, pricing, and templates. There is no switching between platforms, no managing separate PDF folders per brand, and no risk of presenting an out-of-date catalog. 

 

 

 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### Does RepSpark's digital line sheet sync with my ERP in real time?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 Yes. RepSpark integrates natively with NetSuite, ApparelMagic, AIMS360, and other leading ERPs and commerce platforms. Product data, inventory levels, and pricing sync automatically — and when a buyer places an order through a RepSpark line sheet, that order flows back into your ERP without manual re-entry. You can find the full list of supported integrations at repspark.com/integrations. 

 

 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

###### How long does it take to build a digital line sheet in RepSpark?

 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 
 

 
 Most brand teams publish their first digital line sheet within a single working session. RepSpark pulls your existing product data and imagery from your connected ERP or uploaded catalog, so you are not starting from scratch. Choose from 45+ professionally designed templates, apply your brand guidelines, and publish. Seasonal updates — adding new SKUs, changing pricing, swapping hero images — typically take minutes, not days.
