## What Wholesale Is
 Wholesale is the practice of selling your products to retailers at a wholesale price (usually 50% of your retail price) so they can sell them to customers in stores.
 But the real work of wholesale is about:
 
- Clarity in your product line and pricing

- Professional materials (line sheet or catalog, wholesale terms)

- Connection and communication with retail buyers

- Consistency over time (follow-ups, reorders, relationships)

- Confidence in how you talk about the value of your work
 When these elements are strong, wholesale becomes steady, repeatable, and sustainable.

## How to Know If Wholesale Is Right for You 
 Wholesale tends to be a strong fit if:
 
- Your product line has a clear identity that hangs together visually

- You can produce or consistently (even in small batches)

- Your pricing allows for a 50% wholesale discount

- You want your products to be in independent shops and real community spaces

- You are willing to build wholesale steadily, not explosively overnight
 Wholesale tends to be less aligned if:
 
- Your product is one-of-a-kind per unit (no repeatability)

- Your pricing is too tight to ever allow 50% wholesale prices

- You want a purely automated or passive sales channel with no relationship-building
 If wholesale is a fit, the next question becomes:
 How do I build wholesale in a way that feels clear, organized, and sustainable?

## The Method: The 6 Pillars of Wholesale
 The course is structured around strengthening six core areas:
 
- Line + Story Photography, product clarity, assortment structure, brand narrative.

- Pricing + Terms Healthy margins, order minimums, policies that support sustainability.

- Materials + Presentation Line sheet or catalog that retailers can easily buy from.

- Store Outreach + Follow-Up Rhythm How to find stores, what to say, how to track and follow up.

- Marketplaces (Optional, Strategic Use) Using Faire and similar platforms intentionally — not dependently.

- Reorders + Stockist Relationships Turning first orders into steady, consistent wholesale revenue.
