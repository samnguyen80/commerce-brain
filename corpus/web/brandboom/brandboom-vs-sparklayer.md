## Frequently Asked Questions

 

#### How does Brandboom&#x27;s marketplace compare to SparkLayer for finding wholesale customers?

 SparkLayer doesn&#x27;t have a marketplace or any buyer discovery features. You have to drive all your own wholesale traffic through sales reps, trade shows, and direct outreach. Brandboom connects you to a marketplace of 200,000+ active wholesale buyers.

 

#### Does Brandboom integrate with Shopify like SparkLayer?

 Yes. Brandboom offers native Shopify integration with real-time inventory sync, automatic product imports, and order management. The difference is that Brandboom can also work standalone. You&#x27;re not locked into running everything through Shopify if that doesn&#x27;t fit your business.

 

#### Can I use Brandboom at trade shows, or is it web-only?

 Yes, Brandboom offers multiple features you can use at trade shows. Brandboom has full-featured iOS and Android apps built specifically for trade shows and field sales. You get barcode scanning, offline ordering (for when convention Wi-Fi fails), and a streamlined interface for writing orders on the floor. SparkLayer is web-only with no mobile apps or offline capability.

 

#### What&#x27;s the difference between SparkLayer and Brandboom for B2B wholesale?

 The main difference is that SparkLayer only adds B2B checkout functionality to your existing e-commerce store, while Brandboom is a complete wholesale platform. Beyond B2B ordering, Brandboom includes line sheet and presentation builders, a buyer marketplace, mobile apps for trade shows, and buyer engagement analytics. SparkLayer handles transactions; Brandboom helps you grow your wholesale business.

 

#### How does Brandboom pricing compare to SparkLayer pricing?

 Brandboom&#x27;s Basic Plan starts at $50/month, whereas SparkLayer starts at $49/month. But SparkLayer also caps you at 50 orders on Starter and 100 on Growth, while Brandboom includes unlimited orders on all paid plans.
