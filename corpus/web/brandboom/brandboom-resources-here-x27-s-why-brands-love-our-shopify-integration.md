## How It Works
 Our Shopify integration is divided into two key components - Product Sync and Inventory Sync - so you can choose what works best for your business.
 Product Sync: This is free for all users. With Product Sync, you can effortlessly integrate your existing store with Brandboom. Once synced, any updates made to your Shopify products will be instantly reflected in Brandboom.
 Shopify Sync (Shopify Premium): If you&#x27;re looking for even more powerful features, our Inventory Sync is available as an add-on for Startup & Business plans. With our Premium integration, you&#x27;ll enjoy up-to-date inventory 24/7.  Never worry about incorrect inventory numbers again! Wholesale your store&#x27;s inventory with separate wholesale pricing, and seamlessly sync orders back to Shopify for streamlined order fulfillment.

## Integrate with Shopify
 Log in to your Brandboom account.
- Connect to an existing showroom or create the showroom you’d like to sync.
- Click on the "Import" button and click “From Shopify.”
- Enter your Shopify name and choose the showroom you want to connect. For example, if “amazingclothing.myshopify.com” is your Shopify URL, your Shopify name would be amazingclothing.
- Follow the prompts to log in to Shopify and download the Brandboom B2B Wholesale App. Click “Install App” to integrate with Shopify. 
 ‍
 
- In Brandboom, follow the steps to ensure the fields are mapped correctly.
- Select whether you want to add Inventory Sync. If you choose to add Inventory Sync, select “Upgrade to use this feature,” which will take you to the subscription page. ‍
