## Product Hotspots on Image Gallery
 May 16, 2026

 

 
 Tag products directly on your images used in your presentation&#x27;s image gallery. Buyers can hover to preview and click to view the product.
 Hover to preview, click to view — buyers see product details on hover and view the product with one click.
- Add to order — if the product is on the presentation, buyers can add it to their cart. If not, it&#x27;s still available for reference.
- Saved to the image — hotspots can be stored on the image file itself. Use the image in another presentation and your tags come with it automatically. For example, you can tag a celebrity photo with three products — two current season, one archived. Buyers see the full look, click through to current styles, and add to order. The archived style shows for context but stays read-only.
 To get started: Open a presentation → add an image gallery section → add images → click "Add hotspots" to start tagging products to hotspots.
